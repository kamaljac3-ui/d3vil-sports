NFL

NFL owners unanimously approve the Seahawks' record $9.6 billion sale to the Khosla family

Team owners voted unanimously at a special league meeting in Atlanta to approve Vinod and Neeru Khosla's agreement to buy the Seahawks from the Allen estate for a record $9.612 billion, ending the Allen family's 29-year ownership of the club. Commissioner Roger Goodell congratulated the Khoslas on their "great timing," since the sale closes on a franchise coming off its second Super Bowl title, with the deal expected to become official within the week.

CFB

Big 12 becomes the third power conference to bar players with pro contracts, and the NFL closes the loophole further

A day after the Big Ten and SEC each adopted rules barring anyone who signed a pro contract, appeared on a pro roster, or entered a draft without withdrawing from ever competing in their conferences, the Big 12 followed with its own ban. Hours later, the NFL confirmed that any player who goes back to college this fall won't be eligible to return to the NFL until 2027 — closing off the path some hoped a court ruling had opened, and directly killing LSU's plan to sign NFL cuts Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris.
