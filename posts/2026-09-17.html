CFB

NFL formally closes the door on the two LSU eligibility-case players

The NFL notified all 32 teams this week that former Ole Miss players Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris are barred from any contact, workout or signing for the rest of 2026, closing out the strange saga that began when a Baton Rouge court briefly granted them extra college eligibility to join LSU. LSU never actually played either player this season after the SEC blocked rostering athletes who'd already signed NFL contracts, so both are now without a team in either league.

Source: CNN

Texas A&M rockets up the polls after routing Arizona State

The Aggies posted the week's biggest ranking jump, climbing six spots to No. 8 in the Coaches Poll after a 48-20 blowout of Arizona State that included two defensive touchdowns. At 2-0, Texas A&M moved into the AP top tier as former top-10 teams Oregon and Oklahoma slid out following idle-week losses.

Coach Mike Elko credited the jump to "winning football" — takeaways, discipline and red-zone defense.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Miami's Darian Mensah widens his Heisman lead through two weeks

The Duke transfer completed 41 of 45 passes for 653 yards and eight touchdowns across Miami's first two games, most recently going 14-for-15 with three scores against Florida A&M. Mensah heads into Week 3 as the clear Heisman front-runner ahead of chasers like Ohio State's Jeremiah Smith and Texas's Arch Manning.

None are assured a roster spot; they're competing for a handful of openings behind the defending champions' core rotation.

Oregon State's unionization hearing grinds on, with no ruling yet

The state employment-board hearing on whether Oregon State women's basketball players can unionize continued this week, with university administrators, including the provost, expected to testify that scholarships and NIL pay don't make the players employees. MLB

Brewers clinch a fourth straight NL Central behind a Misiorowski gem

Jacob Misiorowski struck out seven over five scoreless innings as Milwaukee beat Pittsburgh 5-1, and the division title was locked up about 50 minutes later when Chicago fell to Atlanta. It's the first team to win four consecutive NL Central titles since the division was formed in 1994, and the 94-57 Brewers now own the best record in baseball.

Teammate Brandon Woodruff said of Misiorowski, "he's going to lead the way for us" in October.

Source: MLB.com

Elly De La Cruz chases 30-30, spoils a Dodgers clinch bid along the way

De La Cruz hit his 28th home run of the season as Cincinnati beat Los Angeles 6-2, pushing his stat line to 28 homers and 26 steals with ten games left and delaying the Dodgers' own division-clinching plans in the process. A 30-30 season would be the first of his career.

Source: JOX-FM/AP

Boxing

Afterward, Garcia called out WBA titleholder Teofimo Lopez for a unification bout as security kept Lopez, who'd shown up ringside, from entering the ring.

Garcia shouted, "Unified time, where you at, Teofimo?"

Benn said, "the answer is simple. Time with my kids."

Horse Racing

A $2.25 million Nyquist colt tops a record Book 2 session at Keeneland

The colt, bred by Fred Hertrich III and John Fielding and bought by owner Alex Lieblong, set the highest Book 2 opening-day price since Keeneland's September Yearling Sale moved to its current format in 2021. Fourteen yearlings sold for seven figures in the session.

Keeneland VP of Sales Tony Lacy called it "a really solid, incredible day."

Source: BloodHorse

Further Ado and The Puma headline a loaded Pennsylvania Derby field

Saturday's $1 million Pennsylvania Derby at Parx Racing features an 11-horse field led by Blue Grass Stakes winner Further Ado and The Puma, both looking to rediscover their spring form, with Chad Brown's Iron Honor and West Virginia Derby winner Zihnal also in the mix for the year's final major dirt test for 3-year-olds.
