NFL

49ers lose Ricky Pearsall for the season to knee surgery

San Francisco is placing wide receiver Ricky Pearsall on season-ending injured reserve so he can have surgery on a persistent PCL issue that first flared up last October and never fully settled through camp. GM John Lynch said the procedure carries a six-to-twelve-month recovery window, pushing Pearsall's return toward next season rather than a quick comeback. It's a big loss for a receiver room the Niners were counting on to take a step forward.

Source: ESPN

Seahawks rookie safety Bud Clark's debut season ends before it starts

Second-round pick Bud Clark broke his right ankle on the very first defensive snap of Seattle's preseason game against Tennessee, and the team has now placed him on injured reserve. Coach Mike Macdonald said the rookie will be out "multiple months," ending hopes of an immediate impact in the secondary and sending Seattle looking for safety depth before Week 1.

Source: NBC Sports

Cowboys' backup quarterback job still up for grabs entering the preseason finale

Coach Brian Schottenheimer says the race between Sam Howell and Joe Milton for Dallas's No. 2 job behind Dak Prescott remains "neck and neck," with Howell maybe slightly ahead after two exhibition games. Milton has posted the flashier numbers — 21-of-28 for 286 yards and three touchdowns, plus a rushing score — against Howell's 15-of-22 for 159 yards, and the battle plays out once more before the roster is set.

Source: NFL.com

CFB

Big Ten and SEC bar any player who signed an NFL contract from ever suiting up in their conferences

Responding to a wave of players trying to use recent court rulings to jump back from the NFL to college ball, the Big Ten and SEC each formally banned anyone who signed a pro contract, appeared on an NFL roster, or entered the draft without withdrawing from ever playing in their leagues again. The Big 12 is reportedly weighing a similar rule and the ACC is expected to discuss it too, turning the eligibility fight into one of the sport's biggest governance battles ahead of the season.

Source: CBS Sports

Virginia pulls RB Solomon Beebe off the roster after an animal cruelty arrest

UAB transfer Solomon Beebe was charged with three counts of animal cruelty after a witness reported him striking his dog between Aug. 10-12, and Albemarle County later seized the animal. Virginia removed him from the roster just days before Saturday's season opener against NC State, with no word yet on his longer-term standing with the program.

Source: WDBJ7

Tennessee gets Arion Carter's suspension trimmed to one game — again

The NCAA cut linebacker Arion Carter's suspension from two games to one after a second appeal from Tennessee, continuing a walk-back from an original three-game penalty tied to a $427 flight his agent paid for. AD Danny White called the underlying rule outdated; Carter still sits out the Sept. 5 opener against Furman but is now eligible for the Sept. 12 trip to Georgia Tech.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Kawhi Leonard's trade to the Raptors stays frozen as the NBA nears a settlement

The league's cap-circumvention probe into Kawhi Leonard's endorsement deals with the now-defunct Aspiration and scoreboard maker Daktronics remains unresolved, and it's still holding up Toronto's agreed trade for the forward. Recent reporting says the NBA hasn't found proof Clippers owner Steve Ballmer personally funneled money to Leonard and is now negotiating a penalty rather than voiding the deal, with hope for a resolution before training camp.

Source: CBS Sports

Klay Thompson signs with the Heat after a Mavericks buyout

Dallas agreed to buy out the rest of Klay Thompson's contract on Aug. 21, and after clearing waivers he signed a two-year, $11.5 million deal with Miami two days later. It gives the Heat another proven shooter as they continue reshaping their roster this offseason.

Source: ESPN

Warriors fill out their roster with Georges Niang and Brandon Williams

NCAAM

Judge clears former Big East Player of the Year RJ Luis Jr. to play for LSU

A Louisiana state judge granted RJ Luis Jr. a preliminary injunction allowing the reigning Big East Player of the Year to suit up for Will Wade's LSU this season, despite having signed NBA and G League deals after going undrafted. His attorneys argued he never actually played in a regular-season NBA or G League game, distinguishing his case from players the NCAA has already ruled ineligible, and the NCAA is expected to explore an appeal.

Source: ESPN

Federal NIL bill stalls again even with Big Ten and SEC backing

The Senate's "Protect College Sports Act," meant to set national NIL standards, failed to clear the chamber before recess despite the Big Ten and SEC backing it after late concessions, and a companion House bill has stalled for over a year. Opposition from groups like the AFL-CIO over athlete bargaining rights leaves schools without a uniform NIL framework heading into the season.

Source: The Washington Times

WNBA

Angel Reese breaks a 23-year-old single-game rebounding record

Reese grabbed 26 rebounds for Atlanta in a 78-71 win over the Sparks, topping Chamique Holdsclaw's 23-year-old league record of 24 and pushing her season total to 458 boards, past A'Ja Wilson's single-season mark of 451. It capped a perfect four-game West Coast trip that also included a career-high 31 points against Phoenix in a win that had already clinched Atlanta's playoff spot.

Source: CBS Sports

Wings clinch their first playoff berth since 2023, eliminate Portland

Paige Bueckers (22 points, 11 assists) and Jessica Shepard (21 points, 9 rebounds, 6 assists) led all five Dallas starters in double figures in a 96-78 win over Portland that locked up the league's final playoff spot and knocked the Fire out of contention. It's a franchise-record win total for the Wings with five games still to play.

Source: ESPN

Vandersloot joins Sue Bird as the only players in WNBA history with 3,000 career assists

Chicago's Courtney Vandersloot reached the milestone during the Sky's 87-81 road loss to Connecticut, joining Bird as the only players ever to cross 3,000 career assists. Sun forward Aaliyah Edwards had the big night on the winning side, scoring a career-high 26 points to help beat her former team.

Source: ClutchPoints
