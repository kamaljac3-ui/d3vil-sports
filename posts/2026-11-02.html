NFL

Panthers fire defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero after another blowout loss

Carolina dismissed Evero and passing-game coordinator Jonathan Cooley on Friday, a day after a 27-19 loss at Green Bay dropped the Panthers to 3-5, with defensive line coach Todd Wash taking over as interim coordinator for the final 10 games. Carolina has allowed the sixth-most points and third-most rushing yards per game in the league this season, a steep letdown after an offseason spending spree on that side of the ball.

Source: ESPN

Lamar Jackson hurts his thumb on his first snap back, needs surgery

Jackson, playing his first game back from a high-ankle injury, hyperextended his throwing thumb on his own follow-through in Sunday's 36-10 loss to Buffalo and will need surgery that lands him on injured reserve for at least four games. Coach Jesse Minter wouldn't rule out more, saying only "we'll see" when asked if it could end Jackson's season. Backup Tyler Huntley then left with a toe injury, pushing undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano into relief for Baltimore.

Source: ESPN

Colts push Jonathan Taylor trade talks into overdrive before the Nov. 10 deadline

With Indianapolis sitting at 2-5, the Colts are actively shopping star running back Jonathan Taylor and reportedly want Christian McCaffrey-level compensation, even floating a package that could include Miami receiver Jaylen Waddle. The Vikings, Giants, Broncos, Bengals and Packers have all been linked, adding Taylor to a sell-off that already sent guard Quenton Nelson to Seattle and tackle DeForest Buckner to San Francisco this fall.

Source: CBS Sports

CFB

Florida State fires Mike Norvell, eats a roughly $50 million buyout

Florida State fired head coach Mike Norvell on Sunday, a day after a 36-24 home loss to Clemson, ending a tenure that finished 41-39. The buyout is reported at roughly $50 million — among the largest in college football history — and the school also dismissed defensive coordinator Tony White and safeties coach Evan Cooper the same day, naming director of scouting Rick Stockstill interim head coach.

Source: Washington Post

Iowa State walks off Oklahoma State 51-49 in a wild Big 12 shootout

In one of the headline results from a jam-packed weekend slate, Iowa State beat Oklahoma State 51-49 on a walk-off score, part of a Saturday that also included USC's 30-18 upset of No. 2 Ohio State and Indiana's overtime escape against Minnesota.

Source: Yahoo Sports

First CFP rankings land Tuesday, with Miami eyeing No. 1 and Ohio State set to tumble

The College Football Playoff committee releases its first 2026 rankings Tuesday evening, and previews project unbeaten Miami (7-0) as the likely top seed while one-loss Kentucky is tabbed the season's biggest surprise team by strength of record. Ohio State, fresh off its loss to USC, is expected to fall out of the top 10 entirely.

Source: NCAA.com

NBA

Luka Dončić's 45-point, 5-steal night sets an NBA tracking-era record

Dončić scored 45 points on 12-of-23 shooting with 15 rebounds, 6 assists and 5 steals as the Lakers beat Toronto 131-125 on Sunday to move to 5-2. Per ESPN Research, he's the first player since player tracking began in 2013-14 to record 8 forced turnovers, 5 steals and 3 charges drawn in a single game; Austin Reaves added 19 and Toronto's Scottie Barnes led all scorers with 29.

Source: ESPN

Yaxel Lendeborg erupts for a career-high 37 as Warriors rest Steph Curry

With Steph Curry and Draymond Green both held out to manage lingering knee issues ahead of a Monday game at Phoenix, Golden State still beat the Clippers 124-112 on Sunday behind a career night from Lendeborg. Rui Hachimura led Los Angeles with 24 points in the loss.

Source: Heavy.com

Nets finally break through, beating the Pacers for their first win of the season

Brooklyn ended an 0-start with a 98-90 win over Indiana on Sunday, led by Michael Porter Jr.'s 23 points and 9 rebounds and 20 points from Julius Randle. Andrew Nembhard scored 23 for a Pacers team still missing injured star Tyrese Haliburton.

Source: ESPN

NCAAM

Notre Dame survives a Rome shootout with Villanova to open the season

Notre Dame rallied from a second-half deficit to beat Villanova 74-72 in the Eternal City Tip-Off on Sunday, with Logan Duncomb's go-ahead three-pointer with 1:08 left sealing it. Brady Koehler had 16 points and 8 rebounds and Braeden Smith added 16 for the Irish, while Villanova's 20 turnovers turned directly into 20 Notre Dame points.

Source: CBS Sports

UConn locks up Dan Hurley through 2032

UConn signed head coach Dan Hurley to a two-year extension worth roughly $8 million annually, keeping him under contract through the 2031-32 season and adding more than $16 million in value. Athletic director David Benedict called retaining Hurley a program priority heading into the new season.

Source: NBC Sports

North Carolina's Michael Malone lands his first big recruiting win

Demarcus Henry, the No. 2 overall recruit in the 2027 class, committed to North Carolina on Friday — the program's highest-ranked pledge since 2019 — an early win for new head coach Michael Malone, the former Denver Nuggets championship coach hired last spring after Hubert Davis' firing.

Source: ESPN

WNBA

WNBA sets Nov. 15 draft lottery, with Houston and Toronto tied for the top odds

The league announced its 2027 draft lottery will air on ESPN on Nov. 15, with the relocated Houston Comets and expansion Toronto Tempo tied for the best odds at 32.9% each and Washington — holding Chicago's pick — third at 16.1%. USC's JuJu Watkins, who makes her long-awaited return today, is the widely projected No. 1 pick if she declares.

Source: ESPN

Angel Reese and the Dream vow they'll be back after their Game 7 heartbreak

Days after Atlanta's 68-63 Game 7 loss to Golden State, Angel Reese said "it is tough in the moment, but we'll be back," and coach Karl Smesko praised a defense that held the Valkyries under 65 points in every Finals loss without winning the series. The Dream's biggest hope for next season is a healthy Brionna Jones, who battled injuries throughout 2026, and the team will also move into the larger State Farm Arena.

Source: ESPN

NCAAW

Hannah Hidalgo torches Villanova for 32 as Notre Dame routs its way into the season

No. 13 Notre Dame blew past Villanova 99-75 in Rome's women's Eternal City Tip-Off opener on Sunday, with Hidalgo scoring 32 and transfer Leah Macy adding 27 in her Irish debut. Notre Dame shot 55.9% from the field and turned a sloppy Villanova first half into a comfortable lead that held the rest of the way.

Source: ESPN

Two marquee openers today: JuJu Watkins' long-awaited return, and No. 1 South Carolina in Paris

No. 3 USC plays at UNLV in Las Vegas this afternoon in JuJu Watkins' first real game action since tearing her ACL in last year's NCAA Tournament — she looked sharp in an October exhibition with 18 points, 3 steals and 2 blocks. At the same time, top-ranked South Carolina opens its title defense against No. 20 Maryland in Paris, a matchup the Gamecocks have won five straight times.

Source: Las Vegas Review-Journal

MLB

Orioles decline Luis Robert Jr.'s $20 million option, sending him to a thin free-agent market

Baltimore bought out outfielder Luis Robert Jr. for $2 million rather than pick up his $20 million option, ending an injury-marred run in which he hit just .221 with 11 home runs after the Orioles claimed him off waivers from the Mets in August. He joins Randy Arozarena, Seiya Suzuki and Taylor Ward in a center-field market the Orioles are reportedly still shopping, just not at that price.

Source: ESPN

Mariners down to two managerial finalists as the decision drags into November

Seattle has narrowed its search to former Astros manager Joe Espada and Cubs player-development director Jason Kanzler, with both interviewing in person at T-Mobile Park and a hire expected within days. The Astros, who fired Espada after a Wild Card sweep and hired Randy Flores as GM last week, still have no manager of their own.

Source: MLB Trade Rumors

Qualifying offer hits a record $23.15 million as option decisions pile up

This offseason's qualifying-offer value rose to $23.15 million, with Arozarena and Suzuki both expected to receive and reject one. Elsewhere, San Diego declined mutual options on Miguel Andújar, Lucas Giolito and Griffin Canning, and St. Louis bought out closer Ryne Stanek, all hitting free agency as the Nov. 1 decision window opened.

Source: SI.com

McDavid's season-opening assist streak hits an NHL-record 15 games
