NFL

Pro Football Hall of Famer Mike Ditka dies at 86

Ditka, the Hall of Fame tight end who later coached the Chicago Bears to their only Super Bowl title following the 1985 season, died Friday, his family announced. He was the first tight end ever inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame, in 1988, and spent parts of four decades around the league as a player, head coach and broadcaster.

Source: Pro Football Hall of Fame

Eagles release Andy Dalton as the offensive line crumbles before London trip

Philadelphia cut backup quarterback Andy Dalton on Friday, days after trading starting center Cam Jurgens to Baltimore and losing longtime right tackle Lane Johnson to retirement. Dalton had already lost the No. 2 job to Tanner McKee after a costly red-zone interception in Week 3, though a source told ESPN he could still return on the practice squad.

Source: ESPN

Lamar Jackson ruled out for Sunday Night Football with an ankle injury

Jackson sprained his ankle on the final play of the first half last week against Tennessee and didn't practice all week, clearing the way for Tyler Huntley to start Sunday night at Atlanta. The Ravens haven't said whether Jackson will be ready for the following week's game against Cleveland.

Source: NFL.com

NCAAM

The case now returns to circuit court for a further hearing, but for now coaches are preparing as if the affected players can suit up.

Source: 247Sports
