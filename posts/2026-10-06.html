NFL

Falcons rout Saints on Monday Night Football

Atlanta never trailed in a 45-24 win over New Orleans, with Bijan Robinson running for 145 yards and two touchdowns and Brian Robinson Jr. adding three scores of his own. The Falcons improved to 2-2 while the winless-at-home Saints fell to 1-3, getting outrushed 207-50.

Source: Sky Sports

Lamar Jackson's Week 5 status in doubt after an ankle injury

Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson left Sunday's win over Tennessee in a walking boot after being sacked on the final play of the first half, and Baltimore is calling him day-to-day. Coach Jesse Minter said he isn't worried about a long-term issue but wouldn't commit to Jackson playing against Atlanta in Week 5.

Source: Baltimore Sun

Barkley considered week-to-week with a hamstring injury

Eagles running back Saquon Barkley hurt his hamstring on his only carry of Sunday's loss to the Rams and is considered week-to-week, likely to miss next week's game against Jacksonville in London. Coach Nick Sirianni said Monday it isn't believed to be long-term, but wouldn't commit to a return date.

Source: NFL.com

NCAAM

Fifth-year eligibility fight splits into a state-by-state legal mess

A Kentucky appeals court dissolved the injunction covering 38 players chasing a fifth season, ruling Missouri's Mark Mitchell, Louisville's Seth Trimble and LSU's Skyy Clark ineligible again -- while a New York court granted St. John's transfer Keyshawn Hall a temporary restraining order that keeps him eligible pending a hearing held Tuesday. A separate Ohio court had already reversed a similar injunction for roughly two dozen other players last week, leaving the eligibility picture split by jurisdiction with the underlying lawsuits still pending.

Source: Yahoo Sports

WNBA

Allisha Gray's availability for Game 2 still unresolved

"Hopefully she'll be OK," coach Karl Smesko said. Both semifinal series sit at 1-0 -- Atlanta over New York and Golden State over Las Vegas -- with Game 2s set for Wednesday night.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAW

Purdue loses a second player to a season-ending ACL tear

Incoming freshman Zoja Štirn announced Monday that she tore her ACL in practice and will need surgery, ruling her out for all of 2026-27 -- the Boilermakers' second season-ending knee injury of the preseason after Aysia Proctor went down in September. Coach Katie Geralds had called Stirn and transfer Kyndall Hunter her "one-two punch" at point guard before the injury.

MLB

Yankees' four-error meltdown hands the Rays a 2-0 ALDS lead

Tampa Bay beat New York 5-2 on Monday as the Yankees committed four errors -- three of them in the first inning alone -- in a mess that tied for the second-most in a game in franchise postseason history, behind only a five-error game in the 1976 ALCS. Ben Rice hit both New York runs on solo homers, but it wasn't enough; the Rays can close out the series Wednesday at Yankee Stadium.

Source: CBS Sports

White Sox beat Guardians again, one win from the ALCS

Chicago overcame an early 2-0 deficit to win 4-3 on the road in Game 2, scoring three runs in the sixth and getting 5 1/3 strong innings from Sean Burke, to take a 2-0 ALDS lead. It's the White Sox's fourth straight road win to open the postseason, and they can eliminate Cleveland in Game 3 on Wednesday at Rate Field.

Source: The Boston Globe

Dodgers and Braves send aces Yamamoto and Sale back out for Game 3

Los Angeles and Atlanta are tied 1-1 in the NLDS heading into Tuesday night's Game 3 at Truist Park, a rematch of starters Yoshinobu Yamamoto and Chris Sale, who last faced off in a 1-0 Braves win back in August. It's the reigning World Series MVP Yamamoto's postseason debut this October.

Source: NBC Los Angeles
