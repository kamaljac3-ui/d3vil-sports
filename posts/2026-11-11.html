NFL

Vikings bring Kirk Cousins back home in a trade-deadline shocker

Minnesota reacquired Kirk Cousins from the Raiders just ahead of Tuesday's 4 p.m. ET deadline, sending Las Vegas a 2027 third-round pick for the quarterback it let walk in free agency back in 2023. The move pushes rookie Fernando Mendoza back to the bench a day after the Vikings' offense stalled in a loss to Buffalo, reuniting Cousins with the only other franchise he's started for. Raiders coach Klint Kubiak admitted, "We're going to miss Kirk."

Denver also added linebacker Daiyan Henley from the winless Chargers, and Atlanta and Baltimore swapped offensive linemen in the deal's final minutes before Tuesday's cutoff.
