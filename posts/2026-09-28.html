WNBA

A’ja Wilson puts up a stat line no one in WNBA history has matched

Wilson scored 38 points on 15-of-19 shooting with 16 rebounds, four blocks and two steals as the Aces blew out Caitlin Clark's Fever 102-85 in Game 1 of their first-round series, becoming the first player in WNBA history — regular season or playoffs — to hit at least 35 points and 15 rebounds on 75% shooting. Clark and backcourt partner Kelsey Mitchell combined for just 27 points on 30% shooting in the loss.

Source: Newsweek

It's an immediate statement from the lowest-seeded team in the field against a Minnesota squad that had the WNBA's best record.

The win moves the all-Japan star to 23-2 and hands Nasukawa his second straight loss in a bid for the belt.

Golf

Team USA completes the largest final-day comeback in Presidents Cup history

Trailing 10.5-7.5 heading into Sunday singles at Medinah, the Americans won 9.5 of the 12 points on offer to beat the Internationals 17-13 and claim an 11th straight Presidents Cup. Two-time major champion Collin Morikawa and 21-year-old rookie Jackson Koivun both flipped losing singles matches on the back nine to seal the largest comeback in the event's history.

Source: PGA Tour

Oxley and Victoriam Farm. The win headlined a stakes-heavy Sunday card as tracks around the country crowned their fall qualifiers for the October 30-31 championships at Keeneland.
