NFL

Von Miller heads home, signs with the Cowboys

Dallas added the eight-time Pro Bowler to its pass-rush rotation on a one-year deal reportedly worth up to $7.5 million, giving the DeSoto, Texas native a shot at playing close to home. It's Miller's fifth NFL team and 15th pro season, following stops with Denver, the Rams, Buffalo, and Washington.

Jets expect Breece Hall back for Week 1 despite groin strain

Star running back Breece Hall left training camp practice early Monday with a groin strain, and coach Aaron Glenn says he'll miss roughly two to three weeks. The team's plan still has Hall ready for the season opener, with backup Braelon Allen picking up extra reps in the meantime.

Source: Washington Post

Malik Nabers clears a major hurdle in his ACL comeback

Giants receiver Malik Nabers, recovering from a torn ACL and meniscus suffered last October, took part in full-speed team drills for the first time this week, still in a non-contact jersey. It's the clearest sign yet his recovery is ahead of schedule, with a Week 1 return against Dallas still the target.

CFB

The Buckeyes took 40 of 69 first-place votes to top the preseason AP Top 25 for the first time since 2015, with Oregon, Georgia, Notre Dame, and Texas rounding out the top five. Notably, no SEC team received a single first-place vote this year — a sharp shift after years of conference dominance.

Source: ESPN

Tennessee's Arion Carter suspended two games over a $427 flight

The NCAA ruled star linebacker Arion Carter committed a violation by accepting a $427 flight from an agent during a brief, since-abandoned run at the NFL Draft process. Carter had already repaid the flight and cut ties with the agent; his ban was reduced from three games to two on appeal, and he'll miss the opener against Furman.

Source: ESPN

Lane Kiffin's LSU debut against Ole Miss circled for September

Kiffin left Ole Miss for LSU this offseason after a program-record 11-win season and the Rebels' first College Football Playoff berth — a departure timed so awkwardly Ole Miss kept him off the sideline for the playoff run itself. His new team's schedule sends him back to Oxford on September 19, already one of the sport's most anticipated early-season games.

Source: SI.com

Texas rebuilds around Arch Manning for a championship push

After an uneven first year as full-time starter that still ended with a late-season surge, Manning returns for 2026 behind a retooled offensive line and has already picked up preseason All-SEC honors. Analysts now call Texas's roster one with no glaring holes heading into a season many expect to end in the Playoff.

Source: CBS Sports

NBA

LeBron James ends free agency by signing with the 76ers

After 23 seasons split mostly between Cleveland and the Lakers, LeBron is heading to Philadelphia on a two-year, $8 million deal with a player option, picking the Sixers over reported interest from Miami and Cleveland. He'll join Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, and Jaylen Brown as Philadelphia tries to build a contender around him in what James has called his final free-agency decision.

Source: ESPN

Heat officially land Giannis Antetokounmpo in blockbuster deal

Miami acquired the two-time MVP and Bobby Portis from Milwaukee for Tyler Herro, Kel'el Ware, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kasparas Jakucionis, and a heavy package of first-round picks and swaps stretching to 2033. The trade, finalized in late June, ranks among the largest in NBA history and ended months of speculation about Giannis's future.

Source: ESPN

Kawhi Leonard's trade to Toronto cleared to proceed

The Clippers-Raptors deal sending Kawhi Leonard back to Toronto for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and draft compensation had been paused during an NBA investigation into alleged cap circumvention involving Leonard and a Clippers sponsor. That probe wrapped this week and found no wrongdoing, and both sides now expect the trade to close before training camp.

Source: HoopsHype

Grizzlies complete their rebuild, trading Ja Morant to Portland

Memphis sent two-time All-Star Ja Morant to the Trail Blazers for Jerami Grant and Kris Murray, closing the book on the Morant era after injuries and off-court issues limited him to 79 games over three seasons. It's the third former Grizzlies cornerstone dealt away since last year's playoff sweep, following Desmond Bane and Jaren Jackson Jr.

Source: ESPN

NCAAM

Fifth-year eligibility fight leaves college hoops in limbo

A federal injunction briefly let athletes from the high school class of 2022 return for a bonus fifth season, triggering a surprise transfer window from August 3-10 that programs like Arizona, North Carolina, and Gonzaga rushed to exploit. An Ohio appeals court then stayed that ruling on August 17, throwing the newly-eligible players' status back into uncertainty just as the season approaches.

Source: Bloomberg Law

Longtime UNC guard Seth Trimble, who scored nearly 1,000 career points and was the Tar Heels' top on-ball defender, is using his newly granted fifth year to join Louisville instead of returning to Chapel Hill. With North Carolina's roster already full, Trimble opted for a fresh start over a homecoming.

Source: ESPN

Dan Hurley blasts "total chaos" of the late-summer portal rush

UConn coach Dan Hurley ripped the sudden flood of newly-eligible fifth-year players into the portal in early August, warning it's squeezing out younger recruits. Hurley confirmed the Huskies' roster is finished and he won't be chasing any of the class-of-2022 holdovers.

"It's total chaos and it's not sustainable." — Dan Hurley
