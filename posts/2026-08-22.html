NFL

Nick Chubb retires after eight NFL seasons

The four-time Pro Bowl running back announced his retirement on social media Friday, closing out a career split between Cleveland and one final season in Houston. "I've known for some time now, but I'm finally ready to share that I'm done playing football," Chubb wrote, finishing with 7,349 rushing yards and 54 touchdowns since being drafted in 2018.

Source: NFL.com

NCAAM

Federal appeals court halts fifth-year eligibility for class of 2022 athletes

The 10th Circuit stayed the Colorado district court's injunction that had briefly let athletes from the high school class of 2022 play a bonus fifth season, meaning those players are immediately no longer eligible while the NCAA's appeal proceeds. It's a major reversal that will force rosters that added those players back into limbo just weeks before practice begins.

Source: Sportico
