NCAAM

Kansas quietly filed an eligibility waiver for transfer big man Emmanuel Ogbole

Court filings in Ogbole's lawsuit against the NCAA reveal Kansas submitted a hardship-waiver appeal on his behalf on July 31, the last day such requests were accepted before the association's new five-year eligibility model took effect. The NCAA still hasn't assigned a case manager or set a decision date, leaving the 6-foot-10 center's status - and reported interest from Kansas, Georgia, Oklahoma, Tennessee and UNLV - unresolved.

WNBA

Team USA wins a fifth straight World Cup, and Breanna Stewart makes history

The Americans rallied from a first-half deficit to beat France 97-79 in Berlin, extending their FIBA Women's World Cup title streak to five in a row and 12 overall. Breanna Stewart poured in 22 points and 10 rebounds to become the event's first two-time MVP, and Duke coach Kara Lawson - who guided the team - became the first coach in USA Basketball history to win gold at both the 3x3 and 5-on-5 levels.

Lawson said of Stewart, "She is USA Basketball."

Source: ESPN

Former Duke guard Chelsea Gray also played a key role for the Americans off the bench.
