MLB

Pete Crow-Armstrong becomes the first Cub ever in the 40-40 club

Crow-Armstrong stole his 40th base during Chicago’s playoff-clinching win over Miami, pairing it with his league-leading 45 home runs to become just the seventh player in MLB history — and the first Cub — to reach 40 homers and 40 steals in a season. At 24, he joins Jose Canseco and Alex Rodriguez as the youngest to do it, a feat that effectively locks up his case for NL MVP.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Astros and Rangers enter the final weekend dead-even in a historically bad AL West race

Houston and Texas are both 79-80 with three games left, all but assuring the AL West will be won by a team with a losing record for the first time in a 162-game season. Houston holds the tiebreaker after beating Texas nine times this year, meaning identical final records hand the Astros the division, while the Rangers need to finish strictly ahead to take it.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Phillies down to a magic number of two as Diamondbacks chase the last playoff spot

Philadelphia sits two games up on Arizona for the National League’s final wild-card berth with three games left, needing just two wins or one more Padres or Diamondbacks loss to clinch. Arizona would still hold the tiebreaker if the two finish the weekend tied, keeping alive the last unresolved playoff seat in either league.

Source: Philadelphia Inquirer
