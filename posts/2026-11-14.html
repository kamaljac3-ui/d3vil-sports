NFL

Lamar Jackson's season likely over after thumb surgery

Ravens coach Jesse Minter confirmed Friday that Lamar Jackson had surgery on a broken and dislocated right thumb suffered in the Nov. 1 loss to Buffalo, and will miss 6-10 weeks on injured reserve. NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reports the MVP quarterback is likely done for the year, though Jackson himself has said he hopes to return in 2026; Jacoby Brissett takes over at quarterback.

Source: CBS Sports

49ers make the Keenan Allen trade official as Mike Evans sits a third straight week

San Francisco officially completed its trade for receiver Keenan Allen from Indianapolis for a 2027 sixth-round pick, adding a veteran option after ruling out Mike Evans (Achilles) for a third consecutive game. Deebo Samuel is also banged up, leaving Allen as the 49ers' most reliable healthy receiver heading into Sunday's game against Dallas.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Packers' run game takes a hit as Josh Jacobs needs finger surgery

Coach Matt LaFleur confirmed running back Josh Jacobs needs surgery on a fractured right ring finger, with Green Bay still deciding whether he'll need a trip to injured reserve. Jacobs is already out for Sunday's game against Minnesota alongside linebacker Micah Parsons (knee), with a possible 2-3 week recovery if IR isn't necessary.

Source: ESPN

CFB

Colorado's upset of No. 14 Houston might be the win that saves Deion Sanders' job

Colorado erased a 20-7 deficit with 28 unanswered points to beat No. 14 Houston 42-32 at Folsom Field on Friday night, likely ending the Cougars' College Football Playoff hopes. It's being called the biggest win of Deion Sanders' rocky Boulder tenure, improving the Buffaloes to 4-6 after a 20-27 start under him that had fueled buyout speculation.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Florida State wins again under interim coach Rick Stockstill, inching toward a bowl

Florida State ground out a 16-10 road win at Pittsburgh Friday night, its second straight under interim coach Rick Stockstill since Mike Norvell's firing, with Gabe Panikowski hitting three field goals and the defense forcing turnovers on downs on three of Pitt's final four possessions. The win leaves the Seminoles one victory from bowl eligibility, separate from their ongoing head-coaching search.

Source: ESPN

Robert Griffin III, with zero coaching experience, lands a Baylor interview

Robert Griffin III is expected to interview for Baylor's vacant head-coaching job, according to ESPN and On3, despite never having coached at any level. The former Heisman winner has publicly lobbied for the position, citing his Texas ties and the school's religious mission, as Baylor continues working through its search following Dave Aranda's October firing.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Adebayo and Antetokounmpo both drop 37 as the Heat roll to a third straight win

Bam Adebayo (37 points, 14 rebounds) and Giannis Antetokounmpo (37 points) matched season highs together as Miami beat Cleveland 133-121 in NBA Cup group play Friday night. Davion Mitchell added 22 points and 17 assists, and the Heat improved to 2-0 in East Group B on their third straight win.

Source: Fox Sports

Grizzlies tie an NBA record with 10 players in double figures in a 31-point rout

Memphis blew out New Orleans 142-111, with 10 players scoring in double digits to tie a league record and the bench alone outscoring most teams' starting units with 76 points. The win stretched the Grizzlies' road winning streak to six games, the best road start in franchise history, and keeps them alive for an NBA Cup wild-card spot after an 0-2 start.

Source: CBS Sports

Spurs blast a Curry-less Warriors by 38 in San Antonio

San Antonio routed Golden State 123-85 in the Warriors' first game since Stephen Curry's oblique tear was confirmed, with Stephon Castle leading the Spurs with 32 points. It's a lopsided result that underscores just how much Golden State's offense leans on its franchise centerpiece.

Source: ClutchPoints

NCAAM

UTSA and head coach Austin Claunch mutually agreed to part ways Friday, weeks after his Oct. 27 arrest in Corpus Christi on suspicion of driving while intoxicated led to his suspension. Source: Yahoo Sports

Several other programs, including Stonehill, Idaho State and Auburn, scrambled with bus travel or delays to make their games.

Source: ESPN

MLB

Baseball hands out unanimous Cy Young awards in both leagues for the first time ever

Yankees right-hander Cam Schlittler swept all 30 first-place votes for the AL Cy Young behind a 1.95 ERA and 239 strikeouts, and Brewers ace Jacob Misiorowski did the same in the NL with a league-best 1.80 ERA — the first time both leagues' Cy Youngs have been unanimous in the award's 70-year history. It follows an equally lopsided MVP race, capping a historic sweep of this year's BBWAA honors.

Source: MLB.com

Skubal's free-agency tour runs through the Mets before the Dodgers, with a $355 million price tag attached

With the GM Meetings underway in Las Vegas, ace Tarik Skubal is reportedly meeting with the Mets before heading to see the Dodgers, and one insider projection puts his next deal at roughly eight years and $355 million. Reports are split on whether the incumbent Dodgers or a competing Mets offer wins out, with the Blue Jays and Padres still in the mix.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Two roster deadlines close in as the sport braces for a lockout

Teams face a Nov. 17 deadline to add protected prospects to the 40-man roster ahead of the Rule 5 draft and a Nov. 20 non-tender deadline, both arriving just before the current labor deal expires Dec. 1 — the point at which a lockout is widely expected to begin. Early hot-stove moves have been modest, with Jake Bauers (Red Sox), Emilio Pagán (Tigers) and Evan Phillips (Braves) among the first free agents off the board.

Source: NBC Sports

The commission says it will need help from the legislature to keep covering the federal oversight costs going forward.
