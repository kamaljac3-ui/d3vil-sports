NFL

Seahawks' record $9.6 billion sale to the Khosla family officially closes

The sale — the largest in NFL history — closed this week after owners unanimously approved it in late August, ending three decades of Paul Allen-estate ownership. Vinod Khosla takes the chairman title while his wife Neeru becomes controlling owner, with the family (also part-owners of the 49ers) pledging to keep Seattle's culture intact as the defending-era Seahawks open a new season.

The Khosla family said: "We are honored and excited to begin this next chapter."

Source: NBC Sports

Hall of Fame overhauls its voting process after the Belichick snub

Reacting to backlash over Bill Belichick's rejection on his first Hall of Fame ballot, the Pro Football Hall of Fame is cutting its selection committee from 50 voters to 25, shrinking the modern-era finalist pool, folding the seniors category into one combined ballot, and moving voting back to an in-person February meeting. The Hall says the changes are meant to fix biases in how nominees get presented to voters.

Source: CBS Sports

Vikings bring back Harrison Smith for a 15th season

The 37-year-old safety agreed to a one-year deal worth up to $16.25 million with incentives, choosing to keep playing in Minnesota rather than retire despite a diminished 2025 campaign. It's a modest move, but it keeps a defensive locker-room anchor in place heading into Week 1.

NBA

League suspends Clippers owner Ballmer, strips five picks, clearing the way for a Kawhi Leonard trade

The NBA closed its salary-cap-circumvention investigation into the Clippers this week, suspending owner Steve Ballmer for a full year, fining the franchise a record $30 million, stripping five future first-round picks, and fining Kawhi Leonard $700,000 — while clearing Leonard himself of any suspension. The ruling removes the last obstacle to the Raptors-Clippers trade sending Leonard to Toronto that's been on hold since July.

ESPN's Shams Charania said: "This trade will be going through in the next few days."

Source: NPR

Ben Simmons returns to the league on a minimum deal with Sacramento

It's a low-cost, high-variance bet for Sacramento as Simmons enters his 10th pro season.

A second bidder emerges for the NBA's Seattle expansion franchise

BlackSun Private Equity has entered the race for the league's planned Seattle expansion team, valuing the franchise at $8.1 billion and proposing to build the arena on Tulalip tribal land near Marysville — a direct challenge to the Kraken-linked group that had been the only known bidder. Commissioner Adam Silver has said he hopes to decide on new Seattle and Las Vegas franchises by year's end.
