NFL

Patrick Mahomes throws for 184 yards and two touchdowns in his return from a torn ACL

Making his first start since tearing his ACL and LCL last December, Patrick Mahomes passed for 184 yards and two touchdowns and added a 15-yard rushing score as Kansas City routed Denver 31-10 in Monday night's opener. Free-agent signing Kenneth Walker III made an instant impact with 191 yards from scrimmage and two touchdowns in his Chiefs debut, while rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane set the tone with an early interception of Bo Nix.

Mahomes said afterward, "I've got to be better."

Source: SI.com

Cardinals shock the Chargers as 9.5-point underdogs behind Jacoby Brissett

Arizona pulled off Week 1's biggest upset, beating the heavily favored Chargers 26-14 on the road as Jacoby Brissett completed 27 of 37 passes for 277 yards and a touchdown with no turnovers. Tight end Trey McBride caught nine passes for 95 yards and a score, and the Cardinals controlled the ball for more than 37 minutes in new coach Mike LaFleur's winning debut.

Source: NBC Sports

Kyler Murray in concussion protocol as Carson Wentz rallies the Vikings past Green Bay

Minnesota quarterback Kyler Murray left Sunday's win over the Packers after a helmet-to-helmet hit and entered the concussion protocol, with backup Carson Wentz throwing three touchdown passes in relief to complete the comeback. Coach Kevin O'Connell said Monday that Murray is taking part in meetings but gave no timetable for his return, leaving his Week 2 status against the Bears uncertain.

O'Connell said Murray is "feeling pretty good."

Source: Star Tribune

CFB

Texas leapfrogs to No. 1 in the AP poll, and Oregon craters to No. 21

The new Top 25 released Sunday vaulted Texas to No. 1 after its comeback win over Ohio State, which fell out of the top five for the first time in two seasons. Oregon suffered the week's steepest fall, dropping 15 spots to No. 21 after its home loss to Oklahoma State, while Michigan cracked back into the rankings at No. 19 following its win over Oklahoma.

Source: NCAA.com

Walk-on-turned-kicker Caden Buhr lifts Iowa over Iowa State in the Cy-Hawk game

Buhr, a Bettendorf native who began his career as a walk-on, made three field goals including a 42-yarder with 15 seconds left to give Iowa a 16-13 win over its in-state rival at Kinnick Stadium. It's the Hawkeyes' first win in the series since 2021 and Buhr's first career game-winning kick.

Source: Hawkeyesports.com

NBA

Kawhi Leonard's trade back to Toronto is finally official

The NBA gave final approval Monday to send Kawhi Leonard from the Clippers back to the Raptors, ending a monthslong delay tied to the league's salary-cap-circumvention investigation into Los Angeles. Toronto sends Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks, a pick swap and two second-rounders back to the Clippers, and Leonard is expected to sign an extension and report to Raptors camp later this month.

Source: NBA.com

Nike reveals every team's City Edition jersey at once for the program's 10th anniversary

Instead of the usual staggered rollout, Nike and the NBA unveiled all 30 teams' 2026-27 City Edition uniforms simultaneously Monday, with several franchises bringing back fan-favorite designs from the program's first nine years rather than debuting new looks. Eight of the league's most storied franchises, including the Celtics, Lakers and Knicks, also received a new alternate "Specter Edition" jersey exclusive to their history.

Source: ColorWay Sports

NCAAM

Big East unveils its 2026-27 schedule, with a title-game rematch on the calendar

The conference released its full men's basketball schedule Sunday, with the double round-robin slate opening December 12 when Butler visits Providence in a matchup of two first-year head coaches. UConn and St. John's, who met in last season's Big East championship game, are set to rematch January 29, and the 2027 conference tournament returns to Madison Square Garden.

Source: Big East Conference

Nevada transfer Tayshawn Comer picks Washington, eligibility lawsuit still unresolved

The guard, who started 36 games for Nevada last season and helped it reach the NIT quarterfinals, committed to Washington this week to fill a backcourt need left by other departures. Comer is also one of more than 45 players from the 2022 recruiting class suing the NCAA in California for a fifth year of eligibility, so his actual standing for 2026-27 still hinges on how that case plays out.

Source: On3

WNBA

Lynx and Valkyries in a tight race for the WNBA's No. 1 seed as the league returns from its FIBA break

With all eight playoff teams now locked in, Minnesota holds a two-game lead over Golden State for the top seed heading into the season's final week, helped by the tiebreaker between the two. The Lynx's remaining schedule, including a home-and-home with Caitlin Clark and Indiana, is tougher than the Valkyries', making that stretch likely to decide who gets home-court advantage through the playoffs.

A'ja Wilson closing in on a record fifth MVP award

Minnesota rookie Olivia Miles has emerged as her top challenger after helping the Lynx to the league's best record, with a resurgent Caitlin Clark also entering the conversation.

MLB

Yankees punch their ninth playoff ticket in ten years

New York clinched a postseason berth Monday when Toronto blew a late lead and lost to Detroit, sealing at least a wild-card spot in a season that saw Aaron Judge miss more than three months with a rib stress fracture before returning September 8. It's New York's third straight trip to October, though the club still trails Tampa Bay by four games in the AL East race.

The near-miss included a long foul ball that just missed becoming career home run No. 300.

NHL

Cale Makar signs the richest contract in NHL history

The Avalanche defenseman agreed to an eight-year, $163.2 million extension worth $20.4 million a season, the highest average annual value ever for an NHL player, beating Tuesday's deadline after which the new collective bargaining agreement limits a team's extensions with its own player to seven years. The deal keeps last season's Norris-caliber blueliner in Colorado through 2034-35.

Source: NHL.com

Canucks lock up Zeev Buium, acquired in the Quinn Hughes trade, for eight years

Vancouver signed the 20-year-old defenseman to an eight-year, $75 million extension with a no-move clause, making him the franchise's highest-paid blueliner once it takes effect in 2027-28. Buium came to the Canucks as part of the return in last season's blockbuster trade of Quinn Hughes to Minnesota.

Source: ESPN

Senators racing the clock on a record extension for Drake Batherson

Ottawa and winger Drake Batherson were still negotiating an eight-year deal in the range of $10.5 to $11 million a season - which would be the richest contract in franchise history - with Tuesday's deadline for eight-year extensions bearing down. Talks were reportedly far apart as recently as Monday morning before accelerating overnight, with no deal confirmed as of Monday night.

Source: Pro Hockey Rumors
