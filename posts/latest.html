NFL

Patriots' TreVeyon Henderson hospitalized by a still-unexplained neurological episode

Patriots running back TreVeyon Henderson revealed Friday that he spent several days in the hospital after a sudden episode that affected his strength and coordination, and doctors still haven't identified a cause.
