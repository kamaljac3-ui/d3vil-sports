NFL

Jalen Hurts' blood clot spreads to his lungs, likely ending his season

Philadelphia placed Hurts on injured reserve Saturday after follow-up testing found the deep-vein clot in his calf had progressed into both lungs as pulmonary emboli. The team calls him clinically stable with a good long-term prognosis, but since he can't return to contact football while on blood thinners, he's expected to miss the rest of the season under a recovery timeline of roughly three months.

Source: ESPN

Rams' Davante Adams needs surgery, out 6-10 weeks with a torn hamstring tendon

Coach Sean McVay confirmed Monday that Adams suffered the injury on a noncontact play during Sunday's 18-16 loss to Arizona and will need surgery, with a possible return around Los Angeles' Week 11 bye. Adams had 30 catches, 480 yards and four touchdowns before going down, leaving the Rams without a proven third receiving option behind him and Puka Nacua.

Source: NBC Sports

Colts fire GM Chris Ballard after a 2-4 start

Owner Carlie Irsay-Gordon dismissed Ballard on Monday following a 26-20 loss to the previously winless Titans, ending a tenure that finished 72-82-1 with one playoff win. Assistant GM Ed Dodds takes over personnel duties for now, while defensive tackle DeForest Buckner vented publicly that he's "sick and tired of the same song and dance every year."

Source: NFL.com

CFB

Penn State's Matt Campbell faces a hot seat in year one, but his buyout may save him

The Nittany Lions have lost four straight and remain winless in conference play after a 21-18 defeat to Michigan, drawing weeks of fan boos toward the first-year coach, who responded, "There's a lot of season left." Campbell's fully guaranteed eight-year, $70.5 million deal carries a buyout north of $62 million — a number analysts say is making Penn State, and several other programs nationally, reluctant to make in-season coaching changes.

Source: Sportico

Steve Sarkisian's seat warms at Texas after a second straight loss

The Longhorns fell to Florida 27-24 at home, their second consecutive defeat, dropping Texas to 4-2 (1-2 SEC) and out of the AP top 10 to No. 19. Reports describe growing donor pressure for Sarkisian to hand off play-calling duties, though he's publicly committed to keeping Arch Manning as his starting quarterback.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NBA

Kings' Ben Simmons tears his Achilles in practice, out for the season

Simmons went down during a live drill at Sacramento's practice Sunday, and an MRI confirmed a torn Achilles that will require surgery and sideline him for all of 2026-27. He'd been in line to start after averaging 10.5 points, four rebounds and four assists in the preseason, and posted on social media calling the injury "another chapter," not the end of his career.

Source: NBC Sports

Steph Curry sprains his ankle days before the Warriors' opener

Curry was hurt in Golden State's open scrimmage Sunday, and while the initial worry was a knee issue, the team says it's a right ankle sprain that isn't serious. Coach Steve Kerr called him "fine," and Curry is expected to play Wednesday when the Warriors open their season at home against the Lakers.

Source: ESPN

NCAAM

Florida opens at No. 1 in the AP preseason poll for the first time since 2006

The Gators received 47 of 59 first-place votes in Monday's preseason AP Top 25, with Duke, Illinois, UConn and Arizona rounding out the top five. Defending national champion Michigan starts well back at No. 16, just ahead of Kansas at No. 17 and Kentucky at No. 23.

Source: CBS Sports

Kansas adds Purdue transfer Trey Kaufman-Renn under a court order, with his eligibility still in dispute

The Jayhawks signed Kaufman-Renn to replace injured freshman Grant Mordini after an Indiana court granted him a temporary restraining order letting him play while he fights for a sixth year of eligibility tied to a prior redshirt knee injury. Coach Bill Self said Kaufman-Renn enrolled in classes and began practicing Sunday, even with the underlying case still unresolved.

Source: ESPN

WNBA

Angel Reese's 30-16 night powers Atlanta to a rout, evening the WNBA Finals

The Dream beat the Valkyries 79-65 Monday night at Chase Center, breaking open a tied game with a 21-9 third quarter to level the series 1-1 — Atlanta's first-ever Finals win after an 0-10 start in franchise history. Reese finished with 30 points and 16 rebounds, just the third such line in Finals history, while Rhyne Howard set a Finals record with six steals.

Source: CBS Sports

Dream center Brionna Jones reportedly done for the season

Atlanta is believed to be without Jones for the rest of the Finals after a left leg injury kept her out of Monday's Game 2, a blow to the Dream's frontcourt depth just as the series shifts in their favor.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Series shifts to Atlanta's bigger arena for Wednesday's Game 3

With the Finals tied, Game 3 moves to State Farm Arena — the Hawks' roughly 17,000-seat building, not the Dream's normal 3,500-seat home — after an earlier scheduling conflict forced the switch. Game 4 follows Saturday at the same arena.

Source: ESPN

NCAAW

UConn blitzes Texas in an 83-61 exhibition rout

Sarah Strong scored 27 and Jovana Popovic added 22 as the Huskies pulled away from a 37-37 halftime tie with a dominant second half Monday in Hartford. Madison Booker led Texas with 23 points in the loss.

Source: Yahoo Sports

USC's JuJu Watkins targets Oct. 25 return from her ACL tear

The reigning Big Ten Preseason Player of the Year is set to make her long-awaited comeback in USC's exhibition against Cal on Oct. 25 at the Galen Center, roughly a year after tearing her ACL. A USC practice player said she's "looking really good" and improving daily, though some rust is expected.

Source: USC Annenberg Media

MLB

Rays close out the Guardians in Game 6, clinch the AL pennant

Tampa Bay beat Cleveland 7-3 Monday night at Tropicana Field to take the ALCS 4-2, with Freddy Peralta earning series MVP after 5 1/3 strong innings and Junior Caminero and Yandy Diaz each hitting two-run homers. Cleveland loaded the bases in the eighth, but reliever Kevin Kelly struck out Brayan Rocchio to end the threat and send the Rays to the World Series.

Source: CBC Sports

World Series set: Rays-Brewers opens Friday in Milwaukee

The Brewers, who swept the Dodgers in the NLCS, host Game 1 Friday night with home-field advantage, pitting Jacob Misiorowski against Tampa Bay's Drew Rasmussen. Freddy Peralta — traded away by Milwaukee earlier this year — is lined up to start Game 3 in Tampa Bay, setting up a reunion storyline against his former team.

Source: ESPN

NHL

Red Wings trade of captain Dylan Larkin reported imminent

ESPN's John Buccigross says he expects Detroit to trade Larkin within seven to ten days, with Minnesota and Montreal described as the most aggressive suitors and Florida, San Jose, the Rangers and Pittsburgh also involved. Larkin, who has a no-trade clause and requested a deal after Detroit missed the playoffs for a 10th straight season, is reportedly commanding "three or four pieces" in return, per TSN's Darren Dreger.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Rangers roll to their best home start since 1990-91

New York beat Anaheim 5-2 at Madison Square Garden Monday night for its sixth straight win to open the season at home, with Oliver Bjorkstrand scoring twice and Sean Durzi opening the scoring just 78 seconds in. Backup goalie Dylan Garand made 29 saves in his fifth consecutive start since Igor Shesterkin went down with a season-ending injury.

Source: AP via ABC News

Jets' disciplinary suspension of Connor Hellebuyck set to expire Friday

Winnipeg's team-imposed suspension of the holdout goalie, issued after he skipped training camp, is slated to end Oct. 23, though it hasn't been officially confirmed. Hellebuyck has told the team he wants to end the holdout but still wants a trade, and captain Adam Lowry acknowledged there "are going to have to be conversations" if he shows back up.

Source: Daily Faceoff

UFC

UFC 333 fight week arrives: Volkanovski-Evloev headlines, with the Yan-Dvalishvili trilogy as co-main

Saturday's card at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi is topped by featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski defending against unbeaten contender Movsar Evloev, with the bantamweight title trilogy between Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili serving as the co-main event — their rubber match after splitting the first two meetings. Open workouts are Wednesday at Yas Mall, with ceremonial weigh-ins Friday evening.

Source: Bloody Elbow

Dana White disputes Sean Strickland's pay complaints as the standoff drags on

White said Strickland "just signed a new contract in May" and called the fighter's cited pay figures wrong, though he offered no alternative number and said he hasn't spoken to Strickland directly. The dispute has pushed Strickland's planned middleweight title defense against Nassourdine Imavov off the 2026 calendar, with Strickland floating everything from vacating the belt to a boxing match with Jake Paul.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Boxing

WBA's deadline for a Teofimo Lopez-Jack Catterall unification closes Thursday

The WBA-ordered negotiating window for the two welterweight titleholders expires at close of business Thursday, after which the sanctioning body can order a purse bid if no deal is reached. Lopez wants a WBC unification with Ryan Garcia instead and is reportedly prepared to challenge the WBA's order legally, while Catterall says he intends to pursue the mandatory shot he's earned.

Source: Ring Magazine

Filip Hrgovic calls for a 2027 unification rematch with Daniel Dubois

Two days after Dubois stopped Fabio Wardley to retain his WBO heavyweight title, IBF champion Hrgovic publicly challenged him to a unification bout, vowing to finish it this time. His manager said a mandatory defense against Frank Sanchez likely comes first: "We'll probably fight Sanchez and get that out of the way and then do the bigger fight afterwards."

Source: BoxingNews24

David Benavidez's cruiserweight unification bout with Michal Cieslak reportedly targeted for early December

The WBC has ordered Benavidez, its mandatory challenger, into a unification fight with cruiserweight champion Cieslak, with reports pointing to a Dec. 5 date and a possible Phoenix venue, though the exact date has shifted in earlier reporting. The winner is expected to be next in line for a Jai Opetaia showdown targeted for Cinco de Mayo weekend 2027.

Source: DAZN

Golf

Ruoning Yin wins the Buick LPGA Shanghai

China's Yin closed at 11-under 277 to win her latest LPGA title on home soil, edging South Korea's Hye-Jin Choi by one stroke, with Amy Yang finishing third. The tour heads next to the BMW Ladies Championship in South Korea.

Source: LPGA.com

Bermuda Championship field locks in with five past champions ahead of Thursday's start

The 120-player field at Port Royal Golf Course includes former winners Brendon Todd, Seamus Power, Camilo Villegas, Rafa Campos and defending champion Adam Schenk, plus first-time PGA Tour starter Austin Lemieux — son of NHL Hall of Famer Mario Lemieux. Ben James enters as the betting favorite.

Source: Bernews

Horse Racing

Sovereignty breezes again at Saratoga, with a final Keeneland work still to come

The reigning Horse of the Year worked five furlongs in 1:02.76 Sunday, a time trainer Bill Mott called "a very typical work for what we have been used to seeing from him," attributing the modest clocking partly to a deep training track. Mott plans to ship Sovereignty to Keeneland for one more breeze before the Oct. 31 Breeders' Cup Classic.

Jose Ortiz commits to Golden Tempo over Magnitude in the Classic

After working the dual classic winner five furlongs at Keeneland last week, Ortiz confirmed he'll ride Golden Tempo rather than Magnitude in the $7 million Breeders' Cup Classic. The Puma also worked that week and remains among the Classic's probable runners.

Source: BloodHorse

Magnitude posts the fastest Keeneland breeze among 24 Classic hopefuls

Flavien Prat guided Magnitude through a five-furlong move in 1:00.60 Saturday, matching his prior work and topping every other horse recorded at that distance that morning. Prat called it a "good, good breeze," and trainer Steve Asmussen confirmed he'll keep the mount for the Classic.

Source: Yahoo Sports
