CFB

USC stuns No. 2 Ohio State, reviving Lincoln Riley's job security and CFP hopes

USC beat the Buckeyes 30-18 at home Saturday night, leaning on defensive discipline — Ohio State committed 11 penalties — despite being outgained by 126 yards. True freshman Luke Fahey, making his first career start at quarterback for Ohio State, never got the offense rolling, and the win pushes USC to 7-9 and squarely back into playoff contention.

Defending champion Indiana survives Minnesota in overtime after a halftime QB benching

Indiana improves to 8-1 defending last year's playoff run, though the finish drew criticism for Cignetti not using a late timeout in regulation.

WNBA

Golden State wins the WNBA's first-ever Game 7, Gabby Williams named Finals MVP

The Valkyries beat the Dream 68-63 on Saturday night in the first winner-take-all Game 7 in league history, clinching the franchise's first title in just its second season of existence. Williams scored 30 points, including a postseason-record 28 in the second half, to claim Finals MVP; Atlanta got 18 points from Jordin Canada and 12 rebounds from Angel Reese in a loss that extends the Dream's run of Finals appearances without a championship.

Source: NBC Sports
