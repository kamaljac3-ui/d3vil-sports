NFL

Browns hand the ball back to Watson, pulling Sanders after just one start of run

ESPN says it's aware of the situation and in contact with Ryan, leaving his on-air role unresolved for now.

Golf

McIlroy chases a record-tying eighth Race to Dubai crown at the season finale

Rory McIlroy trails Eugenio Chacarra by roughly 534 points entering this week's DP World Tour Championship at Jumeirah Golf Estates, with Patrick Reed also mathematically alive. McIlroy wins the title outright with a victory, while Chacarra needs to win and have McIlroy finish outside the top two to deny him what would be a record-equaling eighth order-of-merit title.

Source: PGA Tour

LPGA scraps warning shots, toughens slow-play fines for 2027

The LPGA told players during The Annika that it's eliminating warning shots for slow play next season: fines kick in immediately, the per-shot clock shrinks from 70 to 60 seconds, and the fine threshold on timed holes drops from 40 seconds to 35. The crackdown follows a 2026 season in which pace of play showed no improvement.

Source: Golf.com

Marshall Gramm's HISA hearing paused for mediation

HISA board panel chair Joseph De Francis granted a joint motion to pause owner Marshall Gramm's disciplinary hearing while both sides pursue mediation over allegations he used AI-assisted code to improperly pull roughly four million confidential veterinary records from HISA's portal. No mediation date is set, and HISA's attorney has signaled the agency will likely seek a lifetime ban if the case returns to a hearing.
