NFL

Days after his torn ACL from a sack-celebration landing was made official, Hutchinson posted a reflective message processing the end of his season, admitting the injury has forced a change in how he'll play going forward. It's a personal coda to a six-sack campaign cut short through nine games for a Lions defense that has struggled much of the year.

"That's the last time I go airborne," Hutchinson wrote.

Dallas placed cornerback DaRon Bland on injured reserve Saturday with a high-ankle sprain suffered in last week's loss to San Francisco, ruling him out at least four games with practice-squad call-up Derion Kendrick taking his spot. It's the second straight season Bland's year has ended early on IR, and multiple outlets are now openly wondering whether his time in Dallas is winding down.

CFB

Washington stuns defending national champion Indiana in overtime

Washington beat No. 6 Indiana 27-24 in overtime in Seattle on Tyler Robles' 46-yard field goal, after Indiana missed a potential game-tying kick in the final seconds of regulation. The loss drops Indiana to 9-2 and is being called a potentially crippling blow to the defending champions' playoff hopes, a sharp turn after coach Curt Cignetti spent the week publicly lobbying the CFP committee for a higher ranking.

Source: Yahoo Sports/AP

Nebraska ends a 31-game skid against ranked teams by knocking out Ohio State's playoff hopes

Nebraska beat No. 14 Ohio State 31-28 in Lincoln, snapping a 31-game losing streak against AP-ranked opponents behind three touchdown passes from Anthony Colandrea. Buckeyes receiver Jeremiah Smith tied a single-season school receiving-touchdown record with two more scores, but it wasn't enough — the loss drops Ohio State to 7-4 and ends both its playoff and Big Ten title hopes.

Source: ESPN

No. 1 Georgia routs South Carolina to lock up a spot in the SEC title game

Georgia beat South Carolina 34-6 in Columbia to close the regular season 10-1 and clinch its berth in the SEC Championship Game. Nate Frazier ran for 94 yards and two touchdowns, and quarterback Gunner Stockton went 19-of-23 for 281 yards in a game that was never close.

NBA

Victor Wembanyama sets an NBA first with a 50-point, 18-rebound, 5-block, 5-three game

Wembanyama scored 50 points with 18 rebounds, 5 blocks, 5 three-pointers and 4 steals as San Antonio routed New Orleans 114-91 — the first 50-point, 5-block, 5-three-pointer game by anyone since the NBA adopted the three-point line in 1979-80. It was his third career 50-point game and included a personal-best 17 points in the first quarter alone.

"Of course I had to show off for Selena," Wembanyama said, on Selena Gomez attending.

Source: NBA.com

Tyler Herro's career-high 50 isn't enough to stop the Bucks' freefall to 1-16

Herro scored a career-high 50 points on 15-of-23 shooting, adding 11 rebounds and 7 assists, but Milwaukee still fell 132-129 at Philadelphia after a potential tying three left his hand just after the final buzzer. The Bucks had no replay challenges left to contest a missed call on the previous possession, and their record fell to a league-worst 1-16.

Joel Embiid called Herro "a top-5 player in the league" afterward.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Mikal Bridges' buzzer-beating three salvages a near-collapse for the Knicks

Bridges hit a three at the final buzzer to beat Portland 97-96 after New York let a big lead evaporate late, with Jalen Brunson adding 30 points. It's the second straight wild finish for a Knicks team that's shown it can win ugly as well as comfortably this season.

Source: KELO/wire

Notre Dame's Hannah Hidalgo becomes the first Division I player ever to reach a 2,500/500/500/500 line

Hidalgo posted 31 points, 7 rebounds, 7 assists and 4 steals in a rout of DePaul earlier this month, becoming the first player in Division I history — men's or women's — to reach 2,500 career points, 500 rebounds, 500 assists and 500 steals. The milestone capped a dominant start to her junior season at Notre Dame.

MLB

MLB lockout now looks all but inevitable as the Dec. 1 deadline nears

With the current labor agreement expiring at 11:59 p.m. ET on Dec. 1, commissioner Rob Manfred said he "can't imagine" owners ratifying a new deal without a salary cap and floor, with MLB reportedly pushing for roughly a $258 million cap and $180 million floor. A lockout would freeze all free-agent and trade activity, which is why teams are racing to close deals — like a reported nine-year offer to Tarik Skubal — before the clock runs out.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Giants reverse course, now open to trading ace Logan Webb

San Francisco is listening to offers for Logan Webb, according to new reporting, a reversal from earlier this month when the team said it had no plans to move him. Webb has two years left on his five-year, $90 million deal and posted a 4.31 ERA in 27 starts this season, though other reporting suggests an actual trade remains unlikely since the Giants are more focused on adding pitching than subtracting it.

Source: NBC Sports Bay Area

Freddy Peralta's free-agent market heats up, with the Rays "seriously engaged"

Roughly six teams — including the Rays, White Sox, Padres, Cubs and Blue Jays — are pursuing free-agent right-hander Freddy Peralta, with Tampa Bay described as unusually aggressive for a club that rarely spends big in free agency. Peralta turned his season around after an August trade from the Mets to Tampa Bay, posting a 3.25 ERA over his final 55.1 innings, and is reaching free agency for the first time at 30.

Source: NBC Sports

Boxing

WBA disputes Oscar De La Hoya's claim that a Teofimo Lopez-Ryan Garcia fight is cleared

The WBA says no special permit has been granted letting Teofimo Lopez fight Ryan Garcia instead of mandatory challenger Jack Catterall, directly contradicting a claim from promoter Oscar De La Hoya that the sanctioning body had signed off. Lopez's camp says it filed for the permit on Nov. 5, but the WBA's own Nov. 11 ruling says no formal request existed at that time, leaving the proposed unification fight in limbo.

"No special permit has been granted to Teofimo Lopez," said WBA president Gilberto Mendoza.

Ali's grandson Nico Ali Walsh has backed this version after objecting to an earlier House draft.
