NCAAM

LSU legend Dale Brown dies at 90

Dale Brown, LSU's all-time winningest men's basketball coach, died Friday at age 90, just days after the school announced he'd receive the 2027 John R. Wooden Legends of Coaching Award. Brown went 448-301 across 25 seasons from 1972 to 1997, leading the Tigers to two Final Fours and famously recruiting and developing Shaquille O'Neal.

Source: CBS Sports
