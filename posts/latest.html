NFL

Rams safety ejected for the helmet-to-helmet hit that concussed Packers receiver Christian Watson

Los Angeles beat Green Bay 26-13 in the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game Wednesday night, but the result was overshadowed when safety Kamren Kinchens was disqualified for leading with his head into a defenseless Watson on a fourth-down play. Watson was ruled out with a concussion at halftime and has entered the league's protocol; Kinchens said afterward he "came with no ill intent."

NBA

Kevin Durant becomes the fifth player in NBA history to reach 33,000 career points

Durant hit the milestone on a three-pointer in Houston's 116-104 win over Phoenix on Wednesday, finishing with 20 points and 10 rebounds at age 38. He joins LeBron James, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Karl Malone and Kobe Bryant in the club, trailing Bryant by roughly 637 points for fourth place.

Source: NBA.com

LeBron James sets his own age record with his first triple-double as a 76er

James posted 25 points, 12 assists and 10 rebounds in a 111-104 NBA Cup win over Miami on Wednesday — his 126th career triple-double and a new mark for the oldest player to log one. Tyrese Maxey added 30 points for Philadelphia.

Source: NBA.com

Wembanyama's 44 points aren't enough as the Spurs fall to Portland in Cup play

Victor Wembanyama scored 44 points in San Antonio's 116-111 loss to the Blazers on Wednesday, with Deni Avdija (33) and Damian Lillard (29) leading Portland. The Spurs were again missing De'Aaron Fox with a shin injury.

SEC sets a Dec. 3 vote on whether to expel LSU amid the RJ Luis eligibility fight

MLB

MLB sues its own players' union days before the lockout deadline

Major League Baseball filed a federal lawsuit against the MLBPA on Wednesday, asking a court to declare in advance that a post-CBA-expiration lockout would be legal and can't be blocked by an antitrust injunction. The league says it's trying to keep negotiations alive ahead of the Dec. 1 deadline, but the union calls the suit "completely frivolous" and accuses MLB of seeking leverage through the courts instead of at the table.

Source: Washington Post

Tarik Skubal's record $450 million deal with the Mets is now finalized

The two-time Cy Young winner's nine-year contract — the richest ever for a pitcher — was finalized this week with full no-trade protection and no opt-outs, closing out an agreement first reached over the weekend.

Source: KIRO 7

A wave of hot-stove deals broke loose this week as the lockout deadline nears
