White Abarrio's owners settle their Breeders' Cup-scratch lawsuit

The owners of White Abarrio dismissed their lawsuit against the Breeders' Cup and Del Mar "with prejudice" and reached a separate resolution with the California Horse Racing Board, which now takes responsibility for the colt's 2025 race-day scratch rather than the event or track. The original suit had sought more than $10 million in damages tied to the horse's stallion value.
