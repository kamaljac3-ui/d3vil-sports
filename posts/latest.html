NFL

Colts bench Daniel Jones, hand the offense to Anthony Richardson

Indianapolis released Daniel Jones on Friday after he went 3-8 as the starter this season, completing just 58% of his passes with a league-worst 13 interceptions. Coach Shane Steichen named Anthony Richardson the starter beginning next week against the Giants, even though Richardson still faces a possible four-game suspension tied to an October marijuana charge.

Source: NFL.com

Patriots' Drake Maye likely done for the season with a fractured fibula

Imaging confirmed New England quarterback Drake Maye fractured his right fibula in Sunday's loss to Detroit in Munich, and while he won't need surgery, he's expected to miss most or all of the rest of the year. Coach Mike Vrabel said he "wouldn't imagine him being available" once the team returns from its bye, leaving Tommy DeVito to run the offense, while receiver A.J. Brown is separately expected to miss multiple weeks with a hamstring injury.

Source: ESPN

Nacua's suspension complicated by surgery the NFL reportedly didn't know about

Beyond the three-game suspension already handed down this week, new reporting says Rams receiver Puka Nacua underwent MCL surgery and is likely out for the season — and that the league wasn't fully aware of the injury's extent when it issued the discipline.

Source: Yahoo Sports

CFB

Texas A&M benches Marcel Reed for Brady Hart against Oklahoma

Coach Mike Elko is turning to backup Brady Hart over struggling starter Marcel Reed for Saturday's game against Oklahoma, continuing a switch that began late in last week's loss to Tennessee. Reed looked like a Heisman contender in 2025 but has thrown nine interceptions against just eight touchdowns this season as the Aggies have fallen to 4-6 overall and 1-6 in the SEC.

Source: CBS Sports

No. 14 Oregon stays alive in the playoff race, routs Michigan State 45-7

Oregon improved to 8-3 overall and 6-2 in the Big Ten with a lopsided road win Friday night, getting 121 yards and two touchdowns from running back Brandon Smith. Michigan State fell to 3-8 as its lost season continued.

Source: ESPN

CFP rankings shuffle: Wake Forest in, Kentucky out, and Alabama's No. 3 spot questioned

The committee's latest rankings made just one change to the projected field, slotting Wake Forest into the last at-large spot after its win over SMU and bumping Kentucky out. The top four held steady, but analysts remain split on whether two-loss Alabama deserves to sit at No. 3 ahead of undefeated UCLA.

Source: Athlon Sports

NBA

James Harden's 51-point outburst moves him past Kobe Bryant

Harden scored 51 points on 15-of-26 shooting, including seven threes, to lead Cleveland past Indiana 128-119 in NBA Cup group play Friday, outscoring the Pacers 43-23 in the third quarter alone. It was his 26th career 50-point game, pushing him past Kobe Bryant into third place on the all-time list, behind only Wilt Chamberlain and Michael Jordan.

Source: ESPN

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander erupts for 40 as a shorthanded Thunder rally past the Pelicans

Gilgeous-Alexander scored 40 points with eight assists as Oklahoma City erased a 16-point third-quarter deficit to beat New Orleans 126-120 in NBA Cup play, his fourth 40-point game of the season. Rookie Aday Mara added career highs of 22 points and 16 rebounds off the bench.

Source: Washington Post via AP

Shorthanded Raptors snap a six-game skid, beating the Pistons without Kawhi Leonard

Toronto ended its longest losing streak of the season with a 105-102 road win over Detroit, doing it without Kawhi Leonard, RJ Barrett and Collin Murray-Boyles. Scottie Barnes outdueled Detroit's Cade Cunningham in the closing minutes as the Raptors improved to 2-1 in NBA Cup group play.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAM

VCU erases an 18-point deficit to stun unbeaten Santa Clara in overtime

VCU trailed by 18 points in the second half at Santa Clara before an 11-0 run forced overtime, where Lazar Djokovic's go-ahead basket sealed an 83-77 road win. Blake Harper led the Rams with 28 points, and the comeback snapped Santa Clara's unbeaten start to the season.

Source: VCU Athletics

Unbeaten Indiana outlasts Kentucky at a neutral site in Indianapolis

Indiana trailed 47-45 at halftime before outscoring Kentucky 45-29 after the break to win 90-76 at Lucas Oil Stadium, improving to 6-0 on the season. Sixth man Bryce Lindsay led the Hoosiers with 20 points, while Kentucky's Milan Momcilovic scored 26 in the loss.

Source: Indiana University Athletics

WNBA

Sparks sale closes in on a record price with two front-runners emerging

New reporting identifies Jose Feliciano and Kwanza Jones as the leading bidders to buy the Sparks from owner Mark Walter, with the price reported between $705 million and $750 million — which would be a record sale price in the WNBA. A rival group linked to Pau Gasol, which Vanessa Bryant has publicly denied joining, remains in the mix, and a deal could close within weeks pending league approval.

Source: Sportico

WNBA's search for its next commissioner picks up steam

More than a dozen candidates are under consideration to replace Cathy Engelbert, who is retiring, with the league hoping to name a successor before the end of the year. NBA deputy commissioner Mark Tatum, who has filled in on an acting basis before, is seen as a fallback option if the search firm's outside candidates don't pan out.

Source: Washington Post

NCAAW

Northwestern State guard collapses on the court during a blowout loss to Oklahoma

Guard Stephie Salumu lost consciousness near midcourt while retreating on defense during a 113-43 loss at No. 14 Oklahoma, stopping play for several minutes while medical staff attended to her. She walked off with help and did not return, and as of this week no outlet has reported a diagnosis or update on her condition.

Source: KRMG

Six former Iowa State players demand an independent review of coach Bill Fennelly

Beyond the one-game suspension already handed down this month, six former players — two named, four anonymous — sent Iowa State's president and athletic director a letter alleging a pattern of "verbal, emotional, and psychological abuse" over Fennelly's 32-year tenure and calling for an outside, trauma-informed review. The school has acknowledged receiving the letter but hasn't committed to one.

Source: Yahoo Sports

MLB

Dodgers land Royce Lewis from the Twins in a rotation-for-infield swap

Los Angeles acquired infielder Royce Lewis, the former No. 1 overall pick, from Minnesota in exchange for right-hander Bobby Miller and a catching prospect. Lewis fills a hole at the corner infield after Miguel Rojas's retirement, and the trade also spares the Twins a projected $5.1 million arbitration salary for a player who hit just .227 with 15 home runs last season.

Source: MLB.com

Diamondbacks add injury-prone right-hander Johan Oviedo from Boston

Arizona acquired Johan Oviedo from the Red Sox for a minor-league reliever and cash, a boom-or-bust depth move after losing both Michael Soroka and Zac Gallen in free agency. Oviedo missed almost all of 2026 with a flexor strain on top of 2024-25 Tommy John surgery, but can't be sent to the minors without his consent, making him a likely rotation piece on Opening Day.

Source: MLB Trade Rumors

Mets reportedly offer Tarik Skubal nearly $400 million to beat the lockout clock

With MLB's collective bargaining agreement set to expire Dec. 1 and a lockout considered likely, the Mets are reported to have put a nine-year, roughly $400 million offer in front of free-agent ace Tarik Skubal. Insiders expect top free agents like Skubal to get deals done before the deadline even as the rest of this year's free-agent class braces for a slower market.

Source: BVM Sports

NHL

Ovechkin passes Backstrom for the Capitals' all-time assists record

Alex Ovechkin picked up his 763rd career assist in a loss to Buffalo, passing longtime linemate Nicklas Backstrom for the franchise record. The milestone comes amid an unusual scoring slump for Ovechkin, who has just one goal in his last 20 games.

Source: RussianMachineNeverBreaks

Wild riding the best start in franchise history

Minnesota's 4-1 win over Calgary extended its point streak to 13 games and its road-win streak to nine, pushing the Wild to 17-3-4 with the NHL's longest active point streak and a league-best 38 points. It's being called the best start in franchise history.

Source: MPR News

UFC

UFC Qatar brings Prochazka-Stirling to Doha tonight

Jiri Prochazka, the No. 2-ranked light heavyweight, headlines against undefeated No. 5 Navajo Stirling in Stirling's first UFC main event, with both fighters making weight at Friday's weigh-ins. The card also features Aljamain Sterling against Kevin Vallejos and Dan Hooker against Brian Ortega.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Ranked flyweight Manon Fiorot suspended three months for a positive drug test

Combat Sports Anti-Doping handed France's Manon Fiorot, the UFC's No. 4-ranked flyweight, a three-month suspension after a September sample tested positive for ostarine. The agency reduced the penalty, citing likely supplement contamination and a clean follow-up test, with her ineligibility running through mid-January.

Source: UFC.com

New UFC signee's knockout win clouded by a failed drug test

Igor Cavalcanti, who earned a UFC contract with a roughly 90-second knockout on Dana White's Contender Series in September, has been provisionally suspended by Nevada regulators after testing positive for a nandrolone metabolite. His manager says the result traces to knee-injury injections given months before he knew he'd get a UFC deal, and no final ruling has been made on a penalty or the win itself.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Boxing

Lauren Price sweeps the cards to become undisputed welterweight champion

Price beat Michaela Kotaskova by lopsided unanimous decision in Cardiff, becoming Wales' first undisputed four-belt female world champion. The win completes a title unification that had been building for weeks after Mikaela Mayer vacated the WBO belt.

Source: The News, via AFP

Gabriela Fundora retains her undisputed flyweight title with a wide decision

Fundora dropped Jasmina Zapotoczna twice, including a body-shot knockdown in the seventh round, en route to a lopsided unanimous decision in Indio, California. On the same card, Amari Jones stopped DeAundre Pettus in the eighth round.

Source: BoxingScene

Haney-Paro unification fight in jeopardy over a drug-testing standoff

IBF titleholder Liam Paro is publicly pressing WBO champion Devin Haney for not yet enrolling in VADA testing ahead of their Dec. 19 unification bout in San Francisco, with one official telling BoxingScene the fight is "in jeopardy." The dispute echoes Haney's 2024 bout with Ryan Garcia, which was later overturned after Garcia failed a drug test.

Source: BoxingScene

Golf

Three-way tie atop the RSM Classic, with PGA Tour cards on the line

Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen, Michael Thorbjornsen and Marco Penge share the lead at 9-under through two rounds of the PGA Tour's season-ending event at Sea Island. Neergaard-Petersen and Penge both began the week outside the top 100 in the FedExCup Fall standings and are now projected to keep their full 2027 cards, while Billy Horschel and Tony Finau missed the cut and lost theirs.

Source: AP via ClickOrlando

A Lim Kim leads the LPGA's season finale as Nelly Korda's Hall of Fame bid stalls

Kim holds a three-shot lead at 15-under through two rounds of the CME Group Tour Championship in Naples, Florida, ahead of two-time defending champion Jeeno Thitikul. Nelly Korda, who needs just one point to clinch the LPGA Hall of Fame, sits six shots back, putting her clinching this week in doubt.

Source: Golf Channel

Horse Racing

Supreme Court to decide whether to take up two challenges to horse racing's federal watchdog

The Court has scheduled a Dec. 4 conference on whether to review two cases challenging the constitutionality of the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Act, after one federal appeals court found its enforcement powers unconstitutional while another upheld them. A grant would only decide whether the justices hear the case, not decide it outright, with oral argument likely months away.

Source: Thoroughbred Daily News

Cosmic Crusader wins the Group 1 Railway Stakes in Perth

Cosmic Crusader, ridden by William Pike, won the A$1.5 million Railway Stakes at Ascot Racecourse in Perth, Australia, taking home the roughly $822,000 winner's share ahead of King of Light, Western Empire and Bustler.

Source: Sportstralia
