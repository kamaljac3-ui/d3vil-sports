NFL

Jacobs and Cooper removed from the Commissioner Exempt List

The NFL is lifting Josh Jacobs and Jonathon Cooper off the Commissioner Exempt List, where both had sat since Aug. 30 while under a personal-conduct-policy review. Cooper can suit up immediately for Denver's Week 7 game against Arizona, while Jacobs returns in time for Green Bay's game versus Detroit; the league says the underlying conduct review continues even as both players are cleared to play.

NCAAM

NCAA sues Ohio's governor over an order shielding "extra-year" players

The NCAA filed a federal lawsuit against Gov. Mike DeWine on Friday over his executive order letting Ohio colleges ignore a new NCAA rule that cuts off eligibility for 2022 high-school graduates who've already used four seasons — several of them basketball players caught in the state's ongoing eligibility fights. The NCAA argues the order violates the Commerce Clause by favoring in-state schools, with president Charlie Baker calling it an "extraordinary step."

Source: ESPN

The game doubled as a reunion with Beavers coach Justin Joyner, a former Michigan assistant now in his first season running his own program.

MLB
