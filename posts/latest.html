NFL

Eagles erase a 21-point deficit to stun the Cowboys 39-38

Philadelphia trailed 31-10 in the fourth quarter before a blocked-punt touchdown ignited the rally, backup Joe Milton threw for a career-high 331 yards and four touchdowns, and Jake Elliott's late field goal won it after Dallas failed on fourth-and-1. It's being called the largest comeback in Thanksgiving-game history.

Source: ESPN

Source: NFL.com
