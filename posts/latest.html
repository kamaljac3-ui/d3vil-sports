NFL

Bears land All-Pro Jeffery Simmons in a blockbuster deal with the Titans

Chicago sent Tennessee its 2027 and 2028 first-round picks plus a 2027 fourth-rounder (getting a 2028 fifth back) for three-time All-Pro defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, a bold swing by a 5-2 Bears team ahead of the Nov. 10 trade deadline. Titans GM Mike Borgonzi said he had no plans to move Simmons until Bears GM Ryan Poles called with an offer he felt he couldn't turn down, and reaction around the league is split on whether Chicago overpaid for a rebuilding team's best player.

Source: ESPN

Colts' fire sale widens: Quenton Nelson dealt, Josh Downs reportedly wants out

Indianapolis shipped longtime guard Quenton Nelson to Seattle for draft capital, and receiver Josh Downs has reportedly asked for a trade of his own as the Colts continue clearing veterans out, even though star running back Jonathan Taylor has not asked to leave. It's the clearest sign yet that Indianapolis plans to be a seller again as the deadline nears.

Source: ABC7 New York
