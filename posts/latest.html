NFL

Colts trade away Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner in a mid-season sell-off

Indianapolis shipped six-time Pro Bowl guard Quenton Nelson to Seattle for a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth, then sent DeForest Buckner back to San Francisco for a 2027 fourth-rounder, continuing interim GM Ed Dodds' teardown of a 2-5 roster. The moves pile more future draft capital onto a rebuild that began with the firing of longtime GM Chris Ballard.

Source: NFL.com

Jonathan Taylor's situation gets murkier as the trade deadline nears

Fresh reporting says Colts owner Carlie Irsay-Gordon doesn't want to trade the star running back, even as the front office is said to be willing to field offers from the Seahawks, Packers, Broncos and Vikings before the Nov. 10 deadline. The mixed signals leave Taylor's future unresolved as Indianapolis keeps dealing away veterans around him.

Source: NBC Sports

Injury wave hits All, Tracy Jr. and McCaffrey

The Bengals placed tight end Erick All on IR with a believed-torn ACL suffered against Baltimore, the Giants put running back Tyrone Tracy Jr. on IR with a hamstring injury, and 49ers star Christian McCaffrey left San Francisco's game against Atlanta with a thigh/hamstring issue. All three teams now head into the stretch run thinner at a key skill spot.

Source: Heavy.com

CFB

Oregon fires offensive coordinator Drew Mehringer

Oregon dismissed Mehringer the day after a 21-10 loss at Illinois dropped the Ducks to 4-3 and out of the AP rankings, having opened the season at No. 2. The offense has fallen to 88th nationally in rushing and 40th in scoring, and no replacement has been named yet.

Source: ESPN

Candidate names surface for the Maryland, NC State and Baylor openings

Early speculation links Maryland to names like Jake Dickert, Jason Eck and Charles Huff; NC State to Philip Rivers drawing heavy buzz alongside Shannon Dawson and Dan Mullen; and Baylor to Spencer Danielson, Jeff Traylor and a reportedly interested Robert Griffin III. Free-agent coach Brian Kelly is said to be courting all three jobs at once.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Auburn dismissed general manager Brian Kloman after he allegedly withheld knowledge that recruit Mantas Rubstavicius had taken college courses in Europe during the player's NCAA eligibility lawsuit. Source: ESPN

Ole Miss settles NCAA tampering case; Chris Beard avoids personal discipline

The NCAA hit Ole Miss with two years' probation and a $455,000 fine over former assistant Wes Flanigan's improper contact with a transfer-portal player's adviser, but head coach Chris Beard was held responsible only under head-coach-accountability rules rather than facing his own penalty. Flanigan, now at Georgia Tech, received a one-year show-cause order.
