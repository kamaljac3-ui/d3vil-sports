NFL

Jaguars quietly open the door on trading Brian Thomas Jr.

GM James Gladstone has publicly dismissed trade rumors and coach Liam Coen says the receiver "remains a significant part" of the offense, but ESPN and The Athletic report Jacksonville has started fielding offers ahead of the Nov. 10 deadline, with the Steelers, Chiefs and Commanders all showing interest. Thomas' high-ankle sprain this season could limit Jacksonville's return to just a Day 2 pick.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Bills cut CJ Gardner-Johnson a day after his suspension ended

Buffalo released the veteran safety just one day after he finished serving a one-game ban for spitting toward Raiders tight end Brock Bowers. Gardner-Johnson had started all six games he played this season, and the team didn't offer a detailed reason beyond the discipline issue and inconsistent play.

Source: ESPN

Ravens turn to an undrafted rookie at quarterback

With Lamar Jackson headed for thumb surgery and backup Tyler Huntley out with turf toe, Baltimore is set to start undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano on Thursday night against Jacksonville. Kicker Tyler Loop also landed on injured reserve with a groin injury, leaving Jake Moody to handle kicking duties.

Source: Yahoo Sports

CFB

Oklahoma fires offensive coordinator Ben Arbuckle, hands play-calling to Jason Witten

Coach Brent Venables fired second-year OC Ben Arbuckle after a 42-25 home loss to South Carolina dropped the Sooners to 4-4, with tight ends coach — and longtime NFL tight end — Jason Witten taking over play-calling despite never having done so at the college level. Oklahoma's offense ranks last in the SEC in yards per game, and transfer quarterback John Mateer, who followed Arbuckle from Washington State, has thrown seven interceptions in eight games.

Source: ESPN

True freshman gets a second straight start for No. 11 Ohio State

Coach Ryan Day confirmed Luke Fahey — just the fifth true freshman to start at quarterback in program history — will start again Saturday against Oregon, with starter Julian Sayin's elbow/shoulder injury looking season-ending. Fahey went 21-of-38 for 260 yards and a touchdown in a loss at USC in his first career start, and Day said "there's things to build on there for sure."

Source: Yahoo Sports

Maryland and Florida State's coaching searches collide over the same names

Maryland has hired a search firm and now finds itself chasing the same candidates as Florida State, which fired Mike Norvell on Nov. 1 — both programs are circling Wake Forest's Jake Dickert (7-1 this season) and Memphis' Charles Huff (5-2). Maryland's interim coach, Aazaar Abdul-Rahim, lost his debut 20-17 to Illinois on a last-second field goal.

Source: On3

NBA

Draymond Green ejected after a scuffle with Dillon Brooks

Green picked up two technicals and an early exit from Golden State's 116-113 win over Phoenix on Monday after a hard foul on Suns center Khaman Maluach drew Dillon Brooks in; Green grabbed Brooks before officials separated them. It was Green's first ejection of the season, and the league hadn't announced any discipline as of this report.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Ja Morant hurt in his return to Memphis

The Trail Blazers guard — traded away from Memphis this offseason — suffered a grade-two left hamstring strain in a game against his former team and is expected to be re-evaluated in about four weeks. It's a fresh setback for a Portland team still finding its footing with its new backcourt piece.

Source: NBA.com

Rockets ride Alperen Şengün to the top of the West

Houston beat Boston 109-93 behind Alperen Şengün to move into first place in the Western Conference, while Shai Gilgeous-Alexander dropped a season-high 43 points for Oklahoma City and Detroit's Cade Cunningham posted a 33-point, 5-rebound, 7-assist, 5-steal line in a blowout of Charlotte.

Source: NBA.com

NCAAM

East Texas A&M pulls one of the biggest upsets in 30 years

Oklahoma was a 28.5-point home favorite against East Texas A&M on opening night, but the Lions led for more than 31 minutes — by as much as 16 — and walked out 68-64 winners as the Sooners committed 15 turnovers and missed 15 free throws. ESPN Research called it the second-biggest spread upset in Division I in 30 years, and coach Porter Moser told the home crowd afterward, "the only thing I know to do is apologize to the fanbase."

Source: ESPN

Duke's 17-year-old freshman shines in his college debut

No. 2 Duke routed Army 84-50, with leading scorer Dame Sarr putting up 16 points, but the bigger story was 17-year-old freshman Joaquim Boumtje Boumtje — the youngest player in Division I — scoring 15 points with 7 rebounds and 3 blocks while going 6-for-6 from the free-throw line in his debut.

Source: ABC11/AP

WNBA

Liberty fire head coach Chris DeMarco after one season

New York fired DeMarco on Tuesday following a semifinal sweep at the hands of Atlanta; the Liberty finished 26-18 and were criticized for a late-game timeout mistake in that series. It's GM Jonathan Kolb's fourth coaching search since 2019, and no successor has been named yet.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAW

Maryland's Addi Mack nearly ruins South Carolina's season opener

No. 1 South Carolina built a 61-33 halftime lead over No. 20 Maryland in Paris, but Terrapins guard Addi Mack caught fire after the break, scoring 31 of her career-high 35 points in the second half to cut the deficit to single digits before the Gamecocks held on, 101-93. Joyce Edwards (23 points) and Agot Mateer (20) led South Carolina.

Source: Yahoo Sports/AP

USC freshman Saniyah Hall erupts in her college debut

Top-ranked 2026 recruit Saniyah Hall scored 22 points on 7-of-8 shooting in her first game for No. 3 USC, a 100-68 win over UNLV that also featured JuJu Watkins' season return (21 points) and Jazzy Davidson leading all scorers with 23. Five Trojans reached double figures in the win.

Source: Washington Post/AP

MLB

Eight players get a qualifying offer — but the Phillies leave off Alec Bohm

The qualifying-offer window closed Monday with Seiya Suzuki, Randy Arozarena, Jake Bauers and Trevor Rogers among the eight players who received the one-year, $23.15 million offer; they now have until Nov. 17 to accept or decline. Philadelphia's decision to leave third baseman Alec Bohm off its list was the surprise of the batch, given his track record.

Source: NBC Sports Philadelphia

NPB Triple Crown winner Teruaki Sato posted for MLB teams

Hanshin Tigers slugger Teruaki Sato, who led Japan's top league in batting average, home runs and RBIs this year, was formally posted on Nov. 1 and now has 45 days to sign — a window that would be swallowed by a looming lockout if MLB and the union can't make a deal by Dec. 1. The Mets, Yankees, Dodgers and Phillies are reported as his top suitors.

Source: NBC Sports

NHL

Nikita Kucherov explains his trade request, still hasn't backed down

The reigning NHL MVP said his rift with Tampa Bay was "never about the money" but about a lack of communication with management, saying he's had only two real conversations with GM Julien BriseBois in four months. He's given the Lightning a 10-team no-trade list and turned down a six-year, $15 million-per-year extension offered in September, but says he'll keep trying to help his teammates win in the meantime.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Hellebuyck wins again as Devils rally past the Oilers

Connor Hellebuyck made 18 saves for his third straight win in his first home start at Prudential Center since his blockbuster trade from Winnipeg, while Jack Hughes scored twice in a shootout win over Edmonton on Tuesday. Hellebuyck faces his old Jets team next on Nov. 10.

Source: NHL.com

Hertl's late goal, a Nashville two-way night, and Winnipeg's wild comeback

Tomas Hertl's power-play goal with 35 seconds left gave Vegas a 3-2 win over Pittsburgh, Roman Josi and Ross Colton each had a goal and an assist as Nashville beat Carolina 4-1, and Winnipeg erased a 4-0 deficit to beat Montreal 5-4 in overtime on Dylan Samberg's winner after Kyle Connor tied it late in regulation.

Source: NHL.com

UFC

Raul Rosas Jr. submits Colby Covington in 40 seconds

Filling in for an injured Dillon Danis at a grappling event in Rome, UFC bantamweight Raul Rosas Jr. tapped former interim welterweight champ Colby Covington with a rear-naked choke in well under a minute. The embarrassing result lands just 11 days before Covington's actual headline bout against Khamzat Chimaev at RAF Abu Dhabi on Nov. 14.

Source: Bloody Elbow

Michel Pereira resurfaces at the Korean Zombie's promotion

Cut by the UFC in July after a four-of-five-fight skid and a public dispute over officiating in his final loss, Pereira has booked his comeback for Dec. 19 in Incheon, South Korea, against In-soo Hwang at Zombie Fight Night 5 — the promotion owned by former UFC title challenger Chan Sung Jung.

Source: Bloody Elbow

Boxing

Terence Crawford calls out Ryan Garcia for a fight at 160 pounds

Crawford, retired since last December, teased a possible return and challenged Garcia to meet him at middleweight — a 13-pound jump from Garcia's recent welterweight fights. Nothing is signed, and Garcia has separately said flatly he "won't go to 160," floating 154 pounds instead.

Source: MiddleEasy

WBO orders a vacant middleweight title fight between Yoenli Hernandez and Michael Zerafa

After stripping Denzel Bentley of the belt, the WBO authorized negotiations between unbeaten top contender Yoenli Hernandez and Michael Zerafa, who moved up from the No. 4 spot after two higher-ranked contenders were unavailable. The camps have until Nov. 23 to reach a deal before purse bids are called.

Source: Boxing News 24

Golf

Jon Rahm says he'd take Koepka-style penalties to return to the PGA Tour

Speaking at this week's Abu Dhabi Aldar Championship, Rahm said he'd likely accept the same terms Brooks Koepka took to rejoin the PGA Tour — forfeiting years of player equity, a FedEx Cup bonus-pool ban and a charity donation — but he hasn't opened talks and doubts commissioner Brian Rolapp will offer the same deal twice. Absent an agreement, Rahm plans to play the DP World Tour in 2027 while serving a likely one-year PGA Tour suspension.

Source: TSN

LIV Golf's roster for a 2027 relaunch is shrinking fast

With the league in Chapter 11 bankruptcy after its funders pulled support earlier this year, only four players — Richard Bland, Victor Perez, Ben Campbell and Cameron Tringale — had confirmed by Nov. 3 that they'll stick around for a proposed "LIV 2.0." Bryson DeChambeau's status remains unresolved, and LIV golfers face a Nov. 16 deadline to reapply for DP World Tour membership.

Source: Golf Magic

Horse Racing

Point Of Law wins the Melbourne Cup

Point Of Law, bred by King Charles III and trained by Ciaron Maher, won Tuesday's Melbourne Cup at Flemington by 3.75 lengths over Zahrann, making Maher the first trainer to sweep the top three finishers in the race's history. It's the first Cup win by a joint favorite since 2013.

Source: ABC News Australia

Breeders' Cup Juvenile winner Hughes will stand at Spendthrift Farm

Hughes, unbeaten in five starts this year including the Grade 1 American Pharoah Stakes and the Breeders' Cup Juvenile, will retire to stud at Spendthrift Farm once his racing career ends. Connections say more races are still ahead, and he's considered a strong contender for the Eclipse Award as champion 2-year-old male.

Source: Paulick Report

Breeders' Cup Distaff winner Nitrogen targets a 2027 comeback

Trainer Mark Casse said Distaff winner Nitrogen will return as a 5-year-old next year, with a possible tune-up start in the Grade 1 Pegasus World Cup at Gulfstream in January, conditional on a clean recovery. Owner Jon Green's stated long-term goal is getting her back to the Breeders' Cup itself, set for Belmont next year.

Source: Paulick Report
