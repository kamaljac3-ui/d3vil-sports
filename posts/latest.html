NFL

Aaron Rodgers is out in Pittsburgh — and no one claimed him off waivers

The Steelers released Rodgers midweek after he criticized head coach Mike McCarthy on The Pat McAfee Show, and he cleared waivers Thursday with no team putting in a claim. Team president Art Rooney II backed McCarthy and called the comments "insubordination," and Pittsburgh still owes Rodgers roughly $8.6 million left on his one-year deal.

Source: NFL.com

Tyler Bass's 57-yard walk-off lifts the Bills past the Chiefs on Thanksgiving

Buffalo beat Kansas City 13-10 in the rain when Bass drilled a 57-yard field goal as time expired, capping a Chiefs drive that stalled at their own 11 with 15 seconds left. The win pushed the Bills to 10-1 and a seven-game winning streak, keeping them the AFC's No. 1 seed.

Source: NBC Sports

Caleb Williams' last-second touchdown throw to Rome Odunze stuns the Lions

Chicago trailed by 11 in the fourth quarter before Williams drove 68 yards in 13 plays and found Odunze for the winning score with 3 seconds left in a 39-35 shootout. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for three touchdowns for Detroit, but coach Dan Campbell called the loss "self-inflicted," and the win moved the Bears to 9-2 atop the NFC North.

Source: ClickOnDetroit

All three kick off today as part of the holiday weekend's marquee rivalry-game slate.

Eastern Michigan fires Chris Creighton after 13 seasons

The Eagles fired Creighton a day after closing the regular season with a win over Kent State to finish 5-7. He leaves as the program's longest-tenured coach in decades, going 66-90 with six bowl appearances over 13 years.

Source: FOX Sports

Los Angeles (15-3) visits San Antonio (15-4) Friday in the NBA Cup's West Group C finale, with the winner finishing atop the conference's group standings. Victor Wembanyama called it a "must-win" after Portland beat the Spurs 116-111 earlier in the week.

Louisville blew a 17-point lead before a disputed timeout ruling let the Cardinals keep the ball after St. John's tied the game late, setting up Wooley's game-winning three at the horn for a 93-90 win in Thursday's Players Era semifinal. Wooley finished with 19 points in the Las Vegas thriller.

Saint Mary's upsets No. 6 UCLA for the first time since 1958

The Gaels made 15 three-pointers to beat the previously unbeaten Bruins 85-70 at the Rady Children's Invitational in San Diego, handing UCLA its first loss of the season. Coach Mick Cronin didn't mince words afterward: "We got carved up."

Source: ESPN

Michigan's athletic director search gets messier as Warde Manuel agrees to stay on

Michigan signed, then backed away from, a deal to hire former Texas A&M AD Trev Alberts after past comments of his resurfaced. Outgoing AD Warde Manuel, who said in July he'd leave by year's end, has now agreed to stay past Dec. 31 until the school finds a permanent replacement.

The league has already ruled both Freedom and fellow ex-NBA player Royce White ineligible, calling their declarations publicity stunts.

Valkyries' free-agency pitch gets awkward next to Kelsey Mitchell's own words

Golden State forward Gabby Williams said she hopes the champion Valkyries prioritize "the right attitude" over star power in free agency, a comment that lands oddly next to free agent Kelsey Mitchell's own admission that she wants to "be a little selfish" in her next move. NCAAW

Hannah Hidalgo becomes Notre Dame's all-time leading scorer

Hidalgo broke Arike Ogunbowale's program scoring record with a third-minute three-pointer in a 78-64 Thanksgiving win over West Virginia at the Battle 4 Atlantis, finishing with 22 points and nine steals to reach 2,648 career points. She's also riding a 110-game streak of double-figure scoring, the longest active run in the country.

MLB

MLB's lockout lawsuit gets specific, and the odds of a deal look grim

Court filings show MLB's federal suit names eight union executive subcommittee members individually, seeking a ruling that an owner-imposed lockout wouldn't violate antitrust law. A new report finds owners far better financially prepared to weather a stoppage than in 2021, and prediction markets now put the odds of a new deal before the Dec. 1 deadline at roughly 2%.

Source: Bloomberg

A handful of mid-tier deals get done before the likely freeze

With free agency expected to shut down once the current CBA expires, the Guardians re-signed lefty Foster Griffin for three years and $48 million, the Royals added infielder Alec Bohm for two years and $20 million, and the Twins locked up reliever Kevin Ginkel for two years and $18 million guaranteed.

Source: MLB.com

NHL

Panthers get Barkov and Marchand back, and it shows immediately

Captain Aleksander Barkov scored his first goal of the season in his return from a long-term injury, and Brad Marchand added three assists in his own return, as Florida beat Ottawa 5-3. It's the clearest sign yet that the Cup-contending roster is getting whole again heading into December.

Source: Spector's Hockey

Devils' Brenden Dillon suspended four games for a blindside head shot

The NHL's Department of Player Safety banned Dillon for leaving his feet to hit Columbus defenseman Denton Mateychuk in the head, an illegal hit that kept Mateychuk out of the rest of the game. It's Dillon's second head-shot suspension in two years, and he'll be eligible to return when New Jersey visits Pittsburgh in December.

Source: ESPN

UFC

All criminal charges against Anthony Smith are dropped

Nebraska prosecutors dismissed the terroristic-threat and domestic-assault charges filed against the former UFC title challenger after a July bar-altercation arrest. Smith, who retired from the UFC in 2025, said the dismissal doesn't erase the toll the case took on him, writing that the damage "cannot simply be erased by the dismissal of charges."

Source: Bloody Elbow

Ulberg's coach rules out a title fight with his own teammate

City Kickboxing coach Eugene Bareman confirmed that light-heavyweight champion Carlos Ulberg and surging contender Navajo Stirling, fresh off knocking out Jiri Prochazka, will never fight each other for the belt, no matter what the UFC wants. With Ulberg still recovering from a knee injury, that stance could push the promotion to let Stirling chase an interim title against Magomed Ankalaev instead.

A week after Price swept Michaela Kotaskova to become Wales' first-ever undisputed world boxing champion, promoter Boxxer is already pushing for a bigger stage — floating a Price-Shields unification bout for next May or June. Price is expected to fight again as soon as February.
