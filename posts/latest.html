NCAAW

Oregon State women's basketball players make a historic push to unionize

A hearing before Oregon's state employment relations board opened Monday in Salem to decide whether Oregon State women's basketball players can be recognized as university employees eligible to unionize — the first formal attempt of its kind by women's college athletes. The petition, filed through the newly formed United College Athletes Association, is being contested by the university's attorneys, who argue the players aren't employees; a ruling could still be appealed in court.
