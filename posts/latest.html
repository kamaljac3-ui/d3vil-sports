NFL

Eagles tear up their offensive line: Jurgens dealt to Baltimore, Dalton cut

Philadelphia traded two-time Pro Bowl center Cam Jurgens to the Ravens for a 2027 fifth-round pick and a 2028 second-rounder, and released backup quarterback Andy Dalton, days after longtime right tackle Lane Johnson abruptly retired. Chicago's leading pass rusher was suspended without pay under the NFL's performance-enhancing substances policy, sidelining him for Sunday at Atlanta and a Thursday-night home game against New England. The Bears activated lineman Shemar Turner to fill the spot; Sweat is the first Bears player suspended since GM Ryan Poles took over in 2022.
