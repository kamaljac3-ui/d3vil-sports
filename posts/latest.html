NFL

Cowboys blow past free-falling Eagles on Monday night

Dallas handled three touchdowns from Dak Prescott in a 38-21 win over Philadelphia, pushing the Cowboys to 3-4 while dropping the Eagles to 2-5 and deepening the defending-champion-adjacent franchise's collapse. The loss leaves Philadelphia searching for answers on both sides of the ball with little margin left in the NFC race.

Source: Philadelphia Eagles

Colts fired GM Chris Ballard mid-season

Indianapolis dismissed longtime general manager Chris Ballard last week after a 2-4 start, with Ed Dodds stepping in as interim GM. Owner Carlie Irsay-Gordon said she chose not to wait for the offseason to make the change: "Waiting would only delay the important work that needs to begin now."

Source: CBS Sports

Two more Bills defenders suspended for spitting at Raiders

The NFL handed one-game suspensions to Bills safety C.J. Gardner-Johnson and defensive back Sam Franklin Jr. for spitting incidents during Buffalo's game against Las Vegas, with both bans running this week. Franklin is disputing the finding and plans to appeal.

Source: The Washington Times

CFB

Wisconsin's defense stuns No. 16 USC for a program-first win

Wisconsin beat USC 24-7 at home, intercepting Jayden Maiava twice and sacking him five times in what the Badgers say is their first-ever regular-season win over the Trojans. It was the worst offensive showing of Lincoln Riley's head-coaching career, a bad sign for a USC team that had been positioning itself for a Big Ten title push.

Source: ESPN

Maryland fires Mike Locksley amid a disappointing season

Maryland dismissed head coach Mike Locksley during the team's bye week, four days after a homecoming loss to Rutgers, with assistant Aazaar Abdul-Rahim taking over as interim coach. It's the latest in a wave of major-conference coaching changes this month, following firings at NC State and Baylor.

Source: Sports Illustrated

Texas loses All-SEC tackle Trevor Goosby for the season

Longhorns left tackle Trevor Goosby was carted off with a serious knee injury in the first quarter of Saturday's win over Ole Miss and will miss the rest of 2026, coach Steve Sarkisian confirmed, calling the injury something that "didn't look very good." Senior Andre Cojoe now takes over protecting Arch Manning's blind side for the stretch run.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Indiana wins at Michigan for the first time since 1967

Indiana beat Michigan 13-6 in overtime in Ann Arbor, the Hoosiers' first win there in nearly six decades, part of a chaotic weekend that also saw BYU fall to UCF in double overtime and Texas Tech lose to Cincinnati.

Source: NCAA.com

NBA

Jalen Williams' injury confirmed as a fractured fibula and dislocated ankle

Thunder GM Sam Presti said Williams, hurt in Sunday's win over the Clippers, has a fractured left fibula and dislocated ankle and will need surgery, with teammate Cason Wallace also requiring shoulder surgery from the same game. Presti wouldn't rule out a season-ending outcome pending what surgeons find — a serious setback for the defending conference finalists.

Source: ESPN

Mavericks rookie Cooper Flagg sidelined with a shoulder sprain

Flagg is dealing with a mild left shoulder sprain after absorbing a flagrant foul from Aaron Nesmith last week, sitting out one game already and entering Monday's trip to Miami as questionable. Outlets differ on whether he's out closer to a week or just a couple of games.

Source: Rotowire

Bucks avoid a historic 0-4 start behind Tyler Herro

Milwaukee beat Indiana 110-102 for its first win of the season, avoiding its first 0-4 start since 1976-77, as Herro posted 23 points, 10 rebounds and 7 assists. Tyrese Haliburton (22 points) and Pascal Siakam (20 points, 13 rebounds) led the Pacers, who were playing the second night of a back-to-back without center Ivica Zubac.

Source: TSN/AP

NCAAM

Florida opens at No. 1 in the preseason AP poll for the first time since 2006

The AP's preseason Top 25 put defending-contender Florida at No. 1, with Todd Golden's team collecting the large majority of first-place votes ahead of Duke and Illinois. It's the Gators' first preseason No. 1 ranking in two decades, built around four returning starters from last season's 27-win team; reigning champion Michigan opened at No. 16.

Source: ESPN

LSU coaching legend Dale Brown dies at 90

Dale Brown, LSU's all-time winningest men's basketball coach with 448 wins and two Final Fours across 25 seasons, died just over a week shy of his 91st birthday. He coached Shaquille O'Neal and Mahmoud Abdul-Rauf and had just been named the incoming recipient of a national lifetime-achievement coaching award days before his death.

Source: LSU Athletics

WNBA

Valkyries beat the Dream in Game 5, push the WNBA Finals to a decisive Game 6

Golden State won 64-55 at home to take a 3-2 series lead, holding Atlanta to under 30% shooting and forcing 15 turnovers behind Kiah Stokes (13 points, 8 rebounds, 3 blocks) and Veronica Burton (13 points); Angel Reese was limited to 7 points on 2-of-8 shooting. Game 6 shifts to Atlanta on Thursday, where the Valkyries can clinch the franchise's first championship.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Fever fire GM Amber Cox; team president also steps down

Indiana dismissed general manager and COO Amber Cox the same day club president Kelly Krauskopf announced she was stepping down, a front-office shakeup that follows the Fever's first-round playoff exit. Head coach Stephanie White remains in place while the team searches for a new GM.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Report: Dodgers and Lakers owner Mark Walter looking to sell the Sparks for a record $700 million

Bloomberg reported that Walter is exploring a sale of the Sparks and their practice facility for roughly $700 million, which would more than double the WNBA's current sale record of $300 million. No buyer or deal has been confirmed, and the move would follow Walter's recent record-setting sale of the Lakers.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAW

South Carolina opens at No. 1 in the AP preseason poll, UConn No. 2, USC No. 3

Dawn Staley's South Carolina took the preseason No. 1 spot for the fifth time in seven years, with UConn at No. 2 for a 33rd straight year in the top 10 and JuJu Watkins' USC at No. 3. Duke landed at No. 4, its highest preseason ranking since 2013, while the Big Ten placed a record nine teams in the poll.

Source: ESPN

Season opens in Rome: No. 13 Notre Dame faces Villanova on Nov. 1

Notre Dame and Villanova will open the 2026-27 season at a Rome arena in an event inspired by Villanova alum Pope Leo XIV, the first meeting between the schools since 2018. It follows a men's doubleheader opener at the same venue and includes a planned papal audience beforehand.

Source: Fox Sports

MLB

Brewers take a 3-0 World Series lead with a record-setting Game 3 blowout

Milwaukee routed Tampa Bay 10-1 at Tropicana Field, breaking the game open with an eight-run fifth inning built on seven consecutive two-out hits — a World Series record. The Brewers are now one win from their first championship, a feat no team has ever let slip from a 3-0 deficit.

Source: ESPN

Rays face elimination in Game 4 with their rotation in flux

Tampa Bay must beat Milwaukee on Tuesday night at home to stay alive, turning to closer Griffin Jax as its starter. The Brewers hadn't named a starter as of the latest reports after planned starter Brandon Woodruff instead closed out Game 3 in relief, leaving Shane Drohan, Dustin May, Kyle Harrison or a bullpen game as options; Milwaukee is favored.

Source: MLB.com

NHL

Matvei Gridin's two goals lift Flames past Matthews-led Maple Leafs

Calgary beat Toronto 5-2 as Gridin scored twice and added an assist, with Joel Farabee and Martin Pospisil each adding a goal and an assist and goalie Devin Cooley winning his fourth straight start. Auston Matthews reached 800 career points in the loss, becoming the fastest player in Maple Leafs history to the mark.

Source: NHL.com

Quinn Hughes booed in return to Vancouver despite hitting 500 career points

Now with the Minnesota Wild, Hughes reached his 500th and 501st career points as Minnesota beat his former team 4-2, getting there in 520 games — the seventh-fastest defenseman in NHL history. He drew heavy boos from the Rogers Arena crowd despite a video tribute, while Vancouver's losing streak reached 10 games.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Ryan O'Reilly's four-point night powers Predators past Avalanche

Nashville routed Colorado 6-2 as O'Reilly posted two goals and two assists and Steven Stamkos added two goals and an assist, with O'Reilly scoring twice on the power play as the Predators went 4-for-7 with the man advantage. The loss was Colorado's third in four games.

Source: NHL.com

UFC

Volkanovski sets UFC record with a ninth featherweight title defense

Alexander Volkanovski stopped previously-unbeaten Movsar Evloev with ground strikes in the second round at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi, breaking his tie with José Aldo for the most featherweight title defenses ever and becoming, at 38, the oldest fighter to defend the belt.

Source: ESPN

Petr Yan edges Merab Dvalishvili to win their bantamweight trilogy

Yan retained his title by unanimous decision on the same card, rallying after losing the first two rounds to take the final three and go 2-1 in a rivalry that's spanned three years. It was the co-main event of UFC 333.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Dana White walks back comments about released fighter Farid Basharat

At the UFC 333 post-fight press conference, White clarified remarks suggesting unsigned fighters are "hard to deal with," saying he wasn't targeting Basharat specifically after seeing his reaction on social media: "I didn't mean to do that to that kid." White predicted Basharat will land with another promotion.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Boxing

O'Shaquie Foster unifies three 130-pound titles over Navarrete

Foster dropped Emanuel Navarrete twice in the 12th round en route to a lopsided unanimous decision, adding Navarrete's WBO and IBF belts to his own WBC and vacant Ring Magazine titles. "I want to become undisputed," Foster said after the fight. "That's my dream."

Source: ESPN

Devin Haney's new DAZN deal reportedly sets up a Dec. 19 unification bout

DAZN announced an exclusive multi-fight partnership with Devin Haney Promotions, and multiple outlets now report his first fight under the deal will be a WBO/IBF welterweight unification against Liam Paro in San Francisco on Dec. 19 — though the date and opponent weren't officially locked as of the latest reporting.

Source: The Ring Magazine

Dalton Smith's disputed split decision over Puello roils the 140-pound division

Smith retained his WBC title with a split decision many ringside observers and media scored for Alberto Puello instead, including rival Adam Azim, who said "Puello won by one round, in my opinion." The contested verdict now complicates promoter Eddie Hearn's push for a Smith-Shakur Stevenson unification fight.

Source: BoxingNews24

Golf

Austin Smotherman wins Bermuda Championship playoff for his second straight title

Smotherman beat Seamus Power on the third sudden-death hole at Port Royal Golf Course in the Monday playoff forced by Sunday's weather washout, winning his second consecutive PGA Tour event. The result locks in starts at Pebble Beach and Riviera next year, while Power's runner-up finish secures his Tour card through 2028.

Source: Golf Channel

Yuto Katsuragawa snaps a two-year drought to win the Genesis Championship

Katsuragawa closed with a 13-under total to beat Frederic Lacroix and Martin Couvra by two shots at the DP World Tour event in South Korea, his first win since April 2024. The result lifted him from 106th to 28th in the Race to Dubai standings, securing his tour card and a spot in the season-ending Rolex Series events.

Source: Sky Sports

Monday's draw at Keeneland locked the Classic field at eight, its smallest since 2022, led by defending champion Forever Young as the 2-1 favorite. With the Oct. 30-31 championships four days out, Phileas Fogg was scratched from Classic consideration with a sore foot, and Aidan O'Brien's European turf contenders — including Diamond Necklace, now 2-1 for the Turf — drew unfavorable outside posts at Monday's ceremony.

Source: Racing Post

Breeders' Cup's own release confirms a record 255 total pre-entries across the 14-race, two-day World Championships at Keeneland, including twelve past Breeders' Cup champions among the contenders — an unusually deep field across the board.
