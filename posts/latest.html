NFL

Jalen Hurts' season ends with blood clots in both lungs; NFLPA wants answers

The Eagles say quarterback Jalen Hurts is done for the year after doctors found deep-vein thrombosis and blood clots in both lungs, a diagnosis the team says carries a good long-term outlook but at least three months of treatment. The NFLPA is now pushing for an investigation into why Hurts was cleared to play in London on Oct. 11 after a clot had already been found in his leg.

MLB

Brewers win the first World Series title in franchise history

Milwaukee closed out Tampa Bay 4-1 in Game 5 at Tropicana Field, capping an 11-2 postseason run with the 57-year-old franchise's first championship. Jacob Misiorowski worked 5⅔ scoreless innings before leaving with a calf cramp and rookie Cooper Pratt drove in two runs in the clincher, while catcher William Contreras was named World Series MVP for steadying Milwaukee's pitching staff all series.

Source: MLB.com
