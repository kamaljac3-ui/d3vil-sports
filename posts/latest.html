NFL

NFL plays its first-ever regular-season game on French soil

The Steelers and Saints kicked off the league's first regular-season game in France this morning at Stade de France, a milestone in the NFL's international push. Pittsburgh (4-2) came in favored over New Orleans (2-4), with Aaron Rodgers and Tyler Shough starting at quarterback.

Source: NFL.com

Jaguars lose LT Anton Harrison for the season to a torn ACL

Jacksonville left tackle Anton Harrison will miss the rest of 2026 after surgery this week confirmed a torn ACL, an injury suffered blocking during the team's Oct. 11 win over the Eagles in London. He'd already gone on injured reserve before the full diagnosis came back, and the Jaguars still owe him a guaranteed $19 million option for 2027.

Source: NBC Sports

Giants and Vikings circle Colts running back Jonathan Taylor

With the Nov. 10 trade deadline two weeks away, New York and Minnesota have emerged as the most serious suitors for Indianapolis running back Jonathan Taylor, according to league reporting, as the Colts keep fielding calls on veteran talent. Taylor's name joins teammates Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner on the list of Colts veterans drawing outside interest since GM Chris Ballard's firing.

Source: Bleacher Report

CFB

No. 1 Miami routs Pitt as Malachi Toney sets a receiving record

Miami beat Pittsburgh 52-23 in its first game as the nation's top-ranked team in nearly 24 years, with receiver Malachi Toney setting a school record of 16 catches for 194 yards and three touchdowns. Quarterback Darian Mensah was nearly flawless, completing 31 of 37 passes for 387 yards and four touchdowns without a turnover as the Hurricanes improved to 7-0.

Source: Miami Hurricanes Athletics

Indiana ends a 59-year losing streak at Michigan Stadium

No. 6 Indiana beat Michigan 13-6 in overtime at the Big House, the Hoosiers' first win there since 1967. Michigan's offense never found the end zone and missed two field goals before Josh Hoover's 4-yard overtime touchdown sealed it, following a game-tying field goal as regulation expired.

Source: Outkick

Sam Leavitt's four touchdowns lead LSU past Auburn

LSU beat Auburn 38-17 at Jordan-Hare Stadium behind quarterback Sam Leavitt, who passed and ran for four total touchdowns, including two scoring throws to Jackson Harris. The win moved LSU to 6-2 overall while Auburn fell to 4-3, adding more heat to a program already under scrutiny.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Magic's Paolo Banchero out 4-6 weeks with ankle sprain

Orlando star Paolo Banchero will miss four to six weeks with a low-grade right high ankle sprain suffered Friday night in a loss to Minnesota, hurt crashing the glass and colliding with teammate Jalen Suggs. Tristan da Silva is set to start in his place while the Magic adjust their rotation.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Sixers go 3-0 on Embiid's triple-double as LeBron sits out

Philadelphia beat Milwaukee 127-116 without LeBron James, held out to manage lower-back and sciatica issues in his first missed game since joining the Sixers. Joel Embiid filled the void with a triple-double, and Jaylen Brown added 27 points on his 30th birthday as coach Nick Nurse downplayed any long-term concern about James.

Source: Bleacher Report

Hawks blow out Hornets for the biggest win in franchise history

Atlanta crushed Charlotte 128-81, setting a new franchise record for largest margin of victory, with Jalen Johnson and reserve Aaron Wiggins each scoring 19 points. It's an early statement from a Hawks team that's opened its season with two lopsided wins over Southeast Division rivals.

Source: AP via West Hawaii Today

NCAAM

No. 12 Arkansas outlasts No. 15 Gonzaga in exhibition thriller

Arkansas beat Gonzaga 76-72 in Fayetteville behind an 18-0 run spanning halftime, surviving a late push from Gonzaga's Braden Huff (19 points, 11 rebounds) before Billy Richmond III sealed it with a free throw with 0.4 seconds left. It's a meaningful early measuring-stick result between two preseason top-15 teams.

Source: Gonzaga Athletics

Florida debuts at No. 1 in the preseason AP poll

Florida received 47 of 59 first-place votes to open the season atop the AP Top 25 for the first time in decades, with Duke second and Illinois third. Defending champion Michigan slid to No. 16 after coach Dusty May left over the summer to take the Dallas Mavericks job.

Source: CBS Sports

WNBA

Valkyries even the WNBA Finals, forcing a Game 5

Golden State staved off elimination with a defensive clinic in Atlanta, holding the Dream to 33.3% shooting and forcing 18 turnovers in a 67-62 Game 4 win led by Gabby Williams' 17 points. The series shifts back to the Bay Area for a win-or-go-home Game 5 Monday night, with both franchises still chasing a first championship.

Source: ESPN

NCAAW

South Carolina opens at No. 1 in the preseason poll again

Dawn Staley's Gamecocks took 23 of 31 first-place votes in the preseason AP Top 25, ahead of UConn and USC, for South Carolina's fifth preseason No. 1 ranking in seven years. The Gamecocks open their season Nov. 2 against Maryland in France.

Source: NCAA.com

MLB

Brewers take a 2-0 World Series lead with a second straight comeback

Milwaukee erased a 4-0 first-inning hole to beat Tampa Bay 5-4 in Game 2, with Sal Frelick's sixth-inning homer tying it and rookie Cooper Pratt's go-ahead single in the seventh putting the Brewers in front for good. Closer Aaron Ashby stranded the tying run at third in the ninth to seal Milwaukee's second comeback from an early four-run deficit in three games.

Source: ESPN

Rays turn to former Brewer Freddy Peralta to avoid a 3-0 hole

With the series shifting to Tampa Bay for Monday's Game 3, the Rays will start Freddy Peralta — who spent eight seasons in Milwaukee before signing with Tampa Bay — against Brewers lefty Robert Gasser. Peralta has been dominant this postseason at 2-0 with a 1.08 ERA, giving the Rays a real chance to avoid a deficit no team has ever come back from.

Source: MLB.com

NHL

Nylander becomes the sixth Maple Leaf to reach 300 career goals

William Nylander scored twice, including an empty-netter, to reach 300 career goals as Toronto routed Edmonton 7-2. Leon Draisaitl answered with two goals of his own to push his league-leading total to 12, while Connor McDavid extended his season-opening points streak to 11 straight games.

Source: NBC Sports

Batherson's hat trick lifts Senators past the Rangers

Drake Batherson scored three goals and added an assist as Ottawa beat New York 7-4, part of a busy Saturday slate that also saw Pittsburgh beat Nashville in overtime and Washington edge Buffalo in OT.

Source: ESPN

UFC

Volkanovski and Yan both survive at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi

Alexander Volkanovski retained his featherweight title by stopping previously-unbeaten Movsar Evloev with a second-round TKO, handing Evloev the first loss of his career and giving Volkanovski the most featherweight title defenses in UFC history. In the co-main, bantamweight champion Petr Yan won a unanimous decision over Merab Dvalishvili to take their trilogy after Dvalishvili had won their previous meeting.

Source: ESPN

UFC 334 is losing fighters by the week ahead of its Madison Square Garden date

With the Nov. 14 card at Madison Square Garden approaching, a lightweight bout was scrapped entirely after both the original fighter and his replacement withdrew with injuries, and bantamweight contender Chris Gutierrez lost his opponent to a late pullout with no replacement yet named. It's an unraveling card with just under three weeks left to sort itself out.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Boxing

O'Shaquie Foster unifies the super featherweight title, dethroning Navarrete

Foster dropped Emanuel Navarrete twice en route to a lopsided unanimous decision, adding the WBO and WBC belts to the IBF title he already held. He said afterward he's eyeing a fight with Charly Suarez or the winner of Nick Ball-Anthony Cacace next.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Canelo's Riyadh title shot stays on despite regional unrest

Canelo Alvarez's Oct. 31 challenge of Christian Mbilli for the WBC super-middleweight title remains scheduled in Riyadh, even as commentators pushed for a cancellation after strikes near the city's airport. Mbilli's promoter said this week the challenger will still travel to Saudi Arabia as planned.

Source: Sky Sports

Golf

Yuto Katsuragawa wins the Genesis Championship, revives Race to Dubai bid

Japan's Yuto Katsuragawa won the DP World Tour's Genesis Championship in South Korea by two shots at 13-under for his second career Tour title. He'd arrived ranked 106th, outside the guaranteed top-100 cutoff for next year's card, and the win jumps him to 28th.

Source: Sky Sports

Yuri Yoshida wins her first LPGA title at the BMW Ladies Championship

Yoshida closed with a one-shot win at 17-under over Haeran Ryu and Choi Hye-jin for her maiden LPGA Tour title, a breakthrough result in a week when most tour attention is on the season-ending CME Race.

Source: LPGA Tour (via Tribune)

Horse Racing

Forever Young overtakes Sovereignty as the Breeders' Cup Classic favorite

Futures markets now install 2025 Classic winner Forever Young as the betting favorite over reigning Horse of the Year Sovereignty, who missed the Jockey Club Gold Cup with a foot issue. Trainer Bill Mott says he's confident Sovereignty will still make the Oct. 31 Classic field at Keeneland, resolving the entry uncertainty that had hung over the horse all week.

Source: Horse Racing Nation

Willy D's wins the Hagyard Fayette at Keeneland

The 8-1 longshot Willy D's, ridden by John Velazquez for trainer Mike Maker, dueled on the front end and held off Stars and Stripes by a neck in 1:49.56. It backs up his Aug. 28 Charles Town Classic win with another graded stakes score heading into Breeders' Cup week.

Source: BloodHorse
