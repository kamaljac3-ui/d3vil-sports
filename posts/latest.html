MLB

Mets land Tarik Skubal with a nine-year, $450 million deal — the richest contract ever for a pitcher

The two-time AL Cy Young winner has agreed to terms with New York on a nine-year, $450 million contract, the largest deal for a pitcher in MLB history by both total and annual value. Skubal reportedly also met with the Dodgers, Padres and Blue Jays before choosing the Mets, closing out his free agency well ahead of the Dec. 1 CBA deadline that's loomed over this entire winter's market.
