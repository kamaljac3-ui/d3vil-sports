NFL

Jaguars reload their secondary again, agreeing to a deal for safety Jessie Bates III

Jacksonville and Atlanta agreed to send Jessie Bates III to the Jaguars ahead of Tuesday's trade deadline, with the Falcons picking up most of his remaining salary to help the move along. It's the second trade between the two franchises this year and continues a run of defensive additions for a Jaguars team looking to upgrade its back end down the stretch.

Source: NBC Sports

Rams lose Puka Nacua to IR, leaving Sean McVay without a healthy top receiver

Sean McVay confirmed that Puka Nacua sprained his MCL and PCL and suffered turf toe on the final offensive snap of Sunday's win over Washington, and the Rams placed him on injured reserve Monday for a minimum four-week absence. He joins Davante Adams, already out with a hamstring injury, leaving Los Angeles's passing game to lean on its depth at wideout for at least a month. McVay said he'd "be lying" if he weren't questioning the decision to call that final play.

Source: therams.com
