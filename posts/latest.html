NFL

Commanders lose Terry McLaurin for the season to a torn patellar tendon

Washington's leading receiver tore his patellar tendon in Monday's loss to the 49ers, head coach Dan Quinn confirmed Tuesday, ending his 2026 season as the Commanders try to salvage their campaign. McLaurin, a four-time 1,000-yard receiver, leaves Washington thin at the position with their playoff hopes already shaky.

Giants release Najee Harris two days after he vented about his role

New York cut the veteran running back Tuesday, just two days after a four-carry, two-yard showing in Sunday's loss to the Saints during which Harris publicly criticized the team's running-back-by-committee approach. Coach John Harbaugh said nobody has "taken the bull by the horns" at the position, leaving the Giants with an unsettled backfield heading into Week 7 against the Texans.

CFB

Ohio State's Julian Sayin is done for the season with a shoulder injury

Coach Ryan Day said Tuesday the Buckeyes are planning to be without their starting quarterback for the rest of the regular season and any postseason games after he injured his throwing shoulder getting sacked in last Saturday's win at Indiana. True freshman Luke Fahey will continue starting in Sayin's place as Ohio State tries to protect its playoff positioning.

Source: ESPN

Texas gives backup KJ Lacey more first-team reps as Arch Manning's slump continues

Coach Steve Sarkisian says Manning will still start Saturday against No. 7 Ole Miss, but confirmed the redshirt freshman backup is getting increased first-team practice reps this week after the Longhorns' second straight loss. "I need to see a better version of Arch Manning," Sarkisian said, with Manning's six-game line sitting at 1,295 yards, 11 touchdowns and 5 interceptions.

MLB

Brewers face a risky roster call on injured ace Brandon Woodruff before the World Series

Woodruff threw 23 pitches of live batting practice Tuesday — his first game action in nearly four months since a shoulder injury — and said "it's gonna be hard to keep me off that roster." Early Voting had already faced breeding setbacks after a fertility issue led Coolmore's Ashford Stud to drop him as a stallion prospect.
