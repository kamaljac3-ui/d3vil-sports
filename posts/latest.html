NFL

Hurts' blood clot flares back up, knocking him out for Carolina

A follow-up ultrasound on Wednesday showed new movement in the superficial blood clot in Jalen Hurts' calf — the same one doctors had cleared him to play through in London the week before — and the Eagles ruled him out for Sunday's game against the Panthers. Tanner McKee steps in at quarterback while the team waits to see whether the setback costs Hurts more than one week.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Miami jury finds Tyreek Hill partly liable in his ex-fiancée's abuse lawsuit

A civil jury in Miami found the free-agent receiver liable on two of ten battery counts brought by Lakeeta Vaccaro, awarding her $500,000 — far below the $8 million she sought — and declining to add punitive damages. Hill's agent says no team is actively pursuing him yet while he rehabs a knee injury, with a possible return eyed for November.

Source: NBC News

Broncos survive a Seahawks rally, win it in overtime on Thursday night

Seattle erased an 11-point fourth-quarter deficit to force overtime, but Denver's Ja'Quan McMillian intercepted Sam Darnold to set up Wil Lutz's 53-yard winner in a 17-14 Broncos victory. Denver's defense forced three takeaways and five sacks to bail out an offense that managed just 30 yards in the second half, moving to 4-2 while Seattle fell to 3-3.

Source: NFL.com

CFB

ESPN and the College Football Playoff edge toward a 24-team expansion

Multiple reports say the CFP and ESPN are closing in on a deal to grow the field to 24 teams starting in 2027, with ESPN paying for the added media rights through 2031. The expected format mixes conference champions with a larger pool of at-large bids, top-8 byes and campus-site play-in games, though neither side has formally signed off and a Dec. 1 target for approval still looms.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Elko walks back a postgame shot at Texas A&M's own fanbase

After the Aggies' 27-6 loss to No. 14 Missouri dropped them to 3-3, coach Mike Elko criticized a vocal slice of A&M's fans and called out a team co-owner by name — then said days later he regretted how it came across. The flap has become a genuine hot-seat flashpoint for a coach who led A&M to the Playoff a year ago.

Source: KBTX

Vanderbilt cuts a receiver after a violent arrest in downtown Nashville

The Commodores dismissed sophomore WR Kayleb Barnett after he was arrested on charges including aggravated assault by strangulation stemming from a weekend altercation near the Shelby Street pedestrian bridge, where police named him the "predominant aggressor." He posted bond and is due back in court Nov. 13; coach Clark Lea confirmed the move is final.

Source: On3

NBA

Duren debuts looking every bit like a $200 million man

Jalen Duren, who sat out Detroit's first two preseason games during contract talks before signing a five-year, $200 million extension, scored 24 points on 8-of-9 shooting in just 15 minutes in his preseason debut, a 128-106 rout of Minnesota. He has one more tune-up before the Pistons open the season Oct. 20 against Boston.

Source: Detroit Sports Nation

Knicks hit pause on extension talks with Hart and three others

New York told Josh Hart, Karl-Anthony Towns, Miles McBride and Tyler Kolek that it's tabling contract-extension talks until later in the season so camp can stay the focus, leaving Hart's reported four-year, $131 million candidacy unresolved for now. Hart said he's "not really too stressed" about it, and Jalen Brunson vouched that the locker room will stay professional either way.

Source: CBS Sports

Lakers and Bucks reportedly discussed a Knecht-for-Sims swap before the roster deadline

With teams needing to trim to 15 standard contracts before next week's opener, Los Angeles and Milwaukee held early talks on sending Dalton Knecht and a second-round pick to the Bucks for center Jericho Sims. Sources are reportedly skeptical it gets done, and the Lakers may simply waive Knecht if no trade materializes.

Source: Hoops Rumors

NCAAM

Louisville's Pat Kelsey testifies in the NCAA's fifth-year eligibility fight

In a case separate from the LSU-SEC dispute already making news, Kelsey took the stand in Jefferson Circuit Court urging the NCAA to resolve the chaos around transfers like Louisville's Seth Trimble and LJ Wells, who are fighting to keep a fifth season of eligibility after an appeals court voided the injunction that had cleared them to play. No ruling has come down yet, with the season under three weeks away and several schools' rosters in limbo.

Source: WDRB

Pitino's retooled St. John's roster looks rough around the edges in its exhibition opener

New point guard Quinn Ellis, cleared by the NCAA just a day earlier, helped St. John's open its preseason with an 81-52 win over Drexel, but coach Rick Pitino was blunt that his team is "so far behind on the fundamentals" after committing 19 turnovers. Babacar Sane led the Red Storm with 17 points and 8 rebounds.

Source: St. John's Athletics

Xavier knocks off a ranked Tennessee team on its home floor

Xavier trailed by 10 early in the second half before rallying behind a late shooting surge to beat Tennessee 86-70 in Knoxville, one of the more competitive exhibition results of the young preseason against a team that opened in the national rankings.

Source: Tennessee Athletics

WNBA

Valkyries open as favorites over the Dream in Saturday's Finals opener

Golden State is favored by roughly 2.5 points at home for Game 1 of the WNBA Finals, backed by a 3-0 regular-season sweep of Atlanta and a 4-0 playoff run, while the Dream arrive having gone 5-0 of their own in the postseason. Tip-off is set for Saturday at Chase Center, with Atlanta's Allisha Gray's hand injury still the key swing factor.

Source: SI Betting

Reese sweeps Defensive Player of the Year as the WNBA wraps up its 2026 awards

Atlanta's Angel Reese won Defensive Player of the Year in her first season with the Dream, while Olivia Miles swept Rookie of the Year unanimously after breaking Caitlin Clark's rookie scoring record. Dallas's Jessica Shepard won Most Improved Player and Golden State's Janelle Salaün took Sixth Player of the Year, rounding out a trophy case topped by A'ja Wilson's already-announced third straight MVP.

Source: Yahoo Sports

It's an early, lopsided look at a UConn team expected to contend for a title.

MLB

Brewers sweep the Dodgers out of the NLCS and reach their first World Series since 1982

Milwaukee rallied from an early 4-0 hole to beat Los Angeles 6-4 on Thursday, capped by Garrett Mitchell's go-ahead homer off Tanner Scott in the sixth, to complete a stunning four-game sweep of the defending-champion Dodgers. First baseman Jake Bauers became the franchise's first-ever LCS MVP, hitting .286 with a homer and six RBIs across the series after a career that nearly ended in 2023.

Guardians take the ALCS lead into a weather-threatened Game 4

The Rays counter with Nick Martinez on the mound as they try to even the series back up.

Source: MLB.com
