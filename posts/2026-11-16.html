NFL

Aidan Hutchinson's knee injury on a sack celebration may end his season again

Detroit routed New England 35-10 in Munich, but the Lions may pay for it: Aidan Hutchinson's right knee gave out as he celebrated a first-quarter sack of Drake Maye, and multiple reporters say the team is bracing for a season-ending diagnosis pending further testing. It would be the second time in three years a leg injury has ended Hutchinson's season, after the tibia/fibula fracture that cost him most of 2024; Patriots quarterback Drake Maye was also hurt in the same game with an ankle injury.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times

Josh Allen runs wild over a Geno Smith-less Jets as Buffalo moves to 8-1

Josh Allen ran for two touchdowns and threw another to DJ Moore as Buffalo beat New York 31-12 Sunday, pulling away after the Jets were downgraded without quarterback Geno Smith (ankle). The win keeps the Bills alone atop the AFC East heading into Week 12.

Source: CBS Sports

CFB

Florida ends Kentucky's playoff hopes while saving its own bowl case

Florida beat No. 13 Kentucky 21-14 at Kroger Field, with quarterback Aaron Philo accounting for 221 total yards including the go-ahead score on a 2-yard run with just over nine minutes left. The win pushes Florida to 6-4 and bowl-eligible while Kentucky drops to 7-3 with its playoff case now effectively over after back-to-back losses.

Source: ESPN

Georgia stays unbeaten at No. 1 as Notre Dame surges into the CFP top five

Georgia blew past Missouri 48-21 to remain the committee's top-ranked team at 9-1, while Notre Dame's 41-7 rout of Boston College has the Irish trending into the next playoff rankings' top five. It's part of a Week 11 slate that reshuffled the playoff picture alongside Saturday's Texas-LSU and Indiana-USC results.

Source: NCAA.com

NBA

LeBron James passes Jason Kidd for third on the NBA's all-time assists list

LeBron James moved into third place in NBA history with 11 assists in Philadelphia's 131-124 win over Indiana on Sunday, pushing his career total to 12,092 and passing Jason Kidd. Only John Stockton and Chris Paul now sit ahead of him on the list.

Source: NBA.com

MLB

Brewers buy low on former Giants closer Ryan Walker

Milwaukee acquired right-hander Ryan Walker from San Francisco for two minor-league pitchers, betting a change of scenery can get him back to his 2024 form (1.91 ERA, 10 saves) after a rough 2026 that saw a 7.16 ERA and a May demotion to the minors. The move has fueled speculation the Brewers could also shop closer Trevor Megill this winter.

Source: MLB.com

Eight players get qualifying offers as Monday's deadline arrives

Teams had until Monday's 5 p.m. ET deadline to extend $23.15 million one-year qualifying offers, and eight did: Randy Arozarena, Brandon Lowe, Seiya Suzuki, Jake Bauers, David Bednar, Ryan Jeffers, Adrián Morejón and Trevor Rogers. Ian Happ, Alec Bohm and Jazz Chisholm Jr. were notable snubs, and with MLB and the union reportedly open to scrapping the system in the next labor deal, this could be among the last qualifying-offer cycles ever.

Source: MLB.com

NHL

Connor McDavid ties Gretzky's pace to 40 assists as his point streak hits 22 games

McDavid set up a Leon Draisaitl power-play goal in Edmonton's 5-2 win over Philadelphia on Sunday for his 40th assist of the season, tying Wayne Gretzky's 1984-85 mark for fastest to 40 helpers — both in their 22nd game. It also stretched McDavid's season-opening point streak to a career-best 22 games; he leads the NHL with 53 points through those 22 games.

Source: NHL.com

UFC

Stephen "Wonderboy" Thompson retires on the exact move he debuted with

Thompson closed his 16-year UFC career at UFC 334 with a third-round spinning head-kick knockout of Charles Radtke — the same finish he scored in his 2012 promotional debut. The 43-year-old, the oldest fighter on the UFC roster, said he wants to spend time with his family after welcoming a baby daughter; the win also earned him a Performance of the Night bonus.
