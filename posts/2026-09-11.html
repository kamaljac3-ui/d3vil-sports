NFL

49ers demolish Rams 27-7 in the NFL's first-ever game on Australian soil

San Francisco routed Los Angeles 27-7 at Melbourne Cricket Ground in front of 100,021 fans — the seventh-largest crowd in regular-season history — to open the league's first-ever game played in Australia. Brock Purdy threw three touchdown passes, while the Rams, who'd landed in the country just 28 hours before kickoff, never found their footing despite a solid rushing day from Kyren Williams and Blake Corum.

Purdy said: "Shout out to our defense, getting that stop. That was huge."

Tua Tagovailoa banged up with an oblique strain days before Falcons' opener

Falcons quarterback Tua Tagovailoa strained his oblique during Thursday practice and is officially day-to-day, with backup Cooper Rush taking the bulk of first-team reps in his place. Atlanta is expected to make a final call on his availability closer to Sunday's road opener against the Steelers.

Source: ESPN

Josh Jacobs pleads no contest to battery charge, setting up a likely suspension

Packers running back Josh Jacobs pleaded no contest to misdemeanor battery and property-damage charges from a May incident at his Green Bay-area home, and was ordered to pay a $1,000 fine under a deferred judgment agreement. The NFL is expected to move him off the exempt list and issue a suspension — commonly six games under the league's personal conduct policy — just as Green Bay opens its season.

Jacobs said: "I take full responsibility."

NCAAW

Kate Koval's eligibility fight ends with an NCAA waiver

The NCAA granted former LSU center Kate Koval a legislative relief waiver, clearing her to transfer and play immediately rather than sit out a season. Koval, whose father serves in Ukraine's military, left LSU after the program signed a Russian recruit who had played professionally in Moscow, and she's now free to enter the transfer portal with two years of eligibility remaining.

His .948 OPS and MLB-leading total bases have him in the conversation for Chicago's first MVP since Kris Bryant in 2016.
