NFL

Buccaneers keep Baker Mayfield with a $165 million extension

Tampa Bay and Mayfield agreed to a three-year, $165 million extension that runs through 2029, days after the two sides had broken off talks earlier in camp over the quarterback's initial offer. It comes just before the Bucs' season opener and locks up the passer who revived his career and led back-to-back NFC South titles.

Source: ESPN

49ers lose starting defensive tackle Alfred Collins for the season

Collins tore his patellar tendon during a practice in Melbourne ahead of San Francisco's opener against the Rams, ending his second NFL season before it begins. Coach Kyle Shanahan confirmed Collins will need surgery and go on injured reserve, compounding a defensive line that also lost end Sam Okuayinonu to a broken foot last week.

Source: ESPN

Aaron Donald's debut pushed back to the Rams' home opener

The Rams confirmed Donald won't make the trip to Australia for Sunday's opener against San Francisco, with coach Sean McVay citing the toll of a 16-hour flight on a player still ramping up after un-retiring last month. His new target is Monday, September 21, when Los Angeles hosts the Giants.

SEC schedules a vote on whether to expel LSU

SEC commissioner Greg Sankey called a special meeting of conference CEOs for Thursday to consider terminating LSU's membership, after a Louisiana judge blocked the league from enforcing its rule barring former NFL players from college rosters. Expulsion needs a two-thirds vote and most reporting considers it unlikely to actually happen, but the filing is widely seen as the SEC drawing a hard line on its authority to govern itself.

Source: ESPN

LSU cracks the top 10 as Alabama craters in the Week 2 poll

Michigan was voted out of the rankings entirely despite a controversial Hail Mary win, and Oregon slipped after needing a late stop to escape Boise State.

Mensah grabs the Heisman betting lead ahead of Texas-Ohio State

Cleveland made Harden's three-year, roughly $97 million contract official on Tuesday, nearly three weeks after the two sides first agreed to terms, in what one outlet called a lengthy salary-cap scramble to fit the deal under the books. Pelicans and Grizzlies swap young pieces in a four-player trade

NCAAM

A D-III lawsuit tries to widen the eligibility fight beyond Division I

Emory forward and reigning D3hoops.com Player of the Year Benjamin Pearce filed a proposed federal class action arguing the NCAA's new age-based eligibility rule unfairly denies a fifth season to Division II and III athletes from the high school class of 2022, even though comparable Division I players got one. A win could open up an extra year of eligibility to thousands of lower-division athletes.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Arizona State lands a second 2027 four-star forward

Source: Zagsblog

WNBA

Hungary's improbable run earns a quarterfinal date with Team USA

Hungary beat Japan 84-63 at the FIBA Women's World Cup on Tuesday to reach the quarterfinals for the first time in 40 years, setting up a Thursday meeting with the four-time defending champion Americans. Réka Lelik led Hungary with 23 points, while the win was overshadowed by a serious-looking arm injury to Japan's Saki Hayashi.

Ogwumike said she doesn't currently see herself as qualified for the job, though she didn't rule out a future run at it.

MLB

Ohtani's pitching comeback is over for 2026

The Dodgers have shut down Shohei Ohtani's throwing program for the rest of the season, with manager Dave Roberts confirming the two-way star will focus solely on hitting down the stretch. Ohtani hasn't pitched since early July because of knee and biceps issues, and his return to the mound now waits until 2027 at the earliest.

Source: MLB.com

Yordan Alvarez chases the majors' first Triple Crown since 2012

The Astros slugger leads the American League in batting average and RBIs and is tied for the home run lead heading into the season's final stretch, putting him in position to become the first Triple Crown winner since Miguel Cabrera. A cooler August narrowed his batting-title cushion, so the closest of the three races remains far from decided with about a month left to play.

Source: MLB.com

Golf

LIV Golf's threatened bankruptcy is now official

LIV Golf filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection Tuesday in New Jersey, listing more than $500 million in debt with players including Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau and Dustin Johnson named as unsecured creditors. The league says it's lined up financing from London firm BC Partners to relaunch a scaled-down "LIV Golf 2.0" in 2027 with player ownership and an expanded, cut-based field.

Source: CNBC

Angel Yin cleared to play at the Solheim Cup after a health scare

U.S. captain Angela Stanford confirmed Yin has completed practice rounds and will compete at Bernardus Golf despite a stomach illness that hospitalized her for six days in June and cost her significant weight and muscle. Alternate Lilia Vu remains on-site as a precaution as foursomes open Friday.

Stanford said: "She's here. She's playing. She's very happy to be here."

Source: Golf Channel
