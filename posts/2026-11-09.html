MLB

Owners' revised cap offer lands as the lockout deadline nears

With the CBA set to lapse Dec. 1, MLB's owners sent the players' union a new economic framework on Nov. 3 proposing a salary cap starting at $258.2 million and a floor of $180.2 million once the system would kick in for 2028. The union has already pushed back on the structure itself, and with under three weeks left before the agreement expires, a lockout that would freeze free agency still looks like the likely outcome.

Source: Last Word on Sports

Eight qualifying offers go out as Tarik Skubal's market takes shape

Clubs tendered one-year, $23.15 million qualifying offers to eight free agents ahead of Monday's deadline, with those players now facing a Nov. 17 decision on whether to accept. Tarik Skubal sits atop this winter's open market, with reports linking the Yankees, Tigers, Angels, White Sox and Padres to his market.

Source: MLB.com

Orioles will trade anyone but Alonso as the GM Meetings heat up

Baltimore's front office, led by Mike Elias and new GM Jared Banner, has told teams it will listen on offers for the rest of the roster but won't move first baseman Pete Alonso, who hit 43 home runs with an .885 OPS in his first season with the Orioles. The club is hunting starting pitching, outfield depth and catching help, with infielders like Coby Mayo and Jackson Holliday among the likely trade chips.

Golf

Teenager Blades Brown stuns Ben James in a Cabo playoff

Nineteen-year-old Blades Brown ran down a seven-shot deficit with a final-round 63 and beat Ben James on the second playoff hole to win the World Wide Technology Championship in Los Cabos on Sunday, claiming his first PGA Tour title. At 19 years and five months, he's the Tour's youngest winner since 1931, and the victory locks up his card through 2028.

Source: Golf Channel

Rahm outlasts McIlroy and Hatton in Abu Dhabi

Jon Rahm closed with a 67 to finish 23-under at the DP World Tour's Abu Dhabi Aldar Championship, beating Tyrrell Hatton by two and Rory McIlroy by four for his 11th DP World Tour title. McIlroy's third-place finish still moved him past Patrick Reed into second in the Race to Dubai with one event left.

Source: Golf Channel

LIV Golf's bankruptcy tips fully into liquidation

At a Nov. 5 hearing, LIV Golf's lawyers confirmed the league is abandoning any revival plan and moving straight into full liquidation, with its remaining assets — including real estate and trademarks — set to be sold off. A formal wind-down plan was due to be filed with the court by Nov. 9.

Source: CNBC

DeChambeau offered a lifeline as a DP World Tour deadline nears

With LIV folding, the DP World Tour has told Bryson DeChambeau's camp he can claim full 2027 playing status under its major-winner exemption if he applies by Nov. 14. Jon Rahm has already committed to a full DP World Tour slate next season, while Cameron Smith's path remains unsettled as he weighs the same deadline.

Source: Golf Channel

Horse Racing

Sovereignty heads to stud after his Breeders' Cup Classic triumph

Godolphin's Sovereignty closed out his career with a length-and-three-quarter win over Forever Young in the Breeders' Cup Classic at Keeneland on Oct. 31, then was retired days later to Darley's Jonabell Farm, where he'll stand the 2027 season for a $150,000 fee. The 4-year-old finishes with eight wins in 13 starts and over $10.5 million earned, and the Classic win is expected to clinch him a second Horse of the Year title.

Source: Horse Racing Nation

Nitrogen's camp eyes a 2027 campaign after her Distaff win

Trainer Mark Casse said it's "quite likely" that Breeders' Cup Distaff winner Nitrogen returns for a 5-year-old season in 2027, with owners Len and Jon Green telling him she'll keep running as long as she stays sound and happy. The connections are considering the Pegasus World Cup at Gulfstream in January as an opening target and are even weighing a run against male horses later in the year.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Forever Young set for one last race before retiring to Japan

After finishing a career-worst fifth behind Sovereignty in the Breeders' Cup Classic, Forever Young's trainer Yoshito Yahagi announced Nov. 8 that the horse will make his final start in the Grade 1 Tokyo Daishoten on Dec. 29 before retiring to Shadai Stallion Station. Owner Susumu Fujita said the operation turned down "numerous offers from overseas" to keep the horse breeding in Japan.

Source: BloodHorse
