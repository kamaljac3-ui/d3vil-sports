NFL

Darnold's hip scare opens the door for Drew Lock in a wild Seattle win

Seahawks starter Sam Darnold left Wednesday night's opener against New England on Seattle's first series with a hip injury, and while a fracture was ruled out, coach Mike Macdonald said he still needed further testing. Backup Drew Lock threw for 187 yards and a touchdown in relief, and Seattle's defense sealed a 13-10 comeback by picking off Drake Maye three times in the fourth quarter.

Source: CBS Sports

Nick Bosa cleared for the NFL's first-ever regular season trip to Australia

The 49ers confirmed All-Pro edge rusher Nick Bosa will play in Thursday's opener against the Rams at Melbourne Cricket Ground, marking his return from a torn ACL and a summer of tendinitis just in time for the league's first regular season game held in Australia. Defensive coordinator Raheem Morris made clear there was never real doubt about his availability.

Morris said: "We're not hiding that."

Source: SF Standard

Cowboys lose All-Pro guard Tyler Smith for 4-6 weeks after thumb surgery

Dallas placed three-time Pro Bowler Tyler Smith on injured reserve after surgery to repair a ligament in his thumb that had been sapping his grip strength through camp. Coach Brian Schottenheimer said the procedure went well, with fourth-year lineman T.J. Bass expected to start at left guard in Smith's absence.

Source: ESPN

Texans lose second-year receiver Jayden Higgins for the season

Houston will open the year without wideout Jayden Higgins after he tore his ACL during a joint practice session with the Raiders, ending a promising second season before it starts. Higgins broke out as a rookie with 41 catches for 525 yards and six touchdowns, and his absence leaves a real hole in the Texans' passing game.

Source: NFL.com

CFB

SEC calls off its LSU expulsion vote as Baton Rouge concedes the fight

Thursday's planned vote on terminating LSU's SEC membership has reportedly been scrapped after LSU president Wade Rousse wrote commissioner Greg Sankey pledging not to challenge the presidents' meeting, effectively conceding the underlying eligibility dispute now that LSU's roster is locked for the season. It's a quiet ending to a standoff that had threatened, at least on paper, actual expulsion.

Source: Arkansas Democrat-Gazette

MAC formally appeals Michigan's controversial Hail Mary win

The Mid-American Conference filed an appeal asking the NCAA to overturn Michigan's 13-12 win over Western Michigan, arguing Big Ten replay officials misapplied clock-restoration rules on the game-winning Hail Mary and that the correct clock would have ended the game with WMU still ahead. The NCAA has since told the conference it has no process for reversing a final score once a game is over.

Source: Detroit News

CFP opens a 30-day exclusive window with ESPN on 24-team expansion

College Football Playoff officials have opened a month-long exclusive negotiating window with ESPN to hash out doubling the field from 12 to 24 teams as soon as 2027, though the SEC and Big Ten still hold final say and must agree by December 1. Reporting suggests the SEC favors a leaner 16-team model while the Big Ten wants a bigger 24-team field with fewer automatic conference-title byes.

Source: Saturday Down South

NBA

Ben Simmons completes his NBA comeback, signs with the Kings

After sitting out all of last season, the former No. 1 pick and three-time All-Star has officially signed a one-year, largely non-guaranteed deal with Sacramento worth roughly $3.5 million. It's a low-risk flier for the Kings and a genuine second chance for Simmons to show he can still contribute after years derailed by back issues.

Source: NBA.com

DeMar DeRozan signs with the Nuggets after Denver's stars recruited him

The six-time All-Star, waived by Sacramento this offseason, is joining Denver on a one-year, roughly $3.9 million deal after Nikola Jokic, Jamal Murray and Aaron Gordon reportedly pushed to bring him in over competing interest from Miami, Washington and New Orleans. At 37, DeRozan gets a real shot at a title with a contender instead of a bigger paycheck elsewhere.

Source: NBA.com

Clippers' front-office shakeup, not the league, is now what's holding up the Kawhi Leonard trade

New reporting says the long-expected Kawhi Leonard trade to Toronto — already cleared by the NBA after the Ballmer/cap-circumvention penalties — is stuck behind a Clippers front-office reshuffle, since the team still needs to install an interim governor for the suspended Steve Ballmer and confirm GM Trent Redden's authority before signing off. Toronto is reportedly ready to extend Leonard two years and $123.7 million once the paperwork clears.

Source: OpenCourt Basketball

WNBA

Team USA survives Italy, romps past Czech Republic to reach today's World Cup quarterfinal

Team USA needed a tense 55-52 win over Italy — reportedly its lowest-scoring World Cup outing since 1971 — before blowing out the Czech Republic 105-64 to close group play at the FIBA Women's World Cup in Berlin. That sets up Thursday's quarterfinal against Hungary, the surprise team that eliminated Japan earlier in the week.

Source: USA Basketball

Six of eight WNBA playoff spots are locked up with two weeks to go

The defending-champion Las Vegas Aces, the Golden State Valkyries, the Washington Mystics, the Indiana Fever, and the Dallas Wings have all clinched postseason berths as the regular season winds down, with the field of 15 teams cutting to the top eight when the playoffs open September 27. The league is currently on a pause for the FIBA international window before the regular season resumes September 17.

Source: ESPN

Red Wings' GM search narrows again as two internal names fall away

Detroit's search to replace Steve Yzerman took another turn as interim GM Shawn Horcoff and assistant GM Kris Draper were both informed they're no longer under consideration for the permanent job, days after outside candidate Jeff Greenberg withdrew. That reportedly leaves Florida assistant GM Brett Peterson, Dallas's Scott White and San Jose's Jeff Kealty as the remaining outside finalists with camp fast approaching.

Panthers captain Aleksander Barkov, who missed all of last season after tearing his ACL and MCL on the first day of training camp, says the knee no longer bothers him — and he backed it up by winning gold and earning tournament all-star honors with Finland over the offseason. Florida is expecting him back at full speed and full captaincy duties for 2026-27.

Source: NHL.com
