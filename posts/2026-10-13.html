NFL

49ers survive a Seattle shootout to improve to 5-0, but lose Deebo Samuel to injury

Eddy Pineiro's 43-yard field goal with 1:56 left capped a late San Francisco comeback in a 32-29 win over the Seahawks on Sunday, with Mike Evans going for 113 yards and a touchdown. The 49ers moved to 5-0, but receiver Deebo Samuel left the game with a hamstring injury, while Seattle's Jaxon Smith-Njigba also topped 100 yards with a score in the loss.

49ers' Dre Greenlaw suspended a game for headbutting a helmetless Seahawk

The NFL suspended linebacker Dre Greenlaw one game without pay for headbutting Seattle's helmetless Ernest Jones IV during Sunday's win, citing the league's sportsmanship rule. Greenlaw will miss the Oct. 19 game against Washington and is appealing; it would be his second suspension in as many years after one for threatening an official last season.

Puka Nacua sets a Monday Night Football receiving record in a losing effort as the Bills edge the Rams in overtime

CFB

Georgia takes over at No. 1 in the AP poll after Oklahoma's upset of Texas

The Bulldogs jumped from No. 2 to No. 1 with 66 of 70 first-place votes in Sunday's AP Top 25, with Notre Dame and Miami rounding out the top three. Texas, the preseason favorite that had been undefeated, tumbled all the way to No. 7, while Kentucky cracked the rankings at No. 15 off its overtime win over LSU.

Source: NCAA.com

SEC fines Kentucky $500,000 for fans storming the field after the LSU win — the money goes to LSU

The conference fined Kentucky a flat $500,000 after students rushed Kroger Field following the Wildcats' overtime win over LSU, with the money paid directly to LSU under the SEC's field-access policy since it was a conference game. Commissioner Greg Sankey said the penalty doesn't bend based on history: "Field rushing is field rushing, the first time or the 18th time."

Source: CBS Sports

Sarkisian refuses to hand off play-calling after Texas' Red River collapse

"Not a chance," Sarkisian said when asked about stepping away from calling plays, while framing the offense's struggles as fixable.

MLB

Brewers' bullpen sets a postseason record as Milwaukee takes a 2-0 NLCS lead

Milwaukee beat the Dodgers 7-4 in Monday's Game 2 at American Family Field, erasing three separate L.A. leads behind William Contreras' go-ahead hit. Game 3 is Wednesday in Los Angeles.
