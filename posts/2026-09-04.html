CFB

LSU wins its eligibility fight — two ex-NFL players cleared to face Clemson

After more than nine hours of testimony, Judge Will Jorden granted a preliminary injunction Thursday allowing tight end Dae'Quan Wright and defensive lineman Zxavian Harris to keep playing for LSU despite the SEC's ban on former pro athletes, part of a broader ruling covering 31 athletes seeking a fifth year of eligibility. The SEC, which had separately sued LSU and coach Lane Kiffin days earlier, says it will appeal immediately — but for now, both players can suit up Saturday when the Tigers host Clemson.

Source: ESPN

Source: Yahoo Sports

Thompson agreed to the rookie-scale extension Thursday, locking him up in Houston through 2032 and including a 10% trade kicker — notably, he left a bigger $251 million maximum extension on the table to get the deal done. He posted career highs across the board last season for a Rockets team building around him and Fred VanVleet.

NCAAM

Roddy Gayle Jr. wins a restraining order, can practice with Michigan for now

A Niagara County judge granted the national champion guard a TRO in his lawsuit against the NCAA on Wednesday, clearing him to practice and compete ahead of a preliminary-injunction hearing set for Sept. 17. Gayle, who played at Ohio State and Michigan, was denied a fifth year under the NCAA's current eligibility rules despite Michigan extending him a roster spot contingent on the outcome.

Horse Racing

New York state troopers make a historic arrest in a horse-slaughter pipeline

Troopers stopped a truck hauling 25 horses toward a Canadian slaughterhouse and arrested driver Robert Seitz, the first enforcement of a 2024 New York law banning horse slaughter for consumption since it took effect. The horses, shipped from a Pennsylvania livestock dealer, were taken to a Saratoga County veterinary clinic and will be put up for adoption.

Source: WCAX
