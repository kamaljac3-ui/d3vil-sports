NFL

Browns stun Steelers on a 56-yard, game-winning field goal

Cleveland erased a 21-7 first-half deficit and beat Pittsburgh 27-24 on Thursday Night Football when Andre Szmyt drilled a career-long 56-yard field goal with 10 seconds left. Deshaun Watson threw for 268 yards to set up the winning kick, extending the Steelers' winless streak in Thursday night divisional road games under Mike Tomlin.

NBA

Jalen Duren ends his Pistons holdout with a five-year, $200 million extension

Duren and Detroit agreed to a fully guaranteed five-year extension late Thursday, ending a months-long restricted-free-agency standoff that had kept the All-NBA center away from media day and the start of training camp. He gives up roughly $87 million compared to the five-year max he was eligible for, closing the book on a dispute both sides had described as more about respect than money.
