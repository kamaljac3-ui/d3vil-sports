October 18, 2026

A chaotic CFB Saturday topples No. 1 Georgia and No. 2 Notre Dame, and Jalen Hurts' blood clot scare rattles the NFL

NFL

Jalen Hurts placed on IR with a blood clot

The Eagles put their starting quarterback on injured reserve Saturday after a superficial blood clot, first flagged on an ultrasound following a Week 4 cleat injury, was confirmed worsening on a follow-up scan. Hurts will miss at least four games with no firm return timeline, and backup Tanner McKee takes over under center in his absence.

Source: ESPN

Broncos outlast defending-champion Seahawks 17-14 in overtime

Denver's defense carried Thursday's win with five sacks and three takeaways, including Ja'Quan McMillian's overtime interception of Sam Darnold that set up Wil Lutz's game-winning 53-yard field goal. Seattle had tied it late in regulation on a Jason Myers kick before the turnover-driven extra period, with the result pushing Denver to 4-2 and dropping the defending champs to 3-3.

Source: ESPN

CFB

Auburn stuns No. 1 Georgia on a last-second field goal

Trailing 14-0 early, Auburn scored 24 unanswered points before Georgia tied it late, and Alex McPherson's 34-yard field goal as time expired won it 27-24 after a forced fumble set up the final drive. It snapped Georgia's nine-game win streak in the series and handed the Bulldogs their first loss of the season.

Source: ESPN

No. 8 BYU knocks off No. 2 Notre Dame

Bear Bachmeier threw the go-ahead touchdown to Walker Lyons with 1:47 left, and BYU's defense held when Notre Dame's final pass fell incomplete in the end zone to seal a 28-24 upset. It's BYU's first win over an AP top-two team since 1990 and snapped Notre Dame's 16-game winning streak.

Source: ESPN

No. 14 Tennessee hands No. 6 Alabama a second straight loss

DeSean Bishop ran for 159 yards and two touchdowns, including the clincher with 3:17 left, as Tennessee's defense held Alabama to 201 total yards and forced four turnovers in a 26-10 win. Alabama fell to back-to-back regular-season losses for the first time since 2007, dropping to 5-2.

Source: ESPN

Florida upsets No. 7 Texas in Austin

First-time starter Tramell Jones Jr. threw two touchdowns to Lacota Dippre and Jadan Baugh added a fourth-quarter rushing score as Florida erased an early 10-0 deficit to win 27-24, with Arch Manning fumbling late while trying to lead a Texas comeback. It's Texas's second straight loss, its first such skid since Steve Sarkisian's first season in 2021.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Nuggets' Julian Strawther out at least six weeks with a knee sprain

Strawther got hurt in Denver's Oct. 14 preseason loss to the Clippers when defenders trapped him and he fell awkwardly; the team announced a six-week re-evaluation timeline Friday, just before the regular-season opener on Oct. 22 against Oklahoma City. The injury also complicates his pending rookie-scale extension talks.

Source: HoopsHype

Magic's Franz Wagner hurt his shoulder in the preseason finale

Wagner left Friday's game against Miami with right shoulder soreness after appearing to tweak it on a third-quarter three-point attempt, finishing with 11 points, six rebounds and six assists in just 19 minutes. Coach Sean Sweeney said only that he'd be "re-evaluated," and he's listed as questionable for Orlando's Oct. 21 opener against Atlanta.

Source: CBS Sports

Roster deadline triggers a wave of cuts ahead of opening night

With final rosters due Monday, teams moved Saturday: the Mavericks exercised Cooper Flagg's rookie option and waived two guards, the Knicks cut Bruce Brown, the Raptors waived five players while converting Nate Bittle to a two-way deal, and Miami released four players to finalize its 14-man roster.

Source: Hoops Rumors

NCAAM

Oregon's post-Altman era opens rocky under interim coach Stubblefield

Longtime Ducks coach Dana Altman retired Oct. 7, and his longtime assistant Tony Stubblefield, now interim coach for the season, lost his exhibition debut 71-65 to Seattle on Thursday in a turnover-plagued game (25 giveaways) as Oregon enters its first season in the Big Ten.

Source: 247Sports

Michigan State's Jeremy Fears Jr. piles up preseason honors

Fears was named a CBS Sports Preseason All-American this week, adding to his Big Ten Preseason Player of the Year honor from earlier in October. He and the Spartans now face a measuring-stick exhibition against UConn at Breslin Center on Oct. 20.

Source: MSU Athletics

WNBA

Valkyries steal Game 1 of the WNBA Finals

Golden State beat Atlanta 68-64 Saturday at Chase Center in the franchise's first-ever Finals game, with Gabby Williams scoring 16 of her 26 points in the second quarter and sealing it with four free throws in the final 78 seconds. Game 2 is Monday in San Francisco.

Source: ESPN

Gray returns from injury, but Dream's offense collapses late

Source: CBS Sports

South Carolina tabbed No. 1 in SEC preseason poll

MLB

ALCS tied 2-2 as Rays force a Game 5

Tampa Bay's offense broke out in a 7-3 win over Cleveland Saturday at Progressive Field, getting a go-ahead sixth-inning RBI double from Junior Caminero, a go-ahead two-run single from Jonathan Aranda and a ninth-inning two-run homer from Yandy Díaz. The series is knotted 2-2 heading into Sunday's Game 5 in Cleveland, with the winner facing a Brewers team already waiting in the World Series.

Source: ESPN

Rays shut down Shane McClanahan for the rest of the postseason

Manager Kevin Cash confirmed Sunday that McClanahan, who'd been dealing with forearm tightness since early September, won't pitch again this October. The move is framed as protecting his long-term health rather than a new setback, ending an encouraging 24-start comeback season just as Tampa Bay fights to stay alive in the ALCS.

Source: Fox Sports

NHL

Panthers make NHL history with a third straight natural hat trick

Carter Verhaeghe and Sam Bennett each scored three in an 8-3 rout of Seattle Saturday, giving Florida natural hat tricks in three consecutive games following Brady Tkachuk's back-to-back ones — a feat no team had ever accomplished before. It's an extraordinary hot streak for a team that was a Cup contender last season.

Source: ESPN

McDavid ties a Messier franchise mark in a five-point night

Connor McDavid had two goals and three assists in a 6-2 Oilers win over Utah Saturday, including a goal 40 seconds into the game that tied Mark Messier's franchise record for fastest opening-minute strike. He now leads the NHL with 23 points through just eight games.

Source: CBS Sports

Maple Leafs lose John Tavares to a week-to-week injury

Toronto placed its captain on the shelf after he was hurt in a collision during Thursday's overtime loss to Buffalo, with the team expecting him out roughly up to six weeks. It's a real blow given the Leafs were already down to 12 healthy forwards with Chris Tanev also out.

Source: CBC

UFC

Mike Malott stops Joaquin Buckley with a vicious knee at UFC Edmonton

Malott, ranked No. 13 entering the bout, knocked out the previously top-10 Buckley at 2:32 of round two Saturday in a result analysts are calling a KO-of-the-year contender. It's Buckley's third straight fight without a win and should push Malott into the welterweight top 10 once rankings update, settling the stakes previewed heading into the card.

Jasudavicius shocks Blanchfield on the same Edmonton card

Jasmine Jasudavicius upset the higher-ranked Erin Blanchfield by unanimous decision (29-28 across the board), while prospect Cristian Perez made a jaw-dropping Octagon debut with a 27-second knockout of Kyle Nelson — both notable results from a deep fight night.

Boxing

Daniel Dubois stops Fabio Wardley in 2 to retain the WBO heavyweight title

Dubois dropped Wardley early and finished him via TKO at 2:21 of round two at London's O2 Arena in Saturday's rematch, extending his heavyweight reign to 24-3. It settles the immediate rematch question and reopens talk of a Hrgovic or Usyk-related follow-up fight.

Source: Sky Sports

Fundora retains his title with a final-second knockout

Sebastian Fundora dropped previously unbeaten Ermal Hadribeaj with a left hand at 2:59 of the final round in Las Vegas, snapping his challenger's undefeated record in a fight Fundora was already winning on points. It's his fourth defense of the WBC 154-pound belt and keeps alive talk of an undisputed fight at the weight.

Source: ESPN

Premier Boxing Champions confirmed the whole fight is off because Figueroa felt too ill to box after missing weight Friday, leaving the WBA featherweight title vacant and bumping a middleweight bout to open the card. Kameda says he's now been promised priority positioning for the next title decider.

Source: The Ring

Golf

Yin overtakes Choi to win the Buick LPGA Shanghai

Ruoning Yin closed with a 68 and birdied the 17th to win at 11-under, running down third-round leader Hye-Jin Choi, who shot 71 and finished one back after her own late birdie came too late. It's Yin's second win of the season.

Source: LPGA.com

Chacarra wins the DP World India Championship, tops the Race to Dubai

After the weather-suspended third round carried over, Eugenio Chacarra played 25 holes Sunday — finishing a 63 in the morning, then a final-round 70 — to beat New Zealand's Daniel Hillier by two shots at Delhi Golf Club. It's his third DP World Tour win of the season and moves him to the top of the Race to Dubai standings, while Rory McIlroy faded out of contention with a third-round 74.

Source: GolfMagic
