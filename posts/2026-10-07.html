NFL

Eagles anchor Lane Johnson retires abruptly after 14 seasons

Johnson announced his retirement Tuesday, two days after a surprise scratch from Sunday's game for unspecified personal reasons. The two-time Super Bowl champion and six-time Pro Bowler, Philadelphia's No. 4 overall pick in 2013, said he needs to focus on his mental health and family after 171 games at right tackle.

Source: The Philadelphia Inquirer

NFL suspends an official for cursing at a Cardinals player — its first such punishment in 8 years

The league suspended line judge Jeff Seeman two games after video showed him swearing at Arizona tight end Trey McBride during the Cardinals' loss at the Giants, resolving the review flagged earlier this week. NFLPA executive director JC Tretter said officials should be held to the same standard as players, and a reporter who witnessed the exchange said McBride never cursed at Seeman first.

Source: ABC News
