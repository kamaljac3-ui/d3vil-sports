NFL

Broncos linebacker Jonathon Cooper placed on the exempt list amid domestic-violence charges

Denver outside linebacker Jonathon Cooper won't practice or play while on the NFL's commissioner's exempt list, placed there after two arrests in a seven-day span this June stemming from a domestic incident involving his girlfriend. He faces a felony strangulation charge and several misdemeanors, with a jury trial now set for Nov. 10-13, and the league says it's still reviewing whether further discipline is warranted.

CFB

North Carolina held TCU quarterback Jaden Craig to just 175 passing yards in a 15-10 defensive grind at Dublin's Aviva Stadium, handing Bill Belichick a win in his college coaching debut. It's a stark turnaround from last year's blowout loss to the Horned Frogs, built almost entirely on the Tar Heels' defense rather than any offensive fireworks.
