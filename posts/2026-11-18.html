NCAAM

Illinois's Andrej Stojakovic sets a 69-year-old Cameron Indoor scoring record in an upset of Duke

No. 10 Illinois stunned No. 5 Duke 99-78 on the road Tuesday, snapping the Blue Devils' 35-game homecourt winning streak, as Andrej Stojakovic poured in 46 points — the most ever by a visiting player at Cameron Indoor Stadium, breaking a mark that had stood since 1957. The Illini also won the rebounding battle 54-34 and outscored Duke in the paint 48-30.

Source: ESPN

Source: Washington Post
