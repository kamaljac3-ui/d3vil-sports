Kelsey Mitchell becomes the first guard to top 1,000 points in a WNBA season

Mitchell scored 33 points, including the record-breaking basket set up by Caitlin Clark, in Indiana's 103-85 win over the Toronto Tempo, passing A'ja Wilson's single-season scoring mark and finishing the night at 1,034 points. She also set new career and franchise 3-point records along the way, becoming just the second player ever to reach 1,000 points in a season.
