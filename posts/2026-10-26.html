NFL

Jonathan Taylor trade market tilts toward Seattle, away from the Giants

With the Nov. 10 deadline closing in, fresh reporting names the Seahawks as the new front-runner for Colts running back Jonathan Taylor, alongside the Packers, Broncos and Vikings — the Giants, floated as a suitor just a day earlier, are no longer in the mix after already addressing their quarterback need. Indianapolis (2-5) reportedly isn't rushing to sell and wants a few more weeks to see where it stands before deciding Taylor's fate.

Source: ClutchPoints

NFL and players' union open joint review into Eagles' handling of Hurts' blood clots

The league and NFLPA agreed to jointly review how the Eagles and team doctors managed Jalen Hurts' blood-clot diagnosis, after the union pushed for an investigation following his hospitalization and season-ending IR placement. The scrutiny centers on Hurts being cleared to play in Jacksonville on Oct. 11 despite an earlier clot finding in London.

Source: Philadelphia Inquirer

Skattebo and Singletary lead Giants past Texans in overtime

New York beat Houston 27-21 in overtime, with Cam Skattebo rushing for a career-high 124 yards — including a 42-yard burst in overtime — and Devin Singletary scoring the winning touchdown against his former team. J.J. McCarthy won his first career start at quarterback as Malik Nabers posted his fourth straight 100-yard receiving game, pushing the Giants to 4-2-1 heading into their bye.

Source: Athlon Sports

CFB

Coaching carousel spins again: Baylor and NC State both fire their coaches

NC State fired 14th-year coach Dave Doeren a day after a 31-28 loss at Stanford dropped the Wolfpack to 3-4, with special-teams coordinator Todd Goebbel taking over on an interim basis while a national search begins. A day later, Baylor fired seventh-year coach Dave Aranda following a 25-24 loss to Kansas that extended a two-game skid to 4-3, promoting defensive coordinator Joe Klanderman as interim. It's the third major-conference firing in about a week, after Maryland dismissed Mike Locksley on Friday.

Source: ESPN

Texas survives an Arch Manning turnover spree to upset No. 7 Ole Miss

No. 19 Texas built a 24-3 halftime lead at home before Arch Manning's three second-half turnovers, including a fumble returned for a touchdown, let Ole Miss tie the game in the fourth quarter. The Longhorns held on for a 34-27 win after intercepting a Hail Mary as time expired, improving to 5-2 in a week where coach Steve Sarkisian had publicly floated benching Manning if he struggled again.

Source: Texas Athletics

NBA

Thunder's Jalen Williams stretchered off with a frightening leg injury

Oklahoma City star Jalen Williams was carried off on a stretcher late in Sunday night's win over the Clippers after a bad landing twisted his lower leg, with coach Mark Daigneault saying the team wouldn't have a diagnosis until Monday morning medical evaluations. An outside sports-medicine doctor speculated an ankle fracture-dislocation is likely, which would typically require months of recovery if confirmed — a potentially serious blow to the defending conference finalists' title hopes.

Source: ESPN

Luka Doncic erupts for 52 as the Lakers rally past the Jazz to stay unbeaten

Doncic scored 52 points on 17-of-30 shooting with 10 assists and 8 rebounds as Los Angeles overcame an 18-point second-quarter deficit to beat Utah 132-123, with Austin Reaves icing it late en route to 29 points of his own. It was Doncic's 10th career 50-point game and made him just the third Laker ever to post 50 points and 10 assists in the same game, pushing LA to 3-0 on the season.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAM

No. 2 Duke survives No. 17 Kansas on the road in its exhibition finale

Duke needed a strong second half to escape Allen Fieldhouse with a 90-84 win over Kansas, its toughest test of the preseason after routing Belmont and Tennessee in earlier exhibitions. The result keeps the Blue Devils, No. 2 in the preseason AP poll, unbeaten heading into the regular season.

Source: Sports Illustrated

Rick Pitino's St. John's upsets No. 7 Texas at Madison Square Garden

St. John's closed its exhibition slate with an 87-74 win over No. 7 Texas, a sharp turnaround from a shaky four-overtime win over Rutgers the week before. Freshman center Theo Edema and guard Ian Jackson, who scored 18 points, led the way, with Pitino calling Jackson's performance his best game yet.

Source: Yahoo Sports

WNBA

Valkyries and Dream meet tonight for the title in a winner-take-all Game 5

Golden State and Atlanta are tied 2-2 heading into tonight's Game 5 at Chase Center, with the winner claiming a first-ever championship for either franchise. Dream guard Allisha Gray, who's been playing through a hand injury since the semifinals, was not on Atlanta's injury report ahead of the game — a good sign for a Dream offense that will need her scoring.

Source: NBC Sports

Valkyries' Kiah Stokes sets a WNBA Finals blocks record heading into Game 5

Golden State center Kiah Stokes passed Brittney Griner and Sylvia Fowles for the most blocks in a single Finals series, piling up 14 across the first four games while also grabbing 16 rebounds and holding Atlanta's Angel Reese to 2-of-12 shooting in Game 4. She's the first player in Finals history to record three or more blocks in four straight games.

Source: The San Francisco Standard

NHL

Jets fall in the Heritage Classic, extend their skid to five in first game since the Hellebuyck trade

Montreal beat Winnipeg 2-1 in the outdoor Heritage Classic at Princess Auto Stadium, with Lane Hutson and Kaiden Guhle scoring for the Canadiens and goalie Jacob Fowler making 33 saves. It was the Jets' first game since trading goalie Connor Hellebuyck to New Jersey, and captain Adam Lowry said there was a sense of "relief" that the drawn-out situation was finally over, even as the loss extended Winnipeg's skid to five straight.

Source: NHL.com

Top jockey Irad Ortiz Jr. was hospitalized after being thrown when She's Good Trouble stumbled leaving the gate in the final race at Keeneland on Thursday. Forever Young, reigning Horse of the Year Sovereignty and Golden Tempo are expected to headline the field, with the post-position draw set for later today at Keeneland.
