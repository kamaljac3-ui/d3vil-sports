NFL

Aaron Donald comes out of retirement, and the Rams' offensive line notices immediately

Aaron Donald, retired since early 2024, signed a one-year, $20 million deal and made his season debut in Monday night's 28-6 rout of the Giants, drawing constant double-teams and finishing with three tackles, including one for a loss. It's one of the more improbable in-season additions in recent NFL memory for a six-time first-team All-Pro who had been out of football for roughly 32 months.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Giants rookie Jaxson Dart's knee injury is worse than first believed

Dart was hurt on New York's opening drive of Monday's loss to the Rams and was initially diagnosed with a sprained MCL, but a follow-up MRI reportedly showed more extensive damage, with season-ending surgery now a real possibility. Coach John Harbaugh said Tuesday the team is still weighing its options, noting “we haven't got any final decisions yet,” while Jameis Winston is positioned to take over as the starter if Dart is out for good.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Dak Prescott passes Tony Romo for the Cowboys' all-time touchdown-passing record

Prescott threw four touchdowns, including a fourth-quarter strike to CeeDee Lamb, in Dallas's 37-20 win over Washington, pushing his career total past Romo for the most passing scores in franchise history. He reached the mark in his 141st game with the team.

Source: CBS Sports

NFL hands out its first fines under a new escalating player-safety rule

Under a 2026 policy where three fines for any of five emphasized safety fouls in a season trigger an automatic one-game suspension, the league issued its first three fines this week to the Bills' Sam Franklin, the Packers' Evan Williams and the Dolphins' Chris Johnson for hits ranging from a blindside block to a hip-drop tackle. The NFLPA confirmed all three players are appealing.

Source: NBC Sports

Taylor, a lightly recruited prospect entering the season, is now drawing real Heisman buzz nationally.

Georgia and Clemson quietly scrap their planned home-and-home series

Records obtained this week show the two programs canceled a four-game series set for 2029, 2030, 2032 and 2033. Georgia AD Josh Brooks pointed to the SEC's move to a nine-game conference schedule and expected College Football Playoff expansion as the reason, while leaving open the door to a future neutral-site matchup instead.

NBA

Kawhi Leonard signs a two-year, $115 million extension to stay in Toronto

Leonard and the Raptors agreed to an extension running through the 2028-29 season, with a player option on the second year; he reportedly took roughly $11 million below his max and waived a trade kicker in the process. The deal signals Toronto expects him to finish his career there.

Source: CP24

Hawks and Hornets complete a Hield-for-Finney-Smith trade after a rookie center's ACL tear

Atlanta sent Buddy Hield, Ryan Nembhard and cash to Charlotte for forward Dorian Finney-Smith, finalizing a deal that had been rumored for days. The move came a day after the Hawks announced second-round rookie Henri Veesaar tore his ACL and will miss the season, pushing Atlanta to shore up its frontcourt depth immediately.

Source: ESPN

Kevin Durant says he isn't worried about a minutes restriction or his contract option

At Houston's Media Day, Durant said he wants heavy minutes again after finishing third in the league in minutes per game last season at age 37, brushing off questions about the option year in his deal. Coach Ime Udoka added bluntly that “I don't think anybody met our expectations” from Durant's first season with the Rockets, which ended in a first-round exit.

Source: Newsweek

Michigan State opens practice with an early ankle scare for Cam Ward
