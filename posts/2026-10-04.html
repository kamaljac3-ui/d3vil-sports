NFL

Cowboys add CB Joey Porter Jr. in third straight trade for a defensive starter

Dallas sent a 2026 second-round pick and a conditional sixth-rounder to Pittsburgh for cornerback Joey Porter Jr., capping a run of trades that's also brought in defensive tackle Quinnen Williams from the Jets and edge rusher Rashan Gary from the Packers. Jerry Jones continues reshaping the defense on the fly rather than waiting for an offseason.

Source: Dallas Cowboys

49ers owner Jed York suspended six games, fined $500,000

The NFL suspended 49ers owner Jed York six games and fined him $500,000 for violating the personal-conduct policy, stemming from his no-contest plea in August to two misdemeanor charges after an arrest in Ohio. York has already served three games and is eligible to return Oct. 20, with his first home game back on Nov. 8 against the Raiders. "I am deeply sorry," York said in a statement.

Source: The San Francisco Standard

Seahawks rookie RB Jadarian Price lands on IR

Seattle placed rookie running back Jadarian Price, the final pick of this year's draft, on injured reserve after he aggravated a chest injury in practice, ruling him out at least four games. Price had managed just 119 yards on 28 carries through three games, and the Seahawks signed running back Velus Jones off the practice squad to help fill the gap.

Source: NFL.com

CFB

No. 25 Missouri blows out No. 8 Florida, 45-17

Missouri snapped a nine-game losing streak against ranked opponents and picked up its first win over a top-10 team since 2013 with a 45-17 rout of Florida on Saturday. Quarterback Austin Simmons threw for 340 yards and two touchdowns while running back Jamal Roberts added 211 yards and three scores on the ground.

Source: ESPN

Alabama's Keelon Russell surges to the top of Heisman odds

Alabama quarterback Keelon Russell jumped to the front of Heisman betting boards after throwing for 285 yards and accounting for four touchdowns in a 56-23 win over Mississippi State. Through five weeks he has 1,129 total yards and 11 touchdowns, with a PFF grade that ranks fourth among all FBS quarterbacks.

Source: Touchdown Alabama

Colorado State fires defensive coordinator mid-game

Colorado State coach Jim Mora stripped defensive coordinator Tyson Summers of play-calling duties in the first quarter of Saturday's 56-26 homecoming loss to Oregon State, after the defense had allowed 100 points over its previous two games combined. Summers was fired once the game ended.

Source: Bleacher Report

NBA

Raptors lock up guard Jamal Shead with a three-year extension

Toronto signed guard Jamal Shead to a three-year, $24 million extension that runs through the 2029-30 season. Shead started all 82 games last season and led the NBA in bench assists.

Source: NBA.com

Haliburton enters camp with "no restrictions," 16 months after Achilles tear

Pacers star Tyrese Haliburton says he's fully cleared heading into training camp, 16 months after tearing his Achilles in the NBA Finals. "There will be no restrictions. I'm good, I'm ready to go for the year," Haliburton said, with Indiana's preseason opener set for Oct. 7 against Minnesota.

Source: ESPN

NCAAM

Appeals court strips back the fifth-year eligibility bid

An Ohio appeals court unanimously reversed a preliminary injunction that had let some players seek a fifth season of eligibility under the NCAA's new age-based rule, immediately making roughly two dozen athletes ineligible again. Judge Candace Crouse wrote that the players "understood that they were entitled to four years... and that is what they received."

Source: Courthouse News

UConn tops the Big East men's preseason poll

UConn earned 8 of 10 first-place votes to top the Big East's men's preseason poll for the first time since 2024-25, with St. John's, Villanova, Marquette and Providence rounding out the top five. The Huskies also swept the conference's individual preseason awards.

Source: Big East Conference

Duke kicks off the year with "Countdown to Craziness"

Duke opened its season festivities Saturday night with the Countdown to Craziness scrimmage at Cameron Indoor, where Team White beat Team Blue 29-24 in an Elam-ending finish capped by a buzzer-beating three from Deron Rippey Jr.

Source: BVM Sports

WNBA

Valkyries host defending-champ Aces in semifinal Game 1

Second-seeded Golden State hosts top-seeded Las Vegas today in Game 1 of the WNBA semifinals at Chase Center, the Valkyries' first semifinal appearance in franchise history. The Aces swept the regular-season series 3-0 despite finishing a game behind Golden State in the standings.

Source: Valkyries / WNBA.com

Dream host Liberty in the other semifinal opener

Atlanta hosts New York today in the other semifinal, with the Dream back in the final four for the first time since 2018 behind Allisha Gray's 22-point effort in their first-round sweep of Washington. The Liberty reached this round as the first No. 8 seed ever to eliminate a No. 1 seed, having swept Minnesota.

Source: The Washington Times

NCAAW

JuJu Watkins named Big Ten Preseason Player of the Year

USC senior JuJu Watkins was named the Big Ten's Preseason Player of the Year as she returns from the ACL tear that cost her all of last season. It's the second time Watkins has won the preseason honor, after going on to claim the in-season award in 2024-25; teammate Jazzy Davidson also made the preseason all-conference team.

Source: USC Athletics

MLB

Brewers escape via a roof-rule call to win NLDS Game 1

Milwaukee held off San Diego 3-2 after a would-be go-ahead two-run homer by Ty France struck the retractable roof's truss over fair territory in the ninth inning and was ruled a live-ball out on replay review. William Contreras' seventh-inning solo shot proved to be the difference.

Source: ESPN

Rasmussen takes a no-hit bid into the eighth as Rays edge Yankees

Rays starter Drew Rasmussen retired 15 straight Yankees and carried a no-hitter into the eighth inning of Tampa Bay's 1-0 ALDS Game 1 win, finishing with eight innings, one hit and 10 strikeouts. Austin Wells broke up the no-hit bid with two outs in the eighth but was thrown out at third trying to stretch it into more.

Source: MLB.com

White Sox blank Guardians behind Murakami's 440-foot blast

Chicago beat Cleveland 3-0 in ALDS Game 1 as Munetaka Murakami's two-run homer — the hardest-hit ball of this postseason at 112.2 mph off the bat — proved decisive, with five White Sox pitchers combining on a three-hitter.

Source: KSAT / AP
