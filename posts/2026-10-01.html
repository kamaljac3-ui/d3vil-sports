NFL

Steelers trade Joey Porter Jr. to the Cowboys

Pittsburgh dealt cornerback Joey Porter Jr. to Dallas for a 2028 second-round pick and a conditional 2027 sixth-rounder, parting ways after a back injury and stalled contract talks kept him out of the lineup early this season. The Cowboys, who had allowed seven passing touchdowns and zero interceptions through three games, add him ahead of Thursday's game at Cleveland.

Source: NBC Sports

Packers' Jayden Reed done for the season after neck surgery

Green Bay placed receiver Jayden Reed on injured reserve after a Sept. 20 hit against the Jets left him needing neck surgery. Coach Matt LaFleur said he doesn't believe the injury is career-threatening, though no recovery timetable has been given.

Source: WEAU

CFB

Michigan State's Cam Edwards done for the year with a knee injury

The Spartans will finish the season without running back Cam Edwards, who went down on a hit from Nebraska's Danny Odem III during Saturday's 31-13 loss. Edwards had rushed for 304 yards and three touchdowns in three-plus games before the injury, and Michigan State turns to Jaziun Patterson and Marvis Parrish in the backfield.

Source: ESPN

Oregon's Dante Moore taken off the field by ambulance, diagnosed with a concussion

Oregon quarterback Dante Moore was stretchered off after a late, head-high hit from USC linebacker Desman Stephens II, who was ejected and later suspended one game. Coach Dan Lanning said clean MRI and CT scans mean the injury isn't expected to be season-ending.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Clippers' Brandon Ingram to miss start of the season

Brandon Ingram, acquired in the Kawhi Leonard-to-Toronto trade, will miss the start of the 2026-27 season after a partially torn Achilles was discovered during his May heel surgery. "My recovery has been going good ... it's a week-by-week thing," Ingram said at media day.

Atlanta center Kristaps Porzingis will miss the start of training camp with an undisclosed issue, a separate setback from the Achilles tendinitis that limited him earlier this year. No return timetable has been set.

MLB

Chicago finished off a 2-0 sweep of Houston in the AL Wild Card Series with a 7-3 Game 2 win capped by a three-run homer from Chase Meidroth — the White Sox's first postseason series win in 21 years. They open the ALDS Saturday in Cleveland against the Guardians.

NHL
