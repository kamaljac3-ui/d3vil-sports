NFL

Aaron Donald ends a two-year retirement to rejoin a loaded Rams front

Three-time Defensive Player of the Year Aaron Donald is returning to the Los Angeles Rams on a one-year deal reported at $20 million, with incentives that could push it to $30 million. He worked out for the team twice in August before making it official, and will now line up alongside newly acquired Myles Garrett on what looks like the most fearsome defensive front in the league.

Source: CBS Sports

Packers running back Josh Jacobs sidelined by the league amid criminal charges

The NFL placed Josh Jacobs on the Commissioner's Exempt List after he was charged Aug. 27 with misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property stemming from a May incident. He'll keep being paid and can attend meetings, but can't practice or play while the league's review continues; his first court appearance is set for Nov. 17.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Cowboys land former first-round tackle Broderick Jones from the Steelers

Dallas acquired 2023 first-rounder Broderick Jones, sending Pittsburgh a 2027 third- and sixth-round pick, with the Steelers attaching a fourth-rounder of their own just to get the deal done. Jones, who had spinal fusion surgery after a neck injury last season and lost his grip on Pittsburgh's tackle job, reunites with fellow Georgia product George Pickens in a contract year.

Source: CBS Sports

CFB

Florida State's season-opening win raises more questions than it answers

Florida State led just 17-7 in the third quarter before closing on a 17-0 run for a 34-17 win over New Mexico State in its opener. Quarterback Ashton Daniels accounted for a touchdown passing and rushing, but a shaky start against an outmatched opponent left the Seminoles' ceiling very much in question heading into a tougher slate.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Transfer QB Beau Pribula runs wild in his Virginia debut

Making his first start after transferring from Missouri by way of Penn State, Beau Pribula threw for 155 yards and two touchdowns and ran for 110 more as Virginia routed NC State 34-8 in both teams' season and ACC opener. It's the first 100-yard rushing game by a Virginia quarterback since Brennan Armstrong in 2022.

Source: ACC

NBA

Pelicans land Bennedict Mathurin on a short, team-friendly deal

New Orleans agreed to a two-year, $16 million contract with restricted free agent Bennedict Mathurin after his camp asked the Clippers to withdraw his $8.8 million qualifying offer. The shorter deal, which includes a player option, gets the 24-year-old wing back to unrestricted free agency next summer instead of locking him into longer team control.

Source: ESPN

Timberwolves complete the Konchar stretch-waiver to finish clearing Kuminga's cap room

Minnesota officially waived guard John Konchar using the stretch provision right at the league's Aug. 29 deadline, spreading his $6.2 million salary over three years at roughly $2 million annually. It's the last step in the plan that began with last week's Josh Green sign-and-trade, closing out the paperwork needed to sign Jonathan Kuminga.

Source: RealGM

Nothing new broke today in the sport's ongoing eligibility litigation — the Dallas County case and the other pending challenges remain on hold without a fresh ruling, with the next major hearing not until Sept. 11. Otherwise, men's college basketball stayed in its typical late-August lull ahead of the season.

WNBA

Angel Reese sets the WNBA's single-season double-double record

Reese posted her 29th double-double of the season — 15 points and 11 rebounds — as the Atlanta Dream beat the Minnesota Lynx 89-81 in the last game before the FIBA World Cup break, breaking Alyssa Thomas' 2023 mark of 28. Allisha Gray added 23 points and 11 rebounds as the Dream led wire-to-wire and snapped Minnesota's eight-game road win streak.

Source: Washington Times

Jessica Shepard closes the regular season with the league's second-most double-doubles

Shepard recorded her 22nd double-double of the year (17 points, 10 rebounds) as Dallas beat Connecticut 97-71 in both teams' finale before the FIBA break, extending the Wings' win streak to four. Arike Ogunbowale added 18 points as Dallas posted its best win total since 2007.

Source: TSN

A quiet stretch for women's college basketball

MLB

Rays complete an MLB-best 14th series sweep with an extra-inning walk-off

Down 3-1 in the ninth against Padres closer Mason Miller, Tampa Bay tied it when Jonny DeLuca's long fly ball bounced off the outfield wall and out of a fielder's glove for a two-run "homer." Jorge Mateo's bases-loaded single in the 11th completed a 5-4 win, giving the Rays their major-league-leading 14th sweep of the season and a fourth straight win.

Source: The Ticket (AP)

LaMonte Wade Jr.'s pinch-hit blast lifts the first-place Astros past the Mets

Houston manager Joe Espada pulled his starting right fielder in the sixth to send up lefty pinch-hitter LaMonte Wade Jr., who needed just two pitches to launch a go-ahead two-run homer off the facade in right field. Jeremy Peña added a leadoff homer in Houston's 6-3 win, keeping the Astros two games up on Texas in the AL West.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Pete Alonso takes a scary fastball off the helmet, but the Orioles finish a sweep anyway

Alonso was drilled in the helmet by a 94.6 mph fastball in the eighth inning Sunday, needed a visit from trainers, but walked to first under his own power before being replaced by a pinch-runner. Manager Craig Albernaz said afterward that Alonso is fine, as Baltimore completed a three-game sweep of Oakland with an 8-5 win.

Source: Athlon Sports

NHL

Red Wings' contract standoff with Simon Edvinsson drags on without a GM in place

Detroit's extension talks with restricted free agent defenseman Simon Edvinsson have been frozen since Steve Yzerman stepped down as general manager in July, leaving the two sides without a lead negotiator roughly three weeks before training camp. Comparable young defensemen have since re-set the market — Chicago's Bowen Byram signed for $12.5 million a year — pushing Edvinsson's eventual price well past earlier projections.

Source: Pro Hockey Rumors

Boxing

Moses Itauma discharged from the hospital after his brutal title-fight loss

A day after being carried out of the O2 Arena on a stretcher and given oxygen, Itauma's promotional team confirmed he was discharged that same night and is recovering at home following his ninth-round stoppage loss to Filip Hrgović. Promoter Frank Warren said Itauma suffered a leg injury during the fight, though no formal diagnosis has been released.

Source: BoxingScene

New IBF champ Filip Hrgović wants unification, not his mandatory defense

Hrgović said he'd rather bypass mandatory challenger Frank Sanchez in favor of a unification bout with WBO titleholder Daniel Dubois or WBC champion Agit Kabayel, both of whom sat ringside for his upset win. The move comes as Dubois has already booked an immediate rematch with Fabio Wardley for Oct. 17.

"I want this guy right here, Dubois, unification... Frank Sanchez will wait." — Filip Hrgović

"If we want the rematch we have to do three-minute rounds... if it means vacating the WBC belt again I don't mind doing that." — Chantelle Cameron

Alycia Baumgardner skips a tune-up, jumps straight into a lightweight title challenge

The unified 130-pound champion will move up two weight classes to challenge IBF lightweight titleholder Elif Nur Turhan on Nov. 8 in Arlington, Texas, bidding to become a two-division champion without a fight at 135 pounds first. A win would put her directly in line for a unification clash with lightweight champ Caroline Dubois, extending a rivalry that boiled over into a face-off after Dubois' win over Amelia Moore.

Golf

Scottie Scheffler runs down Hovland to win a second FedEx Cup

Scheffler closed with a 4-under 66 — sparked by a tee shot to two inches on the par-3 second — to catch and pass 54-hole leader Viktor Hovland and win the Tour Championship by three shots at East Lake. The $10 million bonus pushed Scheffler past Tiger Woods on the PGA Tour's all-time money list and made him just the third player, after Woods and Rory McIlroy, to win multiple FedEx Cups.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Ruoning Yin ends a two-year drought with the FM Championship title

Yin closed with a 2-under 70 to beat Hyo Joo Kim by two shots at TPC Boston, snapping a 672-day winless stretch since her last LPGA title in 2024. It's her sixth career win, with Allisen Corpuz finishing third and Rose Zhang a shot further back.

Source: WRAL (AP)

Horse Racing

Travers winner Leading Change's next stop is anything but settled

Trainer Brad Cox said the lightly raced colt will ship back to Churchill Downs this week with no target picked yet — the Sept. 18 Jockey Club Gold Cup at Belmont is one option, but connections may bypass this fall's Breeders' Cup Classic entirely in favor of a run at next year's Dubai World Cup, a race Cox and owners Wathnan Racing already won with Hit Show.

Source: Thoroughbred Daily News

King's Counsel breaks his maiden in style at Woodbine's Simcoe Stakes

The 2-year-old gelding, bought for just over $59,000 as a yearling, swung wide on the turn and held off Longshot Leo by a head to win the $300,000 Simcoe Stakes on his fourth career start. Scorching also took the $200,000 Elgin Stakes for Canadian-breds on the same Sunday card at Woodbine.

Source: Woodbine
