NBA

NBA strips the Clippers of five first-round picks and fines them $30 million over a Kawhi Leonard scheme

An independent investigation found the Clippers funneled off-court endorsement deals through corporate partners — including Aspiration Partners, Boingo Wireless, Daktronics and Lockton Insurance — to Kawhi Leonard in exchange for contract concessions, and the league responded Wednesday by stripping Los Angeles of a first-round pick in every draft from 2029 to 2033 and fining the team $30 million. Owner Steve Ballmer was suspended a year, two top executives were suspended as well, Leonard himself must pay $700,000 in restitution, and the penalties are final with no appeal — a ruling severe enough that it also cleared the long-paused trade sending Leonard to Toronto to finally go through.

Source: CNN

Russell Westbrook trades his sneakers for a boardroom seat at upstart league Project B

It's the highest-profile addition yet for a venture positioning itself as a global alternative to existing pro leagues.
