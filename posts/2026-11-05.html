NFL

Ravens finalize a trade for QB Jacoby Brissett

With Lamar Jackson out after thumb surgery and backup Tyler Huntley lost for the year to turf toe, Baltimore is finalizing a trade for Cardinals quarterback Jacoby Brissett, who had already been bumped down Arizona's depth chart after requesting a trade of his own. Reports differ on whether he's ready for Thursday's game against Jacksonville or has to wait until next week against the Chargers, and ESPN's Adam Schefter says the deal isn't expected to close until the weekend.

Source: ESPN

Browns' Deshaun Watson enters concussion protocol

Watson took a helmet-to-helmet hit from Steelers safety Jaquan Brisker and was initially cleared to keep playing, but told Cleveland's medical staff Monday night he was feeling concussion symptoms, and the team placed him in protocol Wednesday. Coach Todd Monken said clearing it without practice reps is "a little more difficult for a quarterback," leaving Shedeur Sanders on standby to start Sunday at New Orleans if Watson can't go.

Source: ESPN

Brandon Aiyuk files for reinstatement, set to report to the 49ers

Aiyuk, who hasn't played since tearing his knee in October 2024, formally filed reinstatement paperwork through new agent Drew Rosenhaus and is expected back at San Francisco's facility Friday. The 49ers face a decision by Tuesday's trade deadline on whether to keep him, trade him, or release him, though GM John Lynch has said he doesn't expect Aiyuk to play for the team again.

Source: NBC Sports

Micah Parsons practices for the first time since his ACL tear

Parsons took part in individual drills Wednesday, his first practice reps since tearing his ACL and meniscus last December, opening a 21-day window for the Packers to activate him. Green Bay is targeting a return around Thanksgiving week against the Rams, and Parsons said his knee feels "great."

Source: WBAY/Yahoo Sports

CFB

First CFP rankings land, and the SEC's placement draws backlash

The actual first 2026 College Football Playoff rankings put Miami at No. 1 and slotted several two-loss SEC teams — Alabama, Ole Miss and Tennessee among them — well above where the AP poll had them, while Notre Dame fell seven spots below its AP ranking to No. 11, the widest committee-versus-poll gap on the board. That makes Saturday's Notre Dame-Miami game close to a must-win for the Irish's at-large hopes, and it drops Ohio State to No. 12, outside the field if the season ended today.

Source: NBC Sports

Russell Wilson interviews for the NC State job

Per ESPN's Adam Schefter, longtime NFL quarterback Russell Wilson has interviewed for North Carolina State's vacant head-coaching job, open since Dave Doeren was fired last month. NC State has also reportedly looked at fellow veteran NFL quarterback Philip Rivers for the position, an unusual pairing of candidates for one college opening.

Source: Larry Brown Sports

NBA

Wembanyama's 52-point, 24-rebound game is unlike anything in 52 years

Victor Wembanyama set career highs with 52 points, 24 rebounds and 8 blocks as San Antonio held off Dallas 112-111 on Wednesday, becoming the first player in 52 years to post a 50-point, 20-rebound, 5-block game. His 29-point, 15-rebound first half alone is unlike anything tracked since play-by-play data began in the mid-1990s, and Kyrie Irving's potential buzzer-beating winner for the Mavericks rimmed out.

Source: AP/ABC News

Warriors stop paying Kristaps Porziņģis amid retirement talk

Golden State has started withholding Porziņģis's twice-monthly salary payments, telling the union he isn't "rendering services" after his absence from the team shifted from a general-illness designation to simply "not with team," with retirement increasingly the expectation around the league. The Warriors say they have no plans to void his contract and are leaving themselves trade flexibility through February's deadline, and neither Porziņģis nor the players' union has disputed the withheld pay.

Source: CBS Sports

76ers ride an 8-1 start, but LeBron says it's too soon for Finals talk

Philadelphia beat Detroit 112-106 on Wednesday to move to 8-1, the NBA's best record in the East and two games clear of the Pistons, with Jaylen Brown's season-high 30 points and a game-saving block on Cade Cunningham leading the way. LeBron James credited the team's early chemistry but cautioned it's "too early to be talking Finals."

Source: TSN

NCAAM

Casey Alexander loses his debut as Kansas State's head coach

Alexander, hired in March to replace the fired Jerome Tang, dropped his first game running the Wildcats, a 68-66 home loss to South Dakota State on Tuesday. It's a rough opening note for a rebuild that was supposed to stabilize a program still searching for its footing.

Source: Kansas State Athletics

Texas Tech's JT Toppin still has no timetable for his ACL return

Toppin, a preseason All-American who was averaging 21.8 points and 10.8 rebounds before tearing his ACL in February, still isn't practicing with the team, and coach Grant McCasland says there's no target date yet for his return. The Red Raiders open the season without one of the sport's best players at full strength.

Source: HoopsHQ

WNBA

Fever hire Sheri Sam as assistant GM

Indiana hired former Trail Blazers scouting manager Sheri Sam as assistant general manager this week, as the Fever continue rebuilding a front office gutted by October's departures of team president Kelly Krauskopf and GM Amber Cox. The team has also retained an outside search firm to run its GM search, with ownership planning to weigh in alongside player input.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Liberty's coaching search puts an internal name in the mix

Days after firing Chris DeMarco, GM Jonathan Kolb's fourth coaching search since 2019 has an early name attached: assistant Andrew Wade, whose ties to two underwhelming Liberty seasons are already drawing scrutiny as a complication for his own candidacy.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAW

Oklahoma's Keeley Parks out for the season

Sooners guard Keeley Parks will miss all of 2026-27 after a non-contact knee injury in practice, with surgery scheduled following the team's season-opening trip to Paris. Coach Jennie Baranczyk called the news "heartbreaking" for a player who was expected to be a key piece of the rotation.

Source: Oklahoma Athletics

Columbia wins its opener with its coach still serving a suspension

The Lions beat Davidson 71-65 without head coach Megan Griffith, who is serving the first of a three-game ban issued after an external review of her coaching conduct and is set to return Nov. 12 against Michigan. Junior Mary Ashley Stevenson led the way with 20 points in her Lions debut.

Source: Columbia Athletics

MLB

MLBPA rejects owners' salary-cap proposal as a lockout looms

With the CBA expiring Dec. 1, owners offered to phase in a salary cap and floor starting in 2028 in exchange for shortening free-agency eligibility and raising minimum salaries, and the players' union flatly rejected it, calling the plan one "designed to line their pockets and increase their franchise values." Phillies union rep Garrett Stubbs called a cap a non-starter for the players, and a lockout now looks increasingly likely once the deadline hits.

Source: Dodger Blue

Orioles say nearly everyone is available — except Pete Alonso

Baltimore is listening on offers for almost its entire roster as it chases starting pitching, outfield and catching help, according to the Baltimore Banner, with one notable exception: Alonso, who hit 43 home runs in his first season with the club. It's not a fire sale, and a Gunnar Henderson trade is considered unlikely given what it would cost to pry him loose.

Source: MLB Trade Rumors

Hall of Fame sets its ballot calendar, with Buster Posey headlining 2027

The Contemporary Baseball Era Committee's eight-candidate ballot — including managers Dusty Baker, Bruce Bochy and Joe Maddon — was unveiled this week, with results due Dec. 6. The BBWAA's full 2027 writers' ballot, led by first-time-eligible Buster Posey, will be revealed Nov. 16.

Source: BVM Sports

NHL

Dylan Larkin's trade saga takes a new turn as Boston circles

Detroit's suspended, unpaid captain has already drawn Capitals interest, but Boston.com now reports the Bruins are positioning themselves to pounce if Larkin signals he'd welcome a move there, with his agent saying "Dylan's had enough of the situation." Interim GM Shawn Horcoff says the Red Wings are still exploring trade options as the standoff drags on.

Source: Boston.com

Cutter Gauthier scores his second straight hat trick

Gauthier potted his second consecutive hat trick as Anaheim beat Vancouver 5-4 on Tuesday, and now leads the NHL with 8 power-play goals — 6 of his 8 goals this season have come on the man advantage. It's an early-season breakout for the young Ducks winger.

Source: NHL.com

Jason Robertson's natural hat trick lifts the Stars past Washington

Robertson scored all three of his goals in the second period, within a five-minute span, as Dallas outlasted the Capitals 6-5 on Tuesday for his seventh career hat trick. The teams combined for six goals in that middle frame alone.

Source: NHL.com

UFC

Amanda Nunes comes out of retirement to challenge Kayla Harrison at UFC 334

Nunes, who retired after beating Irene Aldana in 2023, is returning to challenge Kayla Harrison for the women's bantamweight title on the Nov. 14 Madison Square Garden card already headlined by Ciryl Gane's heavyweight title defense against Josh Hokit. The fight was originally booked for January before Harrison's neck surgery postponed it, and Nunes isn't promising this is a one-off: "You gotta defend the belt. And then we're going to see."

Source: Just Women's Sports

Crawford's camp is reportedly pushing for an equally large purse, leaving the fight's fate hinging on whether promoter Turki Alalshikh will fund it.

Jake Paul vows to retire if he loses to Cedric Doumbé

Ahead of his Dec. 24 Dubai bout against Doumbé — his first fight since getting stopped by Anthony Joshua last December — Paul says a loss would mean hanging up the gloves for good. Press conferences in Paris and Dubai are scheduled in the coming weeks to promote the fight.

Source: Reuters via KRRO

Golf

LIV Golf edges toward liquidation as its rescue deal collapses

BC Partners, which had committed in September to lead LIV Golf's post-bankruptcy relaunch, is reportedly pulling out after the league failed to lock down commitments from stars like Jon Rahm and Sergio Garcia. Liquidation proceedings on LIV's remaining assets are expected to begin this week in the New Jersey bankruptcy court overseeing the case.

Source: Bloomberg

McIlroy brushes off UAE safety concerns at Abu Dhabi

At the penultimate DP World Tour event of the season, McIlroy said he wouldn't feel unsafe despite some players reportedly skipping the event, telling reporters "I don't think I would ever feel unsafe." He also backed the tour's decision to let former LIV players return, as he chases a record-tying eighth Race to Dubai title.

Source: ESPN

Horse Racing

Keeneland November sale opens with a $5.2 million topper

Quietside sold for $5.2 million to top Book 1 of the ongoing Keeneland November breeding stock sale, a separate auction from the Fasig-Tipton November sale earlier this week. It's a strong opening to one of the sport's two marquee fall breeding-stock sales.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Santa Anita Derby winner Stronghold retires to Walmac Farm

Stronghold, who earned more than $1.35 million with nine graded-stakes placings across 16 career starts, has been retired and will begin his stud career at Walmac Farm in Kentucky in 2027. Owners Eric and Sharon Waller will have his stud fee announced at a later date.

Source: Thoroughbred Daily News

Breeders' Cup Mile winner Deterministic retires, stud fee set at $25,000

The turf standout, who turned the tables on reigning champion Notable Speech to win the Mile at Keeneland, earned more than $3 million across his career and will stand at Lane's End alongside sire Liam's Map for an introductory fee of $25,000.

Source: Thoroughbred Daily News
