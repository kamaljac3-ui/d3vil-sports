NFL

Case Keenum leads the Bears past the Eagles 27-7 on Monday night

With both Caleb Williams and Tyson Bagent unavailable, Chicago turned to 38-year-old veteran Case Keenum, who ran for a touchdown and threw two more in his first start since 2023 as the Bears blew out Philadelphia. It's a stunning result against an Eagles team that hadn't lost at Soldier Field in nearly 16 years.

Source: NBC Sports

Dolphins’ De’Von Achane out for the season with a torn ACL

Achane went down early in Sunday's loss to Kansas City, planting awkwardly on a cut before walking off under his own power, and is now done for the year just months after signing a four-year, $64 million extension. It's a brutal blow to Miami's offense after Achane racked up 1,350 rushing yards and 12 total touchdowns a season ago.

Source: NFL.com

Giants’ Brian Burns also done for the year with a torn ACL

Head coach John Harbaugh confirmed Burns tore his ACL late in Sunday's 12-7 win over Tennessee and will need season-ending surgery, ending the year for a pass rusher who finished second in the NFL with 16.5 sacks last season. It's a major setback for a Giants defense that was counting on him as its edge-rushing anchor.

Source: Giants.com

CFB

Iowa beats Michigan 20-19 on a last-second touchdown drive

The Hawkeyes marched 91 yards in 11 plays in the final 1:46 to steal a road win in Ann Arbor, handing Michigan its first loss of the season. It's the kind of gutty, down-to-the-wire finish that reshuffles the Big Ten picture heading into the heart of conference play.

Source: CBS Sports

Penn State blows a 17-0 lead, falls to Wisconsin in Matt Campbell's toughest loss yet

The Nittany Lions were outscored 24-3 over the final two-plus quarters in a 24-20 home loss, a result made sharper by the fact that former coach James Franklin never lost to Wisconsin in his own Penn State tenure. Franklin's new team, Virginia Tech, improved to 4-0 the same weekend with a come-from-behind win over Boston College.

Source: StateCollege.com

NBA

Jalen Duren holds out of Pistons training camp amid a contract standoff

Detroit opened camp without its All-Star center, who skipped media day and won't practice while unsigned; Duren has reportedly turned down a fully guaranteed five-year, $200 million offer in pursuit of something closer to the five-year, $287 million maximum he's eligible for. Coming off a breakout year (19.5 points, 10.5 rebounds, 65% shooting), Duren reportedly feels he belongs in a tier above recent Detroit extensions for the Thompson twins.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Nikola Jokic says he plans to sign his extension — just not yet

Speaking at Nuggets media day, Jokic said "I'm gonna sign next year," addressing his eligibility for a new deal without committing to a timeline. The three-time MVP's comment gives Denver fans some reassurance heading into a season where his long-term future has been a lingering storyline.

Source: NBC Sports

Donte DiVincenzo says he intends to play this season after his Achilles tear

The Timberwolves guard, who ruptured his right Achilles in the playoffs last spring, told reporters at media day he plans to return to the court at some point in 2026-27, and people within the organization believe he can make it back. His absence was deeply felt in Minnesota's playoff run, so any in-season return would be a significant boost.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAM

A third court grants a fresh group of players extra eligibility

An Indiana state judge issued a preliminary injunction covering roughly 90 plaintiffs — including Kansas guard Tre White — barring the NCAA from enforcing the rule that would have ended their careers after four seasons. It's the latest twist in a chaotic, multi-state legal fight over fifth-year eligibility that's already left rosters unsettled this month after a federal appeals court stayed a similar injunction and a Georgia court granted its own separate group of players relief.

Source: The Kansan

Source: WNBA.com

Lynx face elimination in Game 2 against the Liberty tonight

Minnesota, the league's No. 1 overall seed, must beat New York on its own floor tonight or be swept out of the playoffs in the first round after an upset Game 1 loss. It's a steep early test for a Lynx team that entered the postseason as the favorite.
