NFL

Giants trim eight players, cutting ties with longtime receiver Isaiah Hodgins

New York released eight players ahead of Sunday's roster deadline, most notably wideout Isaiah Hodgins, who leaves after three and a half seasons with 66 catches, 708 yards and 8 touchdowns. The Giants also parted with DL Leki Fotu, TE Josiah Deguara, LB Anfernee Orji, CB Ugo Amadi, OL Brenden Jaimes, RB Damon Bankston and a receiver they'd just signed days earlier, Kobe Prentice — several are expected to draw practice-squad interest.

Source: NBC Sports

Steelers move on from former first-round linebacker Jamin Davis

Pittsburgh waived Davis, the No. 19 overall pick in 2021, just months after he'd joined the club on a tryout to reunite with new defensive coordinator Patrick Graham. He'd flashed in the preseason opener with 2.5 sacks, but a crowded edge group led by T.J. Watt, Alex Highsmith, Nick Herbig and Jack Sawyer left no room on the final roster.

Source: Steelers Now

CFB

NAIA's Louisiana Christian pulls off the most improbable upset in the sport's history

Down 27-14 at halftime, the tiny NAIA program — fewer than 1,000 undergrads — outscored scholarship-funded, Division I Northwestern State 28-10 the rest of the way to win 42-37, becoming the first NAIA team ever to beat a D-I opponent that offers athletic scholarships. Quarterback Bryce Perkins' 52-yard touchdown strike to Omarius Davis sealed one of the sport's landmark opening-weekend results.

Source: KALB

North Carolina grinds out a defensive slugfest over TCU in Dublin

Billy Edwards Jr. threw for 232 yards and Aeron Burrell added two field goals as the Tar Heels held TCU scoreless for the final 49 minutes to escape with a 15-10 win at Dublin's Aviva Stadium in the Aer Lingus College Football Classic. It's a strong start to year two of Bill Belichick's tenure in Chapel Hill.

Source: UNC Athletics

No. 14 USC storms out to a rout of San Jose State behind Maiava

Jayden Maiava threw for 286 yards and accounted for three touchdowns as the Trojans built a 28-0 halftime lead and cruised to a 42-26 season-opening win, weathering a fourth-quarter push from the Spartans. It's Lincoln Riley's ninth straight win to open a season at USC.

Source: 920/921 The Ticket (AP)

NBA

Timberwolves ship out Josh Green to clear a path for signing Jonathan Kuminga

Minnesota sent guard Josh Green and cash to Utah for forward Cody Williams (the No. 10 pick in 2024) and John Konchar, moving Green's $14.7 million salary off the books so the Wolves can use the taxpayer mid-level exception to formally sign Kuminga. Minnesota plans to waive-and-stretch Konchar's contract to create further roster flexibility.

Source: NBA.com

Cavaliers stretch out Cam Whitmore after failing to find a trade partner

Cleveland waived and stretched Whitmore, the 20th pick in 2023 it had just acquired in the five-team Peyton Watson blockbuster, after the league's stretch-provision deadline passed without a taker. Spreading his $5.46 million salary over three years saves the Cavaliers roughly $3.6 million against the cap this season.

Source: Hoops Rumors

NCAAM

A second, separate eligibility fight leaves roughly 19 players in limbo again

Distinct from the SEC's pro-contract crackdown, the Tennessee Court of Appeals stayed a lower court's injunction in a case over whether 2022 high school graduates are owed a fifth season, again barring players like Vanderbilt's Jalen Washington, Indiana's Sam Alexis and North Carolina transfer Cameron Fens from 2026-27 competition unless the stay is overturned on appeal. It's a live complication for several rosters just as practice ramps up.

Source: Inside the Hall

WNBA

Han Xu's career night sends the Liberty into the FIBA break on a high

Off the bench, Han Xu scored a career-high 19 points on 6-of-8 shooting in just 17 minutes as New York beat Chicago 85-66 in its final game before the league pauses for the FIBA Women's World Cup. Breanna Stewart chipped in 13 points and 9 rebounds for the Liberty.

Source: OurSports Central

Mercury bury Tempo behind a season-high three-point outburst

Phoenix drilled a season-high 15 threes and led by as many as 47 points in a 101-69 blowout of Toronto, its most lopsided win of the year. Shay Ciezki led the Mercury with 15 points, with Marta Suarez and Maddy Westbeld adding 13 and 12.

Source: Yardbarker

The regular season goes dark for two and a half weeks starting tomorrow

The league pauses play from Aug. 31 through Sept. 17 for the FIBA Women's World Cup in Berlin, pulling several playoff-race regulars — including Caitlin Clark, Paige Bueckers, A'ja Wilson, Angel Reese, Breanna Stewart and Jackie Young — off the floor for Team USA and other national teams during the stretch run.

Source: Just Women's Sports

NCAAW

Dawn Staley and South Carolina to launch an HBCU-focused Thanksgiving showcase

The Gamecocks will host North Carolina Central, Alabama A&M and Tennessee State at Colonial Life Arena for the inaugural PowHER Player Classic over Thanksgiving weekend, paired with an NIL marketplace and a community-service day. Staley has featured an HBCU opponent in 15 of her 18 seasons in Columbia, and this builds that commitment into a standalone event.

Source: WIS-TV

MLB

Pete Crow-Armstrong's first career three-homer game boosts his MVP case

Crow-Armstrong went deep three times and drove in six as the Cubs routed the Reds 17-5 at Wrigley, pushing his season total to 36 home runs. He's the first Cubs player since 1974 to post at least four hits, three homers and six RBIs in a single game.

Source: CBS Sports

Wilyer Abreu's career-best power year continues in a Red Sox shutout of the Yankees

Abreu launched his 23rd home run of the season — a new career high — in the first inning at Yankee Stadium, and Boston's bullpen backed it up with a six-scoreless-inning outing from Hunter Bennett in a 6-0 win in Game 1 of Saturday's doubleheader. The win tightens Boston's grip on the AL wild-card race.

Source: Boston.com

Ohtani's return to the mound is scrapped again as knee soreness lingers

The Dodgers called off Shohei Ohtani's planned Sunday start against Detroit after a lighter-than-usual, 20-pitch bullpen session Friday left him still sore, more than seven weeks after his last pitching appearance. Manager Dave Roberts said flatly that Ohtani won't pitch Sunday or Tuesday, and it's unclear if he takes the mound again in 2026.

Source: MLB Trade Rumors

NHL

Hellebuyck's market takes shape, but no deal is close

Two days after his trade request went public, reported suitors for Connor Hellebuyck have thinned to Buffalo, New Jersey and a cap-strapped Vegas, with Florida and Philadelphia now considered out after addressing their own goaltending needs elsewhere. Winnipeg still hasn't signaled any urgency to move quickly on the three-time Vezina winner.

Source: Spector's Hockey

UFC

Song Yadong stuns hometown crowd's rival with a second-round KO of Umar Nurmagomedov

A near 5-1 underdog, Song caught Nurmagomedov with a short right hand as he shot for a takedown, ending the UFC Shanghai main event at 1:48 of round two after Nurmagomedov had dominated the wrestling exchanges in round one. Song immediately called for a bantamweight title shot, saying "I can finish everyone" in the division.

Source: ESPN

Denise Gomes' brutal elbow finish vaults her into the strawweight title picture

Gomes dropped former title challenger Yan Xiaonan with a lead elbow, then closed the show with a follow-up combination just before the five-minute mark of round one on the Shanghai co-main card. It's her fifth straight win — the division's longest active streak — and ties her with Jessica Andrade for the most knockouts in UFC strawweight history.

Source: LowKick MMA

Boxing

Filip Hrgović shocks unbeaten Moses Itauma to win the vacant IBF heavyweight title

Despite dropping Hrgović in round two, the 21-year-old Itauma faded badly after an exhausting seventh round spent chasing a finish, and Hrgović seized on the tank being empty to force a stoppage in round nine at London's O2 Arena. Itauma was reportedly taken from the arena for hospital treatment on a leg injury, with promoter Frank Warren saying only, "He's done something to his leg."

Source: CBS Sports

Chantelle Cameron edges Mikaela Mayer to unify three super-welterweight belts

Cameron won a close but unanimous decision (97-93, 97-93, 96-94) over Mayer in Birmingham to add the WBA, WBC and WBO titles to her collection, capping a fight week full of shoving matches between the two. Mayer left the ring furious, calling the scorecards a "robbery," and has since pushed for a rematch under three-minute rounds.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Caroline Dubois retains her lightweight titles, then squares off with Alycia Baumgardner

On the same Birmingham card, Dubois dropped and out-pointed Amelia Moore across the board to keep her belts, then had a heated face-off with Baumgardner afterward — an escalation of Friday's presser confrontation that now points toward a potential unification fight between the two.

Source: Most Valuable Promotions

Golf

Hovland takes a one-shot lead into the Tour Championship's final round

Viktor Hovland shot 66 Saturday to move to 15-under and edge ahead of Ryan Gerard by a shot entering Sunday's finale at East Lake, with Scottie Scheffler, Chris Gotterup, Adam Scott and Ludvig Åberg three back. Horse Racing

16-1 shot Leading Change runs down two Triple Crown winners to take the Travers

Making just his third career start, the Brad Cox-trained Leading Change ran down Preakness winner Napoleon Solo in the final strides to win the 157th Travers Stakes by a half-length under jockey Manuel Franco, with Kentucky Derby-Belmont winner Golden Tempo settling for third. It's one of the biggest upsets in the race's recent history, beating two of the year's classic winners on the same afternoon.

Source: DraftKings Network

Three more Grade 1s go in the book on a loaded Saratoga Saturday
