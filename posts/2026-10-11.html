October 11, 2026

No. 1 Texas falls in the Red River Rivalry, the Guardians strike out 21 to sweep into the ALCS, and the Cardinals move to trade Marvin Harrison Jr.

NFL

Cardinals finalizing a trade of Marvin Harrison Jr., with Houston the frontrunner

Arizona is finalizing a deal to move on from the 2024 first-round receiver, who was told he'd be inactive Sunday against Detroit while the trade gets done. The Cardinals are seeking a 2027 second-round pick or better, with the Texans — who'd reunite him with Ohio State teammate C.J. Stroud — seen as the likeliest landing spot over the Raiders, Chiefs, Eagles and Bills.

Source: ESPN

Ravens land center Cam Jurgens from the Eagles

Baltimore sent a 2028 second-round pick and a 2027 fifth-rounder to Philadelphia for Jurgens, getting back a 2027 seventh-rounder, after injuries wiped out the Ravens' top three centers and a day after Lane Johnson's retirement. GM Eric DeCosta called Jurgens "a young foundational piece along our offensive line," and the Eagles reportedly let Baltimore's doctors conduct his physical at their own facility to speed the deal through.

Source: ESPN

CFB

Oklahoma stuns No. 1 Texas on a last-second field goal in the Red River Rivalry

Unranked, 2-2 Oklahoma erased a 17-10 halftime deficit and beat previously-undefeated Texas 27-24 on Tate Sandell's 47-yard field goal as time expired, handing the Longhorns their first loss of the season. Arch Manning threw a pick-six that briefly put OU ahead before Texas tied it late, setting up the winning kick — Oklahoma's first Red River win since 2023.

Source: Yahoo Sports

No. 21 UCLA knocks off No. 13 Oregon in Eugene despite losing its starting QB

UCLA won 17-15 even after starter Nico Iamaleava left with an injury, as the defense stuffed a 2-point try following Oregon's late touchdown and then forced a fumble on the Ducks' final possession. Wayne Knight scored both a rushing and a receiving touchdown for UCLA, which improved to 5-0 while Oregon fell to 3-2.

Source: ESPN

Kentucky walks off No. 10 LSU in overtime for the second straight week

LSU's Sam Leavitt scored first in overtime to make it 34-27, but Kentucky answered with a touchdown and then won it on quarterback Kenny Minchey's 2-point conversion run, 35-34 — the same final score Kentucky used to beat South Carolina in overtime the week before. The Wildcats improved to 5-1 overall and 3-1 in the SEC; LSU dropped to 4-2.

Source: UK Athletics

NBA

Warriors lock up Brandin Podziemski on a 5-year, $110 million extension

The fully guaranteed deal, which includes a trade kicker and a mutual option for 2031-32, jumps Podziemski's salary from $5.6 million this season to $18.9 million the year after. It closes out one of Golden State's last extension priorities not long after Stephen Curry signed his own new deal; GM Mike Dunleavy said the team was "thrilled to get an extension done with Brandin."

Source: NBA.com

Kings center Maxime Raynaud carted off in a wheelchair with a knee injury

Raynaud hurt his knee setting a screen in the second quarter of Saturday's preseason loss to Golden State and had to be helped into a wheelchair and taken to the locker room after X-rays; he was seen leaving on crutches and getting an MRI that night. He'd had 10 points, 5 rebounds and a block in just 9 minutes before exiting, and Sacramento had no diagnosis to share after the game.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAM

Top 2027 wing Micah Ojo picks North Carolina over Duke

Ojo, a 6-foot-1 wing from Virginia Beach, committed to the Tar Heels over Duke and Vanderbilt — a notable early recruiting win as Michael Malone works to rebuild the UNC roster. It's the kind of program-building news that matters in a week with no games on the schedule.

Source: Yahoo Sports

UConn and Syracuse set for a rare Hall of Fame exhibition doubleheader

The programs meet at Mohegan Sun Arena this week — the women Monday, the men Tuesday — in just the second meeting between the schools' women's teams since Syracuse left the Big East in 2013, following their 2016 national-title clash. The men's side carries its own history, including a 6-overtime Big East Tournament classic back in 2009.

Source: WWLP

WNBA

WNBA sets the Finals schedule: Valkyries host Dream starting Oct. 17

Golden State, the No. 2 seed, opens the best-of-seven Finals at home against Atlanta on NBC and Peacock, with Game 3 the following Thursday and a possible Game 7 on Oct. 30. It's the expansion Valkyries' first trip to a championship round in just their second season.

Source: Yahoo Sports

NCAAW

Former Virginia star Heidi Burge Horton dies at 54

Horton, who with twin sister Heather helped build Virginia into a power alongside teammate and now-South Carolina coach Dawn Staley, died from glioblastoma after the brain cancer returned this summer. Staley posted that she loved Horton and hoped she was at peace; Horton later played in the WNBA and was the subject of Disney's "Double Teamed."

Source: Legacy.com

UConn tops the Big East women's preseason poll

The Huskies swept all 10 first-place votes, with Villanova and Creighton rounding out the top three, as reigning national Player of the Year Sarah Strong heads into the season as a projected top overall WNBA pick.

Source: Big East Conference

MLB

Guardians strike out 21 in a Game 5 shutout, sweep into the ALCS

Cleveland beat Chicago 2-0 at home to complete a rally from an 0-2 series deficit, with starter Gavin Williams striking out 12 in nearly six scoreless innings — tying a franchise postseason record — before the bullpen finished the job. The 21 combined strikeouts set an MLB record for a winner-take-all postseason game, and the Guardians now face Tampa Bay in the ALCS.

Source: Boston Globe

Brewers host Dodgers tonight in an NLCS rematch of last year's sweep

Milwaukee, which finished with baseball's best regular-season record at 103-59, sends rookie Jacob Misiorowski against Tarik Skubal as the Dodgers try to avoid a repeat of their four-game sweep of the Brewers in last year's NLCS. Yoshinobu Yamamoto is lined up to start Game 2 for Los Angeles.

Source: Fox Sports

MLB sends teams a lockout playbook as the Dec. 1 CBA deadline nears

With the current labor deal expiring at the end of Dec. 1, the league circulated a memo telling front offices to freeze 40-man roster moves and free-agent talks if a lockout begins. The core dispute remains a salary cap — MLB has floated a $245.3 million cap for 2027, which the union has rejected outright.

Source: MLB Trade Rumors

NHL

Rangers' Igor Shesterkin ruled out for the rest of the season

The knee injury Shesterkin suffered crashing into the post Friday against Washington will keep him out for all of 2026-27, New York confirmed Saturday. With backup Joonas Korpisalo also out for a month, Dylan Garand becomes the presumptive starter as the Rangers recall Spencer Martin from the AHL.

Source: NHL.com

William Nylander ties his career high with 5 points as the Maple Leafs rout Colorado

Nylander had two goals and a career-high three assists at Ball Arena, with Auston Matthews adding two goals and two assists of his own, in a 6-3 Toronto win.

Source: NHL.com

Panthers' Brad Marchand facing a 6-week absence after re-injuring his knee

Marchand crashed knee-first into the goalpost after being high-sticked in last week's win over the Kings, just as he was returning from offseason surgery that had already cost him the preseason and the first two games. Coach Paul Maurice set the six-week timeline — three weeks of rest, three of rehab — with captain Aleksander Barkov also out separately on a similar schedule.

Source: NHL.com

UFC

Brendan Allen chokes out Christian Leroy Duncan in Round 5 at UFC Vegas 122

Allen was behind on the scorecards after getting outstruck in the fourth round, but took Duncan down and finished him with a rear-naked choke at 2:46 of the fifth to headline Saturday's card at the UFC Apex. Earlier on the card, Andre Fili knocked out Kai Kamaka III with a head kick just 22 seconds into the second round.

Source: Yahoo Sports

PFL books Carmouche-Bishop rematch for its first women's flyweight title

Liz Carmouche defends against Jena Bishop in the main event of PFL Chicago on Oct. 16, a rematch of the pair's 2025 World Tournament final that Carmouche won, this time for the promotion's new Women's Flyweight World Championship.

Source: PFL

Boxing

Floyd Schofield wins the vacant WBA lightweight title; a flyweight title fight on the same card ends in upset

Schofield improved to 20-0 with a unanimous-decision win over Lucas Bahdi in Chicago, surviving a knockdown in the 11th round to take the belt Gervonta Davis left behind; promoter Oscar De La Hoya said he's now eyeing a path to "unify" the division. On the same card, Sergio Mendoza scored a fifth-round TKO of Ricardo Sandoval to take the vacant WBA and WBC flyweight titles.

Source: Ring Magazine

David Benavidez vacates his WBC light heavyweight title

Benavidez will stay at cruiserweight, where he's expected to face Michal Cieslak for the vacant WBC title there in December. The WBC has upgraded Joshua Buatsi's planned rematch with Willy Hutchinson into a bout for Benavidez's now-vacant 175-pound belt on Nov. 7 in Manchester.

Source: Ring Magazine

Golf

Michael Brennan wins the Baycurrent Classic for his third PGA Tour title

Brennan closed with a 68 in Japan to finish 16-under, one shot clear of Keith Mitchell and Jacob Bridgeman, picking up his third career win in just 33 starts and a $1.44 million payday. Defending champion Xander Schauffele finished three shots back in a tie for fourth.

Source: PGA Tour

19-year-old Blades Brown chases a PGA Tour card in today's Korn Ferry finale

Brown takes a one-shot lead over Austin Hitt into Sunday's final round of the Korn Ferry Tour Championship in Virginia, with a win putting him in position to become the second-youngest winner in tour history. The round also finalizes the top 20 players who earn 2027 PGA Tour cards and decides the season's points title between Sandy Scott and Ross Steelman.

Source: PGA Tour

Horse Racing

Tam Tam wins the QEII Challenge Cup at Keeneland, denying favorite Luster

Tam Tam led gate-to-wire and held off Faithful Departed by 2½ lengths to take the $750,000 Grade 1 turf race, her second straight Grade 1 win after August's Del Mar Oaks. Pre-race favorite Luster, the field's marquee name, failed to win.

Source: Horse Racing Nation

Lambeth breaks through for his first graded win in the Sycamore Stakes

Lambeth, who only got into the field after a late scratch, won the Grade 2 turf race by a length at Keeneland, and trainer Joe Sharp said he won't rule out a start in the Breeders' Cup Turf on the same track Oct. 31.

Source: BloodHorse
