NFL

Micah Parsons starts the season on the shelf, eyeing a Week 6 return against his old team

Green Bay officially placed star pass rusher Micah Parsons on the Reserve/PUP list, ruling him out for at least the first four games as he continues rehabbing the torn ACL and meniscus repair from last December. He's eligible to return as early as Week 6 — fittingly, a home date against the Cowboys, the team that traded him to the Packers this offseason.

Commanders lose Pro Bowl tackle Laremy Tunsil to a torn triceps

Washington placed left tackle Laremy Tunsil on injured reserve after he tore a triceps in practice and will need surgery, guaranteeing he misses at least the first four games. Brandon Coleman shifts into the starting left tackle spot while the Commanders re-signed veterans Fabian Moreau and Shy Tuttle for depth.

Source: ESPN

Tank Dell's long road back hits another detour

Texans wideout Tank Dell landed on injured reserve during final roster cuts, sidelining him at least into Week 5 as he keeps working back from the dislocated kneecap and torn ACL, MCL and LCL he suffered in a Week 16, 2024 game. It's a fresh reminder of how long Houston's offense has been without one of its top young targets.

Source: ESPN

Waiver Monday: Dolphins load up on depth, Jets hand the backup job to a rookie

Miami was the league's most active team on waivers, claiming seven players including quarterback Brady Cook, while the Raiders used the No. 1 waiver priority to grab cornerback Darrell Luter Jr. off San Francisco's cuts. In New York, the Jets confirmed rookie Cade Klubnik beat out veteran Bailey Zappe for the backup quarterback job behind Geno Smith.

"Going into the regular season" is where coach Aaron Glenn says the Jets are comfortable with the rookie as QB2.

CFB

North Dakota State wins its first-ever game as an FBS program

In its long-awaited jump from FCS to FBS, North Dakota State throttled Jacksonville State 33-7, piling up 373 total yards and scoring on its opening possession. It's an emphatic debut for a Bison program that won nine FCS national titles before making the move up a level.

Source: NDSU Athletics

Deion Sanders and Colorado face an early referendum at Georgia Tech

Colorado opens its season Thursday at Georgia Tech under real pressure after last year's 3-9 finish, with returning quarterback Julian Lewis leading an offense that needs to show fast improvement. Georgia Tech counters with well-traveled running back Justice Haynes, a transfer by way of Alabama and Michigan.

Sanders says his staff's communication heading into the opener has been "second to none."

Source: SI.com

NBA

Wembanyama picks up where last season left off in a rout of Sweden

Playing for France in FIBA World Cup qualifying, Victor Wembanyama scored 18 points on 6-of-7 shooting with three blocks in an 84-69 win over Sweden in front of a record Stockholm crowd. It's a strong tune-up before he reports back to Spurs training camp later this month.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Nurkic delivers a monster game for Bosnia in the same qualifying window

Jazz center Jusuf Nurkic put up 25 points and 14 rebounds to power Bosnia and Herzegovina to a crucial FIBA World Cup qualifying win, one of several standout showings from NBA players suiting up for their national teams this week. Canada's Luguentz Dort and Nickeil Alexander-Walker also starred in a win over Argentina.

Source: NBA.com

NCAAM

Mark Mitchell's nine-day flirtation with Kentucky ends back at Missouri

Missouri forward Mark Mitchell reversed his decision to transfer to Kentucky and will instead re-enroll at Missouri, his agent confirmed, just over a week after he'd committed to the Wildcats. The SEC's stance against intraconference transfers for players chasing a fifth year of eligibility left his path to actually playing at Kentucky messier than simply returning to Columbia.

Source: ESPN

Duke sells ad space on Coach K Court for the first time ever

Duke announced a four-year deal putting an Edward Jones logo on both sides of halfcourt at Cameron Indoor Stadium, the first sponsor branding the historic floor has ever carried. Mike Krzyzewski was consulted on the design, and the partnership — also covering North Carolina and Oregon — includes financial-education programming for Duke athletes.

Source: ESPN

WNBA

A'ja Wilson and Kelsey Plum both pull out of the FIBA World Cup

Team USA confirmed four-time MVP A'ja Wilson and Kelsey Plum won't play in the FIBA Women's World Cup in Berlin, with Plum already ruled out for the rest of the WNBA season by a lower-leg injury and Wilson's absence described as health and workload management. Mystics teammates Kiki Iriafen and Sonia Citron were added to the roster and will make their senior national-team debuts.

Plum wrote that she "did everything I could to be back on the court, but just ran out of time."

Source: ESPN

Aces sign veteran center Kalani Brown to replace the injured NaLyssa Smith

The defending-champion Aces added six-year veteran Kalani Brown after NaLyssa Smith went down with a season-ending leg injury. GM Nikki Fargas pointed to Brown's rebounding and interior defense as exactly what the roster needed down the stretch.

Fargas said Brown "brings an extremely diverse defensive skillset" and a "strong paint presence."

Source: Las Vegas Review-Journal

NCAAW

South Carolina locks in a UConn rematch for late November

Dawn Staley finalized South Carolina's 2026-27 non-conference slate, headlined by a Nov. 24 meeting with UConn at Mohegan Sun — the first rematch since the Gamecocks beat the Huskies in last season's Final Four. The schedule also includes games against Maryland (in Paris), North Carolina, USC and Notre Dame.

Source: Gamecocks Online

MLB

Cubs blast a franchise-record nine homers, cap the best home-run month in NL history

Chicago hit nine home runs — three from Alex Bregman — in a 17-3 rout of Milwaukee, setting a new franchise single-game record and closing out a 62-homer August that broke the National League's single-month record. Tyrone Taylor, Nico Hoerner, Seiya Suzuki, Carson Kelly, Matt Shaw and Michael Conforto also went deep, with Clay Holmes tossing six scoreless innings.

Bregman said it's a night he'll "definitely remember."

Source: MLB.com

Padres call up catching phenom Ethan Salas as rosters expand

San Diego is promoting 20-year-old catcher Ethan Salas as part of the league-wide Sept. 1 roster expansion, capping a rapid rise from Double-A this season. If he gets behind the plate, he'll be the youngest catcher to appear in a big-league game since Iván Rodríguez debuted in 1991.

Source: MLB Trade Rumors

Golf

Scheffler's FedEx Cup win vaults him past Tiger Woods on the career money list

Scottie Scheffler's $10 million Tour Championship payday pushed his career PGA Tour earnings past $130 million, overtaking Tiger Woods' mark that had stood atop the list since February 2000. It took Scheffler barely five seasons to go from winless on Tour to the sport's all-time money leader.
