NFL

Giants hand the offense back to J.J. McCarthy for the trip to Houston

Coach John Harbaugh confirmed McCarthy, not Jameis Winston, will start at quarterback in Week 7 after relieving Winston in last week's loss to New Orleans and throwing for 243 yards and a touchdown. It's McCarthy's first start since he was traded in from Minnesota, and it comes with the Giants still sitting atop the NFC East at 3-2-1 despite the shuffle at the position.

Source: CBS Sports

Titans release defensive tackle who grabbed a gun from his car during a locker-room dispute

Tennessee cut Jordan Elliott after he clashed with a teammate at practice and walked to the parking garage to retrieve a firearm from his vehicle, which reportedly never left the car before a teammate talked him down. Coach Robert Saleh called it "a terrible lapse in judgment" rather than a criminal act, but said the team's no-guns-on-property policy left no room for exceptions.

Source: CBS Sports

Josh Jacobs speaks for the first time since the arrest that cost him six games

Back at Packers practice off the commissioner's exempt list, Jacobs said he's "remorseful" and apologized to teammates for the fallout from his May arrest, to which he pleaded no contest on two misdemeanor counts. The NFL's own investigation remains open and could still bring further discipline, and his availability for Sunday against Detroit hasn't been confirmed.

Source: ESPN

CFB

Miami vaults to No. 1 for the first time since 2002 as the rankings get turned upside down

The Hurricanes' double-overtime win over rival Florida State catapulted them to the top of the AP poll in a week that saw then-No. 1 Georgia upset at home by unranked Auburn and then-No. 2 Notre Dame fall on the road to BYU. Miami, now 6-0, edged out BYU for the top spot, with Ohio State rounding out the new top three.

Source: CBS Sports

Maryland fires Mike Locksley after dropping to 2-5 and a 12th straight Big Ten loss

The Terrapins moved on from Locksley following a 37-30 homecoming loss to Rutgers, calling it a mutual parting after eight seasons and the program's first Power Four coaching change of the year. Defensive coordinator Aazaar Abdul-Rahim takes over on an interim basis starting Oct. 31 against Illinois, with Maryland reportedly owing Locksley a buyout north of $9 million.

Source: CBS Sports

Auburn dismisses All-SEC linebacker Xavier Atkins days after a mystery suspension

Atkins, who led the SEC in tackles for loss last season, was suspended ahead of Auburn's upset of then-No. 1 Georgia and has now been dismissed outright with no official reason given by the school. He says he plans to enter the transfer portal after racking up 37 tackles and three sacks through four starts this year.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Cooper Flagg sets a teenage scoring record in his NBA debut

The Mavericks rookie poured in 32 points on 10-of-17 shooting with 5 rebounds, 3 steals and a block in his first career game — the most points by a teenager in a season opener in NBA history. Dallas still lost 128-110 at Houston after 21 turnovers fed the Rockets' fast break, and Houston center Alperen Şengün appeared to tweak his back drawing a charge on Flagg in the third quarter.

Source: NBC Sports

Anthony Edwards drops 45 to spoil Giannis Antetokounmpo's Heat debut

Edwards shot 16-of-29 with 10 rebounds as Minnesota blew out Miami 131-106 in Giannis's first game in a Heat uniform, with Giannis managing a team-high 35 points in the loss as Miami shot just 8-of-33 from three. Edwards became only the second player in NBA history, after Wilt Chamberlain, to score 40-plus in consecutive season openers.

Source: Field Level Media

Ty Jerome erupts for a career-high 45 as Memphis's new starting point guard

Stepping into the role vacated by Ja Morant's offseason trade to Portland, Jerome shot 17-of-25 for 45 points with 7 rebounds and 6 assists in a 123-103 season-opening rout of Utah, breaking the Grizzlies' franchise record for points in a season opener that Morant himself had set in 2020. Jazz rookie Cam Boozer made his NBA debut in the loss.

Source: AP via WRAL

NCAAM

Louisville snaps Kansas's 32-year home exhibition win streak

No. 11 Louisville beat No. 17 Kansas 89-83 at Allen Fieldhouse, ending a Jayhawks exhibition winning streak dating back to 1994 after Kansas clawed within two points in the final two minutes. Tre White led Kansas with 25 points on four 3-pointers, while Louisville's balanced scoring held up down the stretch.

Source: Kansas Athletics

Florida opens No. 1 in the preseason AP poll for the first time since 2006

The defending-champion Gators received 47 of 59 first-place votes to debut atop the rankings, ahead of Duke and Illinois, with UConn and Arizona rounding out the top five. Reigning champion Michigan dropped to No. 16 after losing coach Dusty May in the offseason, and North Carolina was left out of the poll entirely.

Source: ESPN

WNBA

Dream blow out Valkyries in Game 3, take a 2-1 Finals lead

Atlanta routed Golden State 97-71 at a sold-out State Farm Arena behind Angel Reese's 21 points and 12 rebounds, putting the Dream two wins from the franchise's first championship. The expansion Valkyries, who won Game 1 behind Gabby Williams, are trying to become the first expansion team to win a title in its second season; Game 4 is Saturday back in Atlanta.

Source: ESPN

Fever's Aliyah Boston has knee surgery after playing through it all season

Boston underwent arthroscopic surgery to repair a lateral meniscus tear that first flared up in February, days after Indiana's playoff loss to Las Vegas ended her season. She still posted a career-high 16.1 points per game and a fourth straight All-Star selection despite the injury limiting her to a career-low 27.1 minutes per game.

Source: Yahoo Sports

Fennelly announced this season will be his last, closing out a tenure with 656 wins at Iowa State, 822 overall, and 24 NCAA tournament appearances including two Elite Eights. He said he wants to give the school's incoming athletic director a clear runway to plan the program's future.

Columbia suspends longtime coach Megan Griffith for the season's first three games
