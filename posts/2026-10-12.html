NFL

Jaguars stun Eagles in London on a 57-yard walk-off field goal

Philadelphia thought it had won it when Jake Elliott's field goal put the Eagles up 17-16 with eight seconds left, but Ameer Abdullah's long kickoff return set up Cam Little's game-winning kick as time expired. Jacksonville improved to 4-1 while the Eagles fell to 2-3 and have now lost three straight; Jalen Hurts said afterward, "I cost us that game."

Source: CNN

Commanders and Giants play to the NFL's first tie of the season, 27-27

Washington led 27-13 in the fourth quarter before Jameis Winston rallied New York, capping the comeback with a touchdown pass to Malik Nabers. In overtime, the Commanders forced a fumble but couldn't get off the field in time to try a winning kick, and Jayden Daniels was tackled as the clock ran out. The Giants improved to 3-1-1 and now lead the NFC East; Washington fell to 1-3-1.

Source: ESPN

Ravens rally past the Falcons in overtime with Lamar Jackson sidelined

Backup Tyler Huntley threw a tying touchdown with 40 seconds left in regulation, Derrick Henry ran in the two-point conversion, and Huntley then capped a seven-minute overtime drive with a sneak for the win, 30-23. Kyle Hamilton sealed it with an interception of Tua Tagovailoa, who started for Atlanta in place of injured Michael Penix Jr., and Baltimore moved into sole possession of first place in the AFC North.

Source: ESPN

CFB

Georgia wins at Alabama for the first time since 2007

The Bulldogs beat the Crimson Tide 20-17 in Tuscaloosa as Gunner Stockton threw just one incompletion outside of an interception and Dwight Phillips Jr. ran for 120 yards. Alabama's final chance died when a potential pass-interference flag never came on a fourth-quarter end-zone target; Tide coach Kalen DeBoer said afterward, "we can't put the game in that situation where the officials come into play." Georgia moved to 6-0 and is poised to take over the No. 1 ranking.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution

LaNorris Sellers accounts for 5 touchdowns as South Carolina upsets No. 16 Florida in the Swamp

Sellers threw for four touchdowns and ran for another, completing 23 of 35 passes for 279 yards with no interceptions, as the Gamecocks routed Florida 38-19 to snap an eight-game SEC road losing streak. The win eased the pressure on coach Shane Beamer after a three-game skid, while Florida turned the ball over three times.

Source: WIS-TV

No. 7 Indiana erases an 11-point deficit to beat Nebraska, extends win streak to 22

Down 17-6 early in the fourth quarter in Lincoln, Indiana got the go-ahead score on a 44-yard touchdown flip from Josh Hoover to a wide-open Nick Marsh on fourth-and-1, then held on for a 20-17 win. It's the Hoosiers' nation-leading 22nd straight victory and their first game trailing since last year's Big Ten title game; Nebraska coach Matt Rhule has now lost 21 straight to ranked opponents.

Source: ESPN

NBA

76ers' Ariel Hukporti out for the season with a torn Achilles

Hukporti ruptured his right Achilles less than five minutes into Philadelphia's preseason opener against the Knicks and had surgery days later; the team confirmed he'll miss all of 2026-27. The injury leaves the Sixers thin at center behind an already-injured Adem Bona, just months after Hukporti signed a one-year, $3.4 million deal with Philadelphia.

Source: The Philadelphia Inquirer

Kyrie Irving scores 17 in his first game back since tearing his ACL

Irving needed just 22 minutes to score 17 points on 6-of-13 shooting as the Mavericks beat the Rockets 132-115 in the second game of the NBA China Games in Macao — his first action since tearing his left ACL in March 2025. Coach Jason Kidd said he didn't expect Irving to look that sharp after the long layoff, which was extended by a calf strain last December.

Source: NBA.com
