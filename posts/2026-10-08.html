NFL

49ers owner Jed York suspended six games, fined $500,000

The league suspended principal owner Jed York for violating the personal conduct policy following his August arrest in Ohio on a prostitution-related charge, later reduced to disorderly conduct in a no-contest plea. York gets credit for three games already spent away from the team during the investigation and is eligible to return Oct. 20; he called it "an inexcusable mistake" and said he takes full responsibility.

Source: CNN

Eagles send Pro Bowl center Cam Jurgens to Baltimore

Philadelphia traded Jurgens to the Ravens on Wednesday for a 2027 fifth-round pick and a 2028 second-rounder, sending back a 2027 seventh-rounder — a day after Lane Johnson's retirement announcement. Baltimore needed a center after losing three to injured reserve; Jurgens is in concussion protocol and expected to debut next week against Cleveland, with GM Howie Roseman calling it a "potential win-win" rather than a sign the Eagles are selling on their season.

Source: NBC Sports Philadelphia

Joey Porter Jr. makes his Cowboys debut after Steelers trade

Pittsburgh dealt the cornerback to Dallas for a 2027 sixth-round pick and a 2028 second-rounder after a contract standoff — Porter wanted long-term security over a front-loaded one-year deal — and he already saw his first 2026 snaps in a Cowboys uniform. It's the third Steelers-Cowboys trade in roughly two years, following last year's George Pickens deal.

Source: ESPN

NBA

Lillard, back from his Achilles tear, added 15 points as the two flashed early backcourt chemistry alongside some defensive questions.

Tyrese Haliburton returns after 472 days, fittingly at his college arena

Haliburton made his first live appearance since tearing his Achilles in Game 7 of the 2025 Finals, playing 16 minutes with 7 points, 6 assists, a steal and two blocks in a 123-112 Pacers win over Minnesota staged at Iowa State's arena, where he played college ball. "Really fun, really fun," Haliburton said of finally feeling like a healthy NBA player again.

NCAAM

Dana Altman retires as Oregon's coach, effective immediately

Altman stepped down Wednesday, less than a month before tipoff, ending a 16-year run that included Oregon's 2017 Final Four trip and 382 wins, the most in program history. Longtime assistant Tony Stubblefield takes over as interim coach while the school runs a search during the season; Altman, 68, cited not having the energy to coach to his own standard along with health factors he described as "nothing serious."

Source: CBS Sports

Kansas State, Jerome Tang settle for $12 million

Kansas State and fired coach Jerome Tang reached a $12 million settlement, payable through 2027, closing out the dispute from his February "for cause" firing after he publicly criticized his own team. Athletic director Gene Taylor said the deal lets "both parties move forward on agreed terms"; Tang has since joined Baylor as an associate head coach.

Source: ESPN

Breanna Stewart scored 25 first-half points for New York but was held to just 10 the rest of the way; Atlanta is now one win from its first Finals trip in 13 years, with Game 3 Friday in New York.

Valkyries survive an Aces rally to take a 2-0 semifinal lead as Loyd is ruled out

Aces star Jewell Loyd, hurt in Game 1, was downgraded to ruled out for Game 2 with a left-leg injury, and the two-time defending champions now face elimination in Game 3 Friday at home.
