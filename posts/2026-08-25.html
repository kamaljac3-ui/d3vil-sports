NFL

49ers and Saints swap running back Zamir White for linebacker Deion Jones

San Francisco sent newly signed running back Zamir White to New Orleans and got back linebacker Deion Jones, who spent years playing under current 49ers defensive coordinator Raheem Morris in Atlanta. The trade fills a Saints backfield thinned by Alvin Kamara's MCL sprain and further injuries to Audric Estime and Devin Neal, while giving San Francisco linebacker depth after Jalen Graham went down hurt.

Source: ESPN

Patriots trade receiver Kayshon Boutte to the Texans

Brown and free-agent addition Romeo Doubs. It gives the Texans fresh receiver depth just days after Jayden Higgins' season-ending ACL tear.

Source: NBC Sports Boston

Allen Robinson II retires on his 33rd birthday

The two-time 1,000-yard receiver announced his retirement Monday, closing out an 11-year run with five teams after Jacksonville drafted him in the second round back in 2014. Robinson finishes his career with 565 catches, 7,058 yards and 43 touchdowns, plus a Pro Bowl nod from his breakout second season.

CFB

Cut by the Browns, Dae'Quan Wright plots an unprecedented return to college ball at LSU

The former Ole Miss tight end plans to join Lane Kiffin's LSU roster once he clears waivers from Cleveland, using a court-ordered eligibility path that could make him the first player ever to leave the NFL and go back to college football. He's one of several former Kiffin players — including veterans recently let go by the Saints and Cardinals — chasing a return to campus under the same nationwide eligibility litigation, with a preliminary injunction hearing set for Aug. 31 in Baton Rouge.

Source: The Washington Times

Florida hands the starting job to Georgia Tech transfer Aaron Philo

First-year coach Jon Sumrall named Philo the Gators' Week 1 starter over redshirt freshman Tramell Jones Jr., settling a battle that ran through fall camp under new offensive coordinator Buster Faulkner. Philo, who threw for 938 yards over two seasons at Georgia Tech, will start Florida's Sept. 5 opener against Florida Atlantic.

Source: OnlyGators

He was coming off a season limited to 50 games by a stress fracture and had averaged 20.8 points a year ago, so the injury removes a key scoring piece from Portland for most of 2026-27.

Source: ESPN

NCAAM

Eligibility chaos returns as a court stay leaves Florida's Denzel Aberdeen in limbo again

A divided 10th Circuit panel granted the NCAA a stay of the nationwide injunction that had briefly restored a fifth season to every Class of 2022 senior, pulling Florida guard Denzel Aberdeen's eligibility back into question since he never secured his own individual restraining order. Athletic director Scott Stricklin and coach Todd Golden say they still expect Aberdeen to play, but more state-by-state lawsuits are expected as schools scramble to protect similarly affected players.

Source: The Independent Florida Alligator

WNBA

The win tied the Wings' single-season franchise record for victories as they push for a playoff spot down the stretch.

Scheffler enters as the betting favorite to close out the season with the $10 million top prize.
