### Two picks on the same player

The bot often bets a player in two markets (rec + rec yds, almost always the same side). Season, FanDuel-priced:
**single-market picks 62-45 (57.9%, +5.50u); two-or-more on one player 23-46 (33.3%, −26.75u)**: 36 players, 8 all won,
19 all lost, 9 split. It held in weeks ≤7 (2-6), 8–9 (17-19) and 10 (4-21).

Out-of-sample check in the backtest (edge calls vs the recent average): no difference. 2025 wk 4–18: single 67.3% of
1,253 vs multi 67.3% of 1,585; 2026 wk 4–10: 63.4% of 558 vs 65.9% of 639. So it's not that the model projects these
players worse. Likelier: when the model disagrees with FanDuel on several of one player's markets, FanDuel knows
something about his role the model doesn't, and the paired bets double the hit. Only 36 independent players: **watch**.
