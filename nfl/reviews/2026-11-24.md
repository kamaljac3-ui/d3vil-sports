### New: game-day picks use old prices

`gameday()` in `nfl-props/run.js` (line 241) reads stored lines. For 1 PM games those are Friday 5 PM prices; for later Sunday
games, the 11:45 AM parlay pull. The run has already downloaded the current prices for the whole slate (to find game ids) and
ignores them. Two results: (1) the price in the alert may not be on FanDuel any more; (2) games outside Friday's pull (6 games
priced in week 11) get no line-priced picks: all 36 picks came from exactly those 6 games. So live records are on quoted prices,
not necessarily bettable ones. It can't be measured backwards (old prices aren't kept). Later Sunday games did no better
(36-47 vs 48-50 for 1 PM, all weeks with ctx), so it is unlikely to rescue the model.
