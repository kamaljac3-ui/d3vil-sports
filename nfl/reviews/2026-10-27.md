## What went right

- Delivery: 7/7 and 14/14 for the second week running, all landing ~80 min out.

  −11.6 to −6.8 after the Oct 24 baseline change.
- Probabilities are calibrated on both sides (P(over) 0.30–0.42 → 34.7% overs, 0.58–0.70 → 64.8%), so the under lean
  is the skewed distribution, not overconfidence.
- Turning the lines ✅ off was right: the 2026 backtest is now 14-23-2 on spreads the model liked by 3+.

## What went wrong (with evidence)

1. **Game-day props priced at books he can't bet.** 7 of 16 week-7 model picks show BetOnline or Pinnacle, including
   all 7 in Sunday's game-day alerts, sent after the fix. Cause: game day reuses Friday's stored lines (`odds.storedLines`, valid 4 days), and
   Friday's were saved Oct 23, before the your-book fix (0f42445, Oct 25 03:08 UTC). Those old files have no
   `atMyBook` field, and `evaluate()` in `nfl-props/run.js` only rejects `atMyBook===false`. It should clear itself
   when Friday Oct 30 re-pulls, but the check is still loose.
2. **Repeated picks counted twice.** A pick in both the Friday and the game-day alert is graded twice (Jennings,
   Jefferson, Downs, Bam Knight; Pierre Strong void). Season record is inflated from 29-25 to 35-28.
3. **The season profit sits on unbettable prices.** FanDuel-priced picks are 13-14 (−2.76u); the +3.54u from
   Pinnacle-priced picks isn't money he could have made.
4. **Review cron didn't fire** on Oct 27 (GitHub skipped both scheduled runs). Started by hand; watch next Tuesday.

## Proposals (waiting on Kamal)

1. **Fix: only price model picks at your book (bug).** `nfl-props/run.js` `evaluate()`: change
   `lines[stat].atMyBook!==false` to `lines[stat].atMyBook===true`, so a stored line without the field is treated as

   BetOnline/Pinnacle. Bug fix: test is a dry run with an old-format stored line showing no non-FanDuel price. Model
   numbers unchanged, no version bump.

   the deduped numbers above. No bot behavior changes.
3. **Turn off the wind-direction multiplier in the props model (the rule).** Repo variables / `nfl-props/model.js`
   defaults: `DIR_CROSS=1`, `DIR_ALONG=1`. Keep the crosswind wording in the ⚠️ wind tag. Evidence: the multiplier
   came from a game-total research script and was never backtested in the props model; the backtest's weather has no
   direction, so out of sample (2025 and 2026) it changes no backtest number: it neither helps nor hurts, which per
   the rule means it doesn't belong in the model. Bump `props` in `VERSIONS`. Can come back if an ERA5-direction
   backtest supports it.
