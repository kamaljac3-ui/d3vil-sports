## 3. What went wrong

- **Process bug (CFB, this week's main finding):** cfb-lines' 15-min cron is reliably skipped by GitHub into
  ~4 actual runs/day, leaving 9-12h daytime gaps that blow through the bot's own 170-min game-day window. Likely
  cost: missed or very-late game-day cards for any game kicking off in the 9am-6pm ET range on a day without a
  lucky cron fire. No sign this corrupted any grading (picks that did go out logged and graded fine) — this is a
  delivery gap, not a scoring bug. **Fixed in PR #2, see section 5.**
- **NBA alert timing (model miss, not a bug):** picks sent under 2h before tip lose units and show 0% CLV (0/18)
  vs picks sent 2h+ out losing nothing and beating the close 55% of the time (11/20) — both season samples now
  over 100 picks (196/184), and the gap repeats this week alone (70 vs 75 picks, −3.4u vs +10.5u). This matches
  `nba-edges/LEARNINGS.md` item 4's prediction exactly: late alerts carry fresher injury news but hit sharper
  lines, and right now the sharper-line effect is winning. New lesson row L7 — not yet a fix candidate (the late
  window exists specifically to catch injury news), but worth watching for a possible "size down or skip the
  late alert when CLV is this one-sided" rule once it's backtestable.
- **Teammate-out tag (NBA):** flipped from a bad week last review (35.7%, −13.4u) to a good one this week (55.6%,
  +3.3u); the season total landed almost exactly at a coin flip (50.4%, 278 picks) with CLV also roughly even
  (7 for / 6 against of 29). Reads as **variance**, not a model miss or a bug — exactly what "no clear edge either
  way yet" should look like at this sample size. L5 updated, still watching.
- **💰 EV picks still mislabeling their model version:** 3 more single-pick float-keyed "model" buckets appeared
  in this week's `SUMMARY.md` (same bug as L4). Confirms PR #1 (open, unmerged) is still needed; nothing new to do
  here beyond waiting on a merge.
- **DvP data finally flowing:** `dvp` is non-null on 111 season picks now (was 0 two weeks ago, once teams cleared
  the 10-game minimum). Bottom-half vs top-half defenses show no real gap yet (props 13-11 vs 15-12, boosts 58%
  vs 58% beat-average, "said" tracks "got" closely both ways) — consistent with the backtest's "no edge" call on
  DvP, not a contradiction. Sample (~50/side) is still half the 100+ bar from `nba-edges/LEARNINGS.md` item 2.
- **No oddities found** in a skim of this week's alerts: no duplicate picks, no sends on already-ruled-out
  players beyond the normal (and expected) void rate, no empty-section or ntfy-failure signs in the ledgers.
