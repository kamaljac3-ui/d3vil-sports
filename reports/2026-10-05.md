## 3. Suggested improvement

**Add a `modelVersion` tag to every pick a bot logs** (`.cache/picks-<date>.json`, which flows into `mlb.json`/`nba.json`/`SUMMARY.md`). Right now nothing in `launch-angle/`, `mlb-live/`, or `nba-edges/` records which model version produced a pick. That's fine while every number is still small-sample noise, but the first time a tuning change lands (the weather multiplier, a threshold, anything per lesson 5), the ledger has no way to split "before" from "after" — it'll just blend into one all-time figure that's already underpowered.

**Effort:** small. One constant per bot, threaded into the object already being appended to `.cache/picks-<date>.json`; no change to model logic, thresholds, or `results/track.js`'s grading.

**How Kamal would know it worked:** the next time a model change ships, a query against `mlb.json`/`nba.json` (or a future weekly report) can filter by version and show two distinct cohorts instead of one blurred history — at that point sample size, not tagging, becomes the only limiter.
