## 3. One suggested improvement

**Add a model-version tag to every logged pick** (`launch-angle/run.js:logPicks` and the equivalent in the other bots). Right now a logged pick has `sport/date/mode/at` plus the score fields but nothing identifying which model/threshold version produced it. Concretely, `launch-angle`'s park/weather multiplier already exists in `env.js` but isn't wired into the edge rule yet per CLAUDE.md ("Not in the edge rule") — when that or any other model change lands, the ledger will silently mix pre- and post-change results with no way to split them apart. Effort: small (~30 min per bot — add one field to the object being logged, no schema change to the ledger itself). You'd know it worked when a future model change shows up in `SUMMARY.md`/`mlb.json` as a version boundary you can filter on, instead of being invisible in the aggregate ratio.

(No threshold changes recommended — every bot's sample this week is far under the ~100-pick bar for that.)
