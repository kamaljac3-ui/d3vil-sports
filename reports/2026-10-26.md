## 3. What went wrong

**NBA props (96 picks, all pre-tag/pre-model-version):**
- By stat: ast 71.4% (n21), reb 60.0% (n15), fg3m 56.2% (n16), **pts only 52.3% (n44)** — points props are a coin flip and carry half the volume.
- By side: overs 62.1% (n66) clearly beat unders 50.0% (n30) — the model's real edge, such as it is, is on the over side.
- By confidence: the 0.70-0.80 `po` bucket (63.6%, n33) beat the 0.80+ bucket (55.0%, n60) — the model's most "confident" picks aren't its safest, echoing the calibration note already in `nba-edges/LEARNINGS.md`.
- **Minutes (LEARNINGS.md item 5):** 27 of 96 props (28%) had the player play 8+ minutes under his logged `mpg`; those went 10-17 (37%) vs 58.3% overall — a real-looking gap, still small. Six of the 27 landed on one slate (10/21, likely several blowouts). Four landed on **Deandre Ayton alone**, 14-16 min on a ~26 mpg average across three straight games (10/23-10/24) — that one reads as a genuine role/minutes cut the model hasn't caught up to (model miss), not blowout noise.
- **Closing-line value is empty.** 0 of 96 pregame props carry a `close` value, despite `ODDS_API_KEY` being set (confirmed in job logs) and the same key pricing the props themselves fine. Spot-checked 6 pregame-run job logs (10-24/10-25) and none printed the `"<game>: closing lines for X/Y props"` line `captureCloses()` should emit. CLV is supposed to be the cheapest signal here (~50 picks is enough per `results/README.md`) and it's been silently dark for the bot's whole month of operation — worth chasing down before anything else NBA-related.
- **Tags/model version were silently dropped since day one.** `logPicks` has destructured `tags` out of every row (`({tags,...r})=>...`) since the Sep 26 commit that introduced it; `model` wasn't added until Oct 24. Both gaps are already fixed on `main` (commit `64bf0bb`, Oct 25), but every one of today's 126 graded picks predates the fix, so none of `nba-edges/LEARNINGS.md`'s "what the weekly review should check" items (b2b voids, DvP, teammate-out CLV, model versions) can be run yet. That data starts flowing with next week's ledger.
- One legitimate void (Oscar Tshiebwe, coach's decision, 10/24). No duplicate picks or alerts on already-ruled-out players found.

**MLB:** too little volume (1-2 graded picks/week) to slice anything; see concentration note above. **NFL/CFB:** no graded ledger to slice yet (see section 2).

**Classification of this week's notable misses:** the Ayton minutes cut is a **model miss** (role change the projection hasn't absorbed). The 10/21 cluster is mostly **variance** (a bad slate, several bench players caught in blowouts). The empty CLV field and the (now-fixed) tags/model stripping are **process bugs** — the CLV one is still open.
